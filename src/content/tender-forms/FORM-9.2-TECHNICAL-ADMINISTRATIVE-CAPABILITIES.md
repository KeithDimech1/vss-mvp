### B.6 AI and Machine Learning Capability - Clean Data Foundation

**Why Clean Data Enables AI Excellence for GDAC-SA:**

Lithodat's core differentiator is our **"Clean Data First"** methodology. Raw geoscience data cannot be directly fed into AI/ML models due to:
- Inconsistent terminology (same mineral = multiple names)
- Missing metadata (coordinates, dates, methods)
- Format chaos (PDF, Excel, legacy databases)
- Unit inconsistencies (ppm vs ppb vs %)
