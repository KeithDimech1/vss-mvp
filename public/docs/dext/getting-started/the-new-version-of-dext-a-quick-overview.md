The updated version of Dext has a new, modern look with a simple and intuitive interface making it even easier to use on a daily basis.

 

Below is a quick overview of the changes and how to continue to access your favourite Dext features to complete your daily tasks!

 

- **Expense Reports **are now **Expense Claims **and can be found under **Costs.**

- **Suppliers **can now be found under **Costs**.

- **Customers** can now be found under **Sales**.

- **Categories**, **Tax Rates**, **Projects**, **Product/ Services** and **Payment Methods** can now be found under **Business Settings**.

- **Bank** **Accounts** can now be found under **Bank**.

- **Connections **can now be found under **Business Settings. **

- **System Settings **are now located under **Extraction **in **Business Settings**.

- **My Details **can now be found under **User Settings. **

- **Account Details **are located under **Business Profile** in **Business Settings**.

- **Subscription** is now located under **Business Settings**.

- **Accountant** can now be found under **Business Settings**.

- **Submission** **History** is now located in the bottom left hand corner of the navigation bar.

 

We’ve also split the settings out into **Practice Settings**, **Business Settings** and** User Settings.**
