# Connecting a Client to Xero or QuickBooks Online for Data Health & Insights

 

Connect your Xero or QuickBooks Online account to Dext to automatically sync the data from your accounting software to the Data Health & Insights area in your Dext account.

 

To Connect to Xero or QuickBooks Online:

 

- Go to **Business settings**, then **Connections**

- On the Connections page, go to the ‘Accounting software’ section and next to &#x27;Connect and manage software for Data Health & Insights&#x27; click **Connect**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668602/bff30064e6074403962f83fc18ed/54381_e0892c11224d118140cc4a7434c19d54.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=8615eb203619b1268cb90d705cd6dedd7cdc4fa7ef32ee9023100a141b1e8c97&req=1dxpw1D8rDVk2hL085ZhoePE3pCCPeCgVYRtTWPWftOYTq85zRsLnsGV7clq%0AdPVnARMdH4hiJcRB%0A) 

- Select Xero or QuickBooks Online, then click **Connect**.

- You will then be directed to the selected accounting software login page. Enter your account login details and follow the steps provided to log in and connect your account to Dext.

 

[See this article](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/connecting-to-xero-or-quickbooks-online-for-data-health-and-insights) for more information on connecting a client to Xero or QuickBooks Online for Data Health & Insights.

 

 

# Set Up Integrations

 

# HMRC (UK Only)

 

Data Health & Insights connects to HMRC to pull back important information on your clients’ VAT filings, current connection status and obligations.

 

To connect to HMRC:

 

- Go to **Practice Settings** and then **Integrations**.

- Click the HMRC option to expand it, then click **Connect to HMRC** and connect to your MTD Agent Account.

 

See more information about connecting to HMRC [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/connecting-to-hmrc-for-vat).

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668619/8425cbad10b918ed14abe737e4f2/54381_86b396b058570a4f2fa620b68b8a714c.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=59888c93f8ad4d8fbdd3397acfc1d6c3f22125cce08c872eadfd4883fb19ee33&req=1dxpw1D8rT5k2hL085ZhoZcVNHOh7U7UjmbIhO%2FJvX4VT90Qy6%2BfJ%2Ba3RSa%2B%0AWEmU2%2Ft566uhSlCS%0A) 

Once connected you can find the [MTD for VAT Insight](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/the-mtd-for-vat-insight) by going to **Insights**, then **MTD for VAT**.

 

 

# Senta

 

Data Health & Insights integrates with [Senta Practice Management Software](https://www.senta.co/) to allow you to pull [health scores](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-the-client-health-score) into Senta to display in your client lists and records. You’ll then be able to use Data Health & Insights health scores in custom views, and to filter or trigger jobs in Senta.

 

Follow the steps in [this guide](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/connecting-with-senta) to integrate with Senta.

 

 

# Go Proposal (All Regions)

 

Dext integrates with GoProposal pricing software to allow you to pull health scores and activity metrics into GoProposal to inform your clients’ pricing for more up-to-date data and insights than ever before.

 

To set up the integration you will need to generate an API key to plug into GoProposal.

 

- Go to **Practice Settings**, then **Data Health**.

- Then go to **API tokens**.

 

[Follow this guide](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/connecting-with-go-proposal) to complete the connection.

# Creating Tags

 

Use tags to easily group and categorise your clients for reports and workflows. Tags are visible in the Practice Insight. You can use tags to search and filter your clients in the Practice Insight.

 

You could tag your practice clients by:

 

- Industry

- GST/VAT Quarters

- End of Year only

- Monthly vs Quarterly Bookkeeping

- Non GST/VAT registered clients

 

**To set up Tags:**

 

- From within your **Practice Account**, go to **Workflows**, then **Tags**.

- Enter the name of the tag and click the **+ **button.

 

See more about tags [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-client-tags).

# Reviewing Lock Dates

 

Lock Dates (in [Xero](https://central.xero.com/s/article/Set-up-and-work-with-lock-dates)) or Closing Dates (in [QuickBooks Online](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-uk/customer-company-settings/close-your-books-to-lock-past-transactions/00/239822#)) prevent changes to records in periods that have already been reported on or filed.

 

Dext Data Health & Insights combines Companies House account filing information (where available) from within these set dates to prevent changes in data in your accounting software and help you keep your financial data stable.

 

The **Lock Date History** page will highlight when these dates get out of sync with filing dates, encouraging active maintenance of monthly reporting period closing or lock dates, enabling you to reduce the number of data inconsistencies between your accounting software and third parties.

 

See more about using lock dates [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-lock-dates).

 

 

# Client Health Score

 

Dext Data Health & Insights calculates an overall health score for each client, giving you an overview of the health of their books. The health score can be seen on the client list and on a client&#x27;s overview page.

 

The health score is derived from various cleanup tools and insights and enables you to focus on improving the data quality of your clients and easily see that progress on a daily basis.

 

You can also customise the health score, by selecting the cleanup tools and insights used to generate the score.

 

[See this article](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-the-client-health-score) to learn more about the health score and how it can be customised.

**MTD for VAT Insight (UK clients only)**

 

For UK clients using our HMRC integration this insight is your location for all things MTD in your practice. Each row is a VAT period detected by Precision for each client, including the filing period, due dates, status, amount and outstanding values.

 

See more about the MTD Insight [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/the-mtd-for-vat-insight).

 

 

**Activity Insight**

 

Designed for accountancy firms utilising Proposal tools to determine pricing for their clients, the Activity page gives a breakdown of the key trading statistics of a company, with a focus on volume, e.g. number of bank transactions for the period. These figures can then be input directly into Proposal tools to quickly calculate pricing when a client is up for renewal.

 

This insight calculates statistics for a rolling 12 month period, starting from the last complete month, and working back from there.

 

See more about the Activity Insight [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/the-activity-insight).

The client overview page gives a summary of all of a client&#x27;s data in one place, giving you a clear and simple view of their current status.

 

The overview includes an overall snapshot of the status of a client&#x27;s documents, including costs, sales, bank and supplier statements as well as the client&#x27;s health score, activity stats and workflows.

 

See more about the Client Overview [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/the-client-overview-page).
