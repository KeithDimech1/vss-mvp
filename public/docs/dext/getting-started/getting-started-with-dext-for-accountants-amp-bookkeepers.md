# Adding a Client Account

 

Creating a Client Account will add a dedicated space to receive review and manage the financial documentation for one of your clients’ businesses.

 

To create a client account:

 

- On your Clients page, click the **Add Client** button at the top of the page.

# Adding a Client User or Colleague

 

You can add additional colleagues to your team in your practice account, and users to your client accounts. When you add a User or Colleague, you have the option to skip the invitation to invite the user to access the Dext account or to invite them to access the account themselves as specific [role and permissions](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions).

 

**Skipping the Invitation**

 

Skipping the invitation allows a user to be added to a document as a Document owner, and submit documents to via their unique email in addresses, but they will not be invited to access the Dext account for themselves.

 

**Invited Users with Roles & Permissions**

 

Users that are invited to access the account will have access to the mobile or web app and have the ability to submit, view and edit paperwork depending on their assigned [role and permissions](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions). You assign this level when the user is created and can change it at any time.

 

[See how to add a User to a client account here.](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-add-a-user-to-a-client-account)

 

[See how to add a Colleague here.](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-add-colleagues)

 

 

# Connecting your Accounting Software

 

Dext can work collaboratively with your accounting software. Once connected, the two systems will share your chart of accounts and all documents will be extracted and can be published with a format that matches your existing financial records.

 

To connect your Dext account to your accounting software:

 

- Navigate to the **Business Settings** section of the sidebar, then **Connections**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608735/bf62bb9acca5c8344a4a44628142/46805_8c3aa8f4e86fa670995672679ec2928e.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=7f0b74b61f1726dda85c0724fdf264b926cad2fe26f2f83a6ba7a1c71fd118a1&req=1tBpxVD9rzJk2hL085ZhoeYsy2SatXX%2FBhWdX%2Fx1Ou4DfJIZrHytSF%2BcLvMB%0Al0VkTTuErhTriK6S%0A) 

- Under Accounting Software click the **Connect** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608745/8b9e1549851221858381691f57cf/46805_0b6920be32301130c8fdf0797f996dc4.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=181d163698fdb8ae345412749ca368c87af66a89e97ddbf8b92bcf1b202c6117&req=1tBpxVD9qDJk2hL085ZhoTWa0CAesLECKnuLJ1NltESM4iLbIIemY8XXQBG2%0AhSo9nlUbN0KRRI22%0A)

You can start submitting your items by:

 

- Clicking the green **Add Documents** button.

# Rules & Automation

 

Use Supplier & Customer Rules, Smart Split and Auto-publish to specify how data should be treated when paperwork from particular suppliers, payment methods or users are submitted.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608781/c8a29e064e8fdeb17e253fafc9f1/46805_1c25f3be1c873676a1fb2481f410f6a2.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=d1b76f4e956b5c1807060daba90e5fc927c32984867eed17456f8a8dad827261&req=1tBpxVD9pDZk2hL085ZhoQcmQY%2BKxZCEhaFgVjU%2F7SRD7cN3g55xwkW6xZ8T%0A4uw6CqTtvrvQWo1i%0A) 

Read more about each automation feature:

 

- [Supplier & Customer Rules](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-supplier-and-customer-rules) – specifications in how paperwork from a particular supplier should be coded or described

- [User rules](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/setting-user-rules) – specifications on which Payment Methods or Projects should be applied to paperwork submitted from a particular user.

- [Smart Split](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-smart-split) – specify how paperwork from a particular vendor should be split into line items

- [Auto-publish](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/automatically-publishing-your-items) – automatically publish coded paperwork from a specific supplier to an integrated accounting software.
