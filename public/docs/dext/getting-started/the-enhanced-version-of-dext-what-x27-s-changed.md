# Managing your Practice:

 

 

# Client List

 

Manage your clients easily from your client list. The first business listed will be your own practice business, from which you can manage your practice’s bookkeeping.

 

Below that all your clients will be listed and you will be able to see the integrated accounting software, account manager, client type, and many other details listed beside them.

# Practice Settings

 

Your practice settings, including your practice profile, registered address, tax, item messaging and subscription, can all be managed by going to Practice Settings in the bottom left of the screen.

 

See more about practice settings [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/practice-settings).

Supplier rules can now be accessed by:

# Business Settings

 

A client’s business settings are accessed via the Business Settings button in the button left corner of the screen.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665658/736838a46ce09ad639851c39f6de/45203_3bbcb44e32150835f527c63e5c380802.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=befbf5a6a8652f24504fb373e043702d301e293a601bcfc004e38b380ffe07c7&req=1dxpw138qT9k2hL085ZhoYrst%2FWFcymEl4hO0KTIcLGehjAO%2BE8EiMOKro5k%0AjyVG0Rvf2lWiGAs3%0A) 

In the business settings area you can access the Business profile, Connections, Extraction, Automation and Export settings, and Lists.

 

Please note that:

 

- ‘System Settings’ are now located under **Extraction** in **Business Settings**.

- ‘Account Details’ are now located under **Business Profile** in **Business Settings**.

- ‘Subscription’ is now located under **Business Settings**.

- ‘Accountant’ can now be found under **Business Settings**.

- And finally ‘My Details’ can now be found in **User Settings** which is accessed by going to the user icon in the top right of the screen.

 

See more about business settings [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/business-settings).
