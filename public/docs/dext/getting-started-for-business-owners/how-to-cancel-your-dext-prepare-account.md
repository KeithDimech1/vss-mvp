# What you need to know:

 

- This article is aimed at Dext Prepare for Business accounts.

- Only Admin users can cancel an account - if you cannot see the Subscription page, contact your account administrator.

- If your accountant or bookkeeper is paying for your Dext Prepare subscription contact them to cancel your account.

- If you’re a Dext Prepare partner and you want to cancel your subscription please contact your account manager.

- If you’ve purchased your Dext Prepare account from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, you’ll need to follow their instructions on how to cancel.

 

 

**Note: **During the 30 day cancellation period, you’ll still have full access to your Dext Prepare account until the prepaid period has ended.

 

 

# To cancel your Dext Prepare account:

 

We recommend you [export your data](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-export-your-data-as-a-csv-file) before you cancel your subscription.

 

- Go **My Profile** in the sidebar, then **Subscription.**

- Go to the** Billing **tab**.**

- Select **Cancel subscription.**
