**Welcome to Dext Prepare!**

 

Here are a few things you can do to get started on the software:

 

**Log in**

 

If you haven&#x27;t already, log in to the [Dext Prepare web app](https://app.dext.com/login).

 

**Take a tour of your account**

 

There are a few places in your Dext Prepare account that you should get familiar with. Let&#x27;s check out the:

 

- [Costs Workspace](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/The-Costs-Workspace)

- [Sales Workspace](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/The-Sales-Workspace)

- [Add Documents](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-add-items-to-Dext-Prepare)

- [Account Settings page](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/The-System-Settings-page)

 

**Set up your Chart of Accounts**

 

Your purchases should each be categorised with an appropriate nominal code. If you already use a specific Chart of Accounts, you can set this up on Dext Prepare by [Integrating](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/Why-link-to-an-accounting-software) with an Accounting Software or by [uploading it as a CSV](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-set-up-your-Category-List).

 

**Upload a receipt**

 

Use any of the Dext Prepare submission methods to upload an image of a receipt or invoice. After it has finished processing, it&#x27;ll be presented in the corresponding Dext Prepare [Workspace](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/The-Inbox-page) with all of the [relevant data](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/What-data-is-extracted-by-Dext-Prepare) already extracted.

 

**Add additional Users**

 

If you&#x27;re planning to have multiple people managing or submitting to Dext Prepare, you can [add additional users](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-add-a-user-to-your-account) to your account and invite them to the service.

 

**Download the Dext Prepare Mobile App**

 

The web app isn&#x27;t the only way to use Dext Prepare. Download the Dext Prepare [iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/receipt-bank-receipts-invoice/id418327708?mt=8) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.receiptbank.android) mobile app and log in to submit and monitor your account.

 

If there&#x27;s anything you need help with, feel free to contact your Account Development Consultant, a member of our support team, or check out our Help Centre.
