**Dext is a platform used to manage the receipts, invoices, and other documents that businesses depend on to keep accurate, secure financial records.**

 

You can [capture and upload](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-add-items-to-Dext-Prepare) your bills and receipts using:

 

- [The camera on your mobile phone](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-use-the-mobile-app-to-upload-paperwork)

- [Email](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-Submit-Items-by-Email)

- [Documents on your computer](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-To-Add-Documents)

- [Automatically fetching them from online platforms](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-set-up-a-Fetch-Connection)

 

After you&#x27;ve sent in a document, Dext&#x27;s automated extraction engine will read all of the [key information](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/What-data-is-extracted-by-Dext-Prepare) and display it in an easily-manageable format, perfect for downloading or processing with a [connected accounting software](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/Why-link-to-an-accounting-software).

An image of the document and its associated data will be kept on file for a minimum of 10 years. Feel free to throw away that receipt; Dext will always have you [covered for compliance](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/Does-Dext-Prepare-comply-with-tax-authorities) and keep your financial documents securely stored online, where they can be shared with your colleagues, accountant or bookkeeper.
