**If you&#x27;ve previously **[**given access to an accountant or bookkeeper**](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-invite-your-accountant-or-bookkeeper-to-access-your-Dext-Prepare-account) ** to view and manage your Dext Prepare account, you can remove or change their access.**

 

 

This article is relevant to the legacy Dext experience. You can access the article for the enhanced Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/removing-or-reallocating-a-bookkeepers-or-accountants-access-to-your-account).

 

 

**Important: **To be able to remove or reallocate a bookkeeper’s or accountant’s access to your account, you must have the admin level [role](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions). If you don’t have this level of access, reach out to your account administrator.

 

- **[Remove Access](#remove-access)**

- **[Reallocate Access](#reallocate-access)**

- [**How to Subscribe when Your Account Has Been Deactivated**](#subscribe)

 

 

# Remove Access

 

To remove their access:

 

- Navigate to **My Profile** in the Manage section of the sidebar, then **Accountant**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662294/f2d5df75473417f9519553a41506/1836_1dd000b8aaffcad85eeda468015796d0.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=7c1bf6f0c906a573e5f03981a2b871268de2b891061601fe13a5947cc412e3e9&req=1dxpw1r4pTNk2hL085ZhodzruDYnAaiXB8E2ulgFXaJkgWtxvvOduo2hF8F6%0Ae2R2vSVU1DmsnuYz%0A) 

- Click on the **Disconnect from accountant** button.

- Review the option on the panel that appears and then click **Disconnect**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662302/1b18d3a0424bb37440748f29b7c5/1836_44da25b76d7711fde39a280c431bbdd9.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=efd377af1cd9742700f8a4875afbeed4d0f88de3a6bfdf9293bb24967438a391&req=1dxpw1r5rDVk2hL085Zhoaa4axNPQpCd5YTaDod%2BDUWw4TkXb%2FDzGHe%2BPCxP%0A2jp7BiFPb%2FDXFDCz%0A) 

 

# Reallocate Access

 

- Navigate to **My Profile** in the Manage section of the sidebar, then **Accountant**.

- Click the **Invite someone else** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662311/f29c752555486c0ce9f9f3b84164/1836_1dd000b8aaffcad85eeda468015796d0.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=0870b51eb9e9c81ff603fce0c4642b6641ea7b6579fc0994ff2986db46577e6f&req=1dxpw1r5rTZk2hL085ZhoUR%2Bxlebz8Ho5OarZ8bhfUY6Z04hwJMUIahXgwrE%0Ag%2BznEnIJqvVyYOu5%0A) 

- Enter the email address of the accountant or bookkeeper you wish to invite and click **Send invite**.

 

 

**Important:** you can only give one accountant or bookkeeper access to your account at any one time. If you choose to invite someone else your current accountant or bookkeeper will be disconnected and the new one invited to access the account.

# How to Subscribe when Your Account Has Been Deactivated

 

If your account has been deactivated or disconnected from your previous accountant/bookkeeper you will see a message at the top of your screen: "Your account has been deactivated/disconnected by your accountant or bookkeeper. Start a Free Trial".

 

To continue using your account you must activate a subscription.

 

To do this:

 

- Go to the **My Profile** in the sidebar then **Subscription**.

 

 

If you cannot see the Subscription page it means you are not an Admin user in your account and must reach out to your account admin or previous accountant/bookkeeper to request this access.

 

- Click **Start a Free Trial**, then select a plan.

- Make sure you select the desired duration of the contract (annual or monthly).

- Click **Buy now**.
