**Each Dext Prepare Account can be integrated with an Accounting Software. This means:**

 

- The two platforms work collaboratively, sharing their Chart of Accounts.

- Dext Prepare data can be Published directly to the Integrated Accounting Software.

- Suppliers, Customers, Projects and Payment Methods are synced between the two pieces of software

 

Simply put, integrating an account will ensure that all data extraction will be done in a format that matches your existing financial records.

 

We advise that if you have a Cloud Accounting Software, you integrate your Dext Prepare account as soon as possible. This will prevent transactions from being coded incorrectly.

 

To integrate your Dext Prepare account, navigate to the &#x27;Integration&#x27; section under &#x27;Connections&#x27; and click &#x27;Choose Software&#x27;. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the integration process.
