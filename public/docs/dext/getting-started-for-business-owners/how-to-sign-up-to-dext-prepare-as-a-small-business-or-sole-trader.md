**If you&#x27;re self-employed or run your own business and would like to subscribe to Dext Prepare, you can choose our Dext Business plan for small to medium businesses.**

 

Alternatively, you can sign up to our [free 14 day trial](https://dext.com/uk/products/free-trial) of our Dext Business account and see how Dext Prepare works.

 

Once the trial is completed you can then upgrade to a full subscription via the **Subscription** tab in the **My Profile** section of the sidebar. Once you purchase a plan, your upgraded account will be available to use immediately.

 

If your Dext Prepare account was previously paid for by your accountant or bookkeeper and wish to take over payment you subscribe on your own by following [this guide](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-transfer-a-business-dext-prepare-subscription-to-be-paid-by-an-accountant-bookkeeper-and-vice-versa).
