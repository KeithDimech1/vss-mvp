**Invite an external accountant or bookkeeper to Dext Prepare, and they’ll be able to view, manage and collaborate with you on the paperwork that you’ve submitted to your account.**

 

 

Note: No changes will be made to any account data when access is revoked or granted to a new accountant.

 

If you’re currently connected to an accountant and wish to change to a different accountant then you can simply reallocate the access to the new accountant.

 

Depending on whether or not your accountant/bookkeeper uses Dext Prepare already, they’ll either be added to your account immediately, or contacted to see if they’d like to join Dext

 

 

You can only invite your accountants/bookkeepers to access your account if theirs is a accountant/bookkeeper account. If their email is linked to a business account you will get an error and won’t be able to invite them. In this case, you&#x27;ll need to contact your accountant/bookkeeper to set up an accountant/bookkeeper account or, alternatively, set them up as an Admin user in your own account.

- **[How to invite if your Accountant/bookkeeper does not currently use Dext Prepare](#does-not-use-dext-prepare)**

 

 

# Where to go to invite your Accountant or Bookkeeper

 

- You can invite your accountant or bookkeeper from the **Accountant** page in the **My Profile** section of the sidebar.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662309/f2fa544bcb6491440ed9ed715d08/1841_cada0d56633a46c0f9fa8dc0ee5cbb8a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=2760bfa5e9912c4f449f68a212bec65bce295afe4b7ebc8f356ff9aa70cbdbd8&req=1dxpw1r5rD5k2hL085ZhoQXaQnrORzvodgLtDMmH85ORBUOJXFnm3ogy2nAV%0AKNbCAG4jpBASTKP%2F%0A) 

You also access this page via your homepage, by going to Invite your accountant and clicking the **Invite Accountant **button**. **

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662318/889847d9936eae0e48fe32ef20fd/1841_26168dd624aaa6b787f11d9b65a60735.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=fe4815f789089e9c9f398bd676b2a9300096d567bf7db9341b1b2444b6a87e0c&req=1dxpw1r5rT9k2hL085ZhoVRf5HNw9jS%2BT1m%2FDUEaZTKgvwG4o12VhmRpN1le%0AV1QCjgjpvyeQmBuq%0A) 

- Once on the Accountant page, enter the accountant’s email address, then click **Send invite**.

 

From here there are two flows:

 

- **How to invite if your Accountant/bookkeeper already uses Dext Prepare**

- **How to invite if your Accountant/bookkeeper does not currently use Dext Prepare**

 

 

# Accountant/bookkeeper already uses Dext Prepare

 

If your accountant/bookkeeper is already using Dext Prepare, you’ll see this message:

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662326/1c0755a5f76a32da8ede02e3dfdb/1841_3d3d5336b5862c597a5eadd35d10cd07.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=25a8de6f308ec761f04c61cd7f890f0a9a290ea793a2e44f77db46220531373b&req=1dxpw1r5rjFk2hL085ZhodMR8JyMcrC37dbkO1fhv6H8BkLXQqjoFt96wYVM%0AuRmSdmVTmckQLxEx%0A) 

- Click **Allow access**. They’ll be given permission to access your account and view, edit and publish your uploaded paperwork.

- When the connection is successful you will see this notification.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662334/0a39362326a4675473f27d2b17d7/1841_1ab2a41eb1da4b71bf0152b8f13ce6c0.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=37f89c31e4bb072963e6c2dbf16f3a66053316513d7d8f236cc9323de1b7e6cc&req=1dxpw1r5rzNk2hL085ZhoRnH9lIPSGsl9Ia%2BNamXqIoJLUvPBwZpzHd%2FIJV5%0ANSIrmYCEvL5V58Cc%0A) 

You can manage this connection at any time by going to **My Profile** in the sidebar, then **Accountant**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662339/8d4f7594ea756537a7f28fa58709/1841_a3853c71f98fff36028c5f764acacb09.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=27853c25d5a109e7fcaadec1aa42bb9322107bd42252b9039e7a302b07300c04&req=1dxpw1r5rz5k2hL085Zhobcb6Zl4yuJM5Rgg%2BiFljIk6LFVqSejttYkKoqp0%0A8MtsbENYQyUUdMbv%0A) 

 

# Accountant/bookkeeper does not currently use Dext Prepare

 

If your accountant/bookkeeper does not subscribe to Dext Prepare, you can invite them to use Dext Prepare:

 

- Enter your accountant or bookkeeper’s email address, then click **Send Invite**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662343/5e570ca5a309ee0c8e6e64e97fbd/1841_cada0d56633a46c0f9fa8dc0ee5cbb8a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=8e13ccb12497074df99cb14a4c66d77bc4e2d1e8d946cd816a2adec097c7edce&req=1dxpw1r5qDRk2hL085ZhoecSV4fQZcopcnHUQxnqP%2BS0WQo4ng%2B7ySh%2Fs3Jo%0AbbqI96Rh7QgBg0cp%0A) 

The accountant will be sent an invitation to start a free trial for accountants & bookkeepers.

 

If you want to see a preview of the email they will be sent, click **See how the email will look**.

 

- You can see the status of the invitation by going to **My profile** in the sidebar, then **Accountant**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662351/952d82d919fd5899ee1ff81996c1/1841_c9174b1a59c9691dda4d179a3dd29006.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=e36aee4913e0510c029026e257aab3611aab0af743ab906700b9122df909c370&req=1dxpw1r5qTZk2hL085ZhoWG3CUyRaWFgRRX8fICS4RoPt3pXMcclXmWcOI2t%0AyK5YlsU%2BFMmaQfKA%0A) 

- When your accountant accepts the invitation a pop up will show on the screen notifying you of the successful connection.
