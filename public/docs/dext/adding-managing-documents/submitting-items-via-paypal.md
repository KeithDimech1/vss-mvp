**Dext can check your Paypal account and collect any new Payments and Automatic Payments at 24 hour intervals**

 

 

This article is relevant to the enhanced Dext experience. You can access the article for the legacy Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-submit-items-via-paypal).

 

 

**Note:** if you&#x27;re an Accountant or Bookkeeper with a subscription to a Dext Partner Account, you&#x27;ll need to instruct your clients to log in to Dext and connect PayPal themselves.

 

To connect your Dext and PayPal accounts:

 

- Go to Business Settings, then **Connections**

- Go to &#x27;Costs connections&#x27;,** **and click **Connect**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668096/a57b534bf2437955b36c47bfc7aa/50680_d13aa6eae8f25bbd099f44ac9a030523.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=783e10a24c18acdd7d376fe0b1f28b231ba69b17f9d2e4e3706de8f9dc43007a&req=1dxpw1D6pTFk2hL085ZhoQRhhGu%2BRcB4kYKEXWlgZguWh8ewGBMYl8neg1sj%0A56fR%2FXgnZyo%2FGcqr%0A) 

- Go to the **PayPal** tab.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668104/42e2762eac188f9e2fc83047e5f3/50680_51b514d210e5f6a09fe43a8863133e5d.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=d95d10258f566137ca998631443bced40de00e94e860acfe365ca469af40996a&req=1dxpw1D7rDNk2hL085ZhodzGRWVh9u4VDJWkjigpf%2BcrnAyZvdzS9pDRmRc8%0AZh%2FMMPodD7czV1OG%0A) 

- Select how frequently you wish for the transactions to be loaded from PayPal, then click **Enable PayPal**.

 

- You&#x27;ll be asked to fill in your Paypal Login credentials to authorize the connection.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668115/5edbdae054e095f09854369119fb/50680_33f2a575616947ffaa7db29f1ad46614.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=b6d7a5934138c2bc5841f90d8eb315f11ee4a1811542ce6eafb1d27f555522e9&req=1dxpw1D7rTJk2hL085ZhoaX3MHKavVkmuVvB0Mm3QJhfpW1QlLSGmSAghWKZ%0AL3AbE10oMzyECjY5%0A) 

- After you&#x27;ve connected your Paypal Account, Dext will display any new and previous transactions in the **Costs** Inbox. Transactions will be imported every 24 hours.
