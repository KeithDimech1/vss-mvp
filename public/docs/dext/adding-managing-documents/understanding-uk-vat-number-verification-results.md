## ⚠️ Important: This article applies to UK customers only. VAT number verification is checked against HMRC’s official records.

 

When you upload a cost item in Dext, we automatically check the supplier’s **UK VAT number** against HMRC’s records. This helps confirm whether the VAT number is genuine and matches the supplier details on your document.

 

💡 **Tip:** If you’re unsure about any result, the safest option is to confirm the VAT number and registered business name directly with your supplier.

 

 

## **What the VAT verification messages mean**

## **1. Verifying VAT number with HMRC**

- We’re checking the VAT number you’ve entered with HMRC’s records.**​

- This usually only takes a few seconds, so you may not see this often.
​

 

## 2. Invalid VAT number. Check and try again.**

- The VAT number entered may appear visually correct, however, it is not a valid sequence format that can be verified with HMRC.**​

- Check the number again, or confirm with your supplier.

 

 ⛔️VAT number - Not a valid VAT number issued by HMRC

 

 

## 3. This VAT number isn’t registered with HMRC. Check with your supplier.**

- The VAT number looks valid (and may have been active in the past), but HMRC does not have a current record of it.**​

- Confirm the number with your supplier before proceeding.

 

 ⛔️VAT number - Not registered or active with HMRC

 

 

## 4. Valid VAT number, but the supplier name is not verified as it&#x27;s missing. HMRC records show it belongs to &#x27;BUSINESS NAME.**

- The VAT number is valid and registered with HMRC.**​

- However, there’s no supplier name entered on the item details.
​

- Adding a supplier name will enable a full check with HMRC.

 

 ✅VAT number - Valid & registered with HMRC

 

 *️⃣Supplier name - No supplier name provided

 

 

## 5. Verified VAT number. Supplier name matches HMRC records.**

- The VAT number is valid.**​

- The supplier name on your document exactly matches HMRC’s records.

 

 ✅VAT number - Valid & registered with HMRC

 

 ✅Supplier name - Exact match to VAT number registration

 

 

## 6. Verified VAT number. The supplier name is a near match to HMRC&#x27;s record of ‘BUSINESS NAME’.**

- The VAT number is valid.**​

- The supplier name is very similar to HMRC’s records (for example, a shortened or slightly different spelling).

 

 ✅VAT number - Valid & registered with HMRC

 

 ℹ️Supplier name - Near match to VAT number registration

 

 

## 7. This VAT number is registered to ‘BUSINESS NAME’. Check this with your supplier.**

- The VAT number is valid, but the supplier name on your document is different from HMRC’s records.**​

- This can be a common scenario where legitimate companies have not registered their ‘trading as’ names, or they are significantly different from their registered business entity name.
​

- If you are unsure or have doubts, double-check this information with your supplier.

 

 ✅VAT number - Valid & registered with HMRC

 

 ⚠️Supplier name - Does not match the HMRC record for this VAT number

 

 

## Key takeaway**

Dext helps you quickly identify if a supplier’s **VAT number is valid and registered with HMRC**. For added confidence, always confirm details directly with your supplier if the name and VAT number don’t clearly match.
