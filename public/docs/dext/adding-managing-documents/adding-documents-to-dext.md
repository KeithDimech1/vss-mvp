# Use the submission method that best suits your needs to Add Costs & Sales items or Bank transaction data to your Dext account.

 

See the sections below for an overview of the submission methods available for each type of document:

 

- [Costs Documents](#costs)

- [Sales Documents](#sales)

- [Bank Transactions](#bank)

- [Where Do Items Go After They’re Added?](#where-submissions-go)

 

After adding your documents, Dext will extract the data you need. See more about how long extraction takes [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-long-does-extraction-take1). Once extraction is complete an item can be found in the inbox of the Costs or Sales workspaces or the Processed Statements section of the Bank workspace.

 

Items can then be edited to ensure they include all the relevant details, before being published to your accounting software.

 

 

Note: To save time, set up supplier, customer & auto-publishing rules to automate the process of preparing items and publishing them to your accounting software.

 

 

# Costs Documents

 

Costs relate to documentation for your accounts payable.

 

You can use the following submission methods to add Cost items to Dext:

 

- [Upload](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-upload-costs-sales-documents) from your computer

- [Email](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/submitting-items-with-extract-by-email) via your dedicated Dext Extract By Email address

- Submit via [Mobile](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-use-the-mobile-app-to-upload-paperwork) app

- Setting up a [Fetch](http://how-to-set-up-a-fetch-connection) connection with a supplier

 

Documents can also be added by:

 

- [Tripcatcher](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-integrate-with-Tripcatcher) (UK only)

- [Dropbox](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/submitting-items-using-dropbox)

- [Paypal](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-submit-items-via-PayPal)

 

 

# Sales Documents

 

Sales relate to the invoices and other financial documents your business has issued to customers for payment.

 

You can use the following submission methods to add Sales items to Dext:

 

- [Upload](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-upload-costs-sales-documents) from your computer

- [Email](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/submitting-items-with-extract-by-email) via your dedicated Dext Extract By Email address

- Submit via [Mobile](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-use-the-mobile-app-to-upload-paperwork) app

- [Commerce Lite](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-commerce-lite) (Important note: this is a paid feature)

 

 

# Bank Statements

 

There are two ways you can add bank transaction data to Dext:

 

- [Upload a bank statement](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/bank-feeds-statements) from your computer

- Connect to your bank via a [Bank Feed](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/bank-feeds-statements#bank-feed)

 

 

# Where Do Items Go After They’re Added?

 

**During extraction
​**Costs & Sales items can be found in the **In Processing** section of the Costs or Sales workspaces.**Bank statements can be found in the Collected Statements** section of the Bank workspace.

 

**Once extraction is completed****
​**Costs & Sales items can then be found in the **Inbox** of the Costs or Sales workspace.**Bank statements can be found in the Processed Statements** section of the Bank workspace. While Bank transactions from a bank feed can be found in the **Transactions** section of the Bank workspace.

 

 

Note: If you can’t find a document after it has been uploaded, it may mean that the item is a duplicate of an item already added to your Dext account and been automatically deleted. This will happen when the automatic duplicate setting is switched **ON**. If this is the case the item can be found by going to **Submission History**. [See more about duplicate documents here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-does-dext-handle-duplicate-documents). And see how to [use the Submission History page here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/submission-history).
