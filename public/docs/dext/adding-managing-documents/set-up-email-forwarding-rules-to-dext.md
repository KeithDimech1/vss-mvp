# **Why use email forwarding?**

If suppliers send receipts or invoices to your inbox, set up an email rule to forward them to Dext automatically. This saves time and eliminates manual uploads.

 

# **What you’ll need**

- Your unique Dext email-in address:

 

**[custom-name]@dext.cc **- forwards one document per email.

- **[custom-name]@multiple.dext.cc** - splits each **page** of the attachment into separate documents in Dext.

 

- Access to your email provider’s forwarding/rules settings.

**Important:** Forward only to your Dext address above. If a different address is used, documents will not reach Dext.

 

# **Quick setup (generic steps)**

- Open your email settings and go to **Forwarding** or **Rules/Filters**.

- Create a new rule to forward messages that contain **receipts**, **invoices**, or are from known supplier addresses.

- Set the forward-to address as your Dext email-in (**@dext.cc** or **@multiple.dext.cc**).

- Save and enable the rule.

- Send a test email with a PDF or image attachment to confirm it arrives in Dext.

# **Verification codes and links**

Some providers require verification before forwarding to a new address:

- **Where it’s sent:** The verification code or link goes **to the email you use to log in to Dext**.

- **Gmail note:** Gmail typically sends a **verification link** instead of a code. Click the link to confirm forwarding.

 

# **Choosing the right Dext address**

- Use **@dext.cc** if you want each email to create **one** document.

- Use** @multiple.dext.cc** if you regularly receive multi-page PDFs and want **each page** imported as a separate document.

 

# **Tips for reliable forwarding**

- Add supplier email addresses to your rule conditions (e.g., **from: [invoices@vendor.com](mailto:invoices@vendor.com)**).

- Include common keywords like **invoice**, **receipt**, **statement**, and **bill**.

- Allow large attachments and supported file types (PDF, JPG, PNG).

- Keep a copy in your inbox or archive (optional), so you have a backup.

 

# **Troubleshooting**

- **Nothing arrives in Dext:** Confirm the rule is enabled and the forward-to address is exactly your Dext email-in.

- **Verification not received:** Check spam/junk. Re-send the verification.

- **Attachments split unexpectedly:** You’re likely using **@multiple.dext.cc,** which imports each **page** as a separate document by design.

- **Forwarding stopped:** Re-verify if your provider prompts you, and make sure mailbox storage isn’t full.

 

# **FAQ**

## **Can I forward from multiple email addresses?**

Yes. Any mailbox can forward to your Dext email-in address.

 

## **Can I CC or BCC the Dext address?**

Yes, but results are most reliable when your rule **forwards** or **auto-forwards** directly to the Dext address.

 

## **Can I change my Dext email-in address?**

You can create or rename the custom part before **@dext.cc**. Update your rules if you change it.
