**You can view a list of all of your account’s submitted items in the ‘Submission history’ section.**

 

The Submission history will show you all the items added to your Dext Prepare account. This includes items from the Costs & Sales inboxes and archives, as well as items that have been deleted, including duplicate items. You can also view the items and their status from this page.

# Searching and Filtering for Items

 

You can search and filter for specific items by going to the search bar in the top right corner of the page.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665586/56c6138a490b9a86f3dd200f2844/45378_2b671cdb1b6291657593938736de62a2.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=d9926de71028339044873c779c28cc14c46ab2b8fa95a43408638459bc63310d&req=1dxpw13%2FpDFk2hL085ZhoR0R3ZgaphEHBRb0eI5fNhGLaXOGeyIxLVYxDQLm%0Ah9oHE%2B3ovTJSh%2FBw%0A) 

To find an item from a specific supplier you can enter their name in the search bar.

 

For more in depth search filters:

 

- Click **Advanced** in the search bar.

- You can then search for and filter items by:

 

- Supplier

- The user who submitted the item

 

**Important**: The **Submitted by** field will be left blank if one of the following submission methods has been used: Email-in, Qonto, PayPal, Dropbox or Tripcatcher. If the receipt is uploaded via web upload or mobile upload, then the uploading user will be shown.

 

- Submission method

- User who owns the item

- Submitted at (submission date)

- Date (date on item)

- Total amount by range

- Showing only deleted items or merged items

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665598/0a9ca1db60790f8a26426ec68c75/45378_9ac6b99041e69fa50faae82f558c5214.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=2d6a865bdf25df69371a5f83ccc1450a4f4513a03fe4cd061564312735509ddb&req=1dxpw13%2FpT9k2hL085ZhoS1Khum4RnLp2kYq1xLfgud%2FaldIqmBWk0YJqIDf%0ATrJJRao3TnYT1ZR0%0A) 

3. Once you selected your filters, click **Apply**

 

4. To view an item click into the item and then click **Show**

 

Filters can be removed by clicking the orange **X** at the top of the submission history view:

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665609/4476649bcafcc61e9c81151a067d/45378_c25f1c53072ae0ec992c5a9636b81825.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=aee14440ea46029446fc6ad9917a108a9a85cc41b93efe9968862ada1dfba03d&req=1dxpw138rD5k2hL085ZhoRQ9JPIg%2Bl13iCSTvfm8PvCGoovvrJ8cxa%2BoQJPR%0AI8BJPBlXJ%2FLccxG2%0A) 

 

# Actioning Items in the Submission History

 

From the Submission History, you can access items from the Costs & Sales inboxes or archives, download item images, view item histories and restore deleted items (including duplicate items).

 

To perform these actions, you’ll need to click into the relevant item.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665630/ea043604fa8303f87a793d1bbce4/45378_3ee4c75c6a5d8142dd58b085f8ddbde1.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=6d035628d382316953b1683603662670aaa6dc5bd7adc3359307a2be69bf3c86&req=1dxpw138rzdk2hL085ZhoYQID82IW9gV2KrSpclOqcQgERfFTF0GAkgDzHzm%0ATwU%2B9Wa5ZTQuysnP%0A) 

You’ll either see Show or Restore as an option for your image:

 

**Show** – see items in the inbox or archive

 

**Restore** – return deleted items, such as a suspected duplicate item, to the Costs or Sales inbox.
