Once you’ve uploaded your documents into Dext, you can **edit** item details, **publish** them to your connected accounting software, or **export** them to share or store elsewhere.

 

# **View and edit item details**

Dext automatically extracts all key information from documents. You can edit any field as needed - your changes save automatically when you click outside the field.

- Go to **Costs** or **Sales**, depending on the document type.

- Click an item to see its details and the document image.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81413976/85c1adcfa10f3e1f48b095ee79f4/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=a0f6b54c2a70077c4af60da02a01d0c71f8be2d6be4ebbe7825c605f39038714&req=2NRrxFvzqzFk2hL085ZhoajqdxcdmFZ3KhP4HoxadauJ4syADTVGG5DRFPGk%0AbBGRnPfBvB6bBkXp%0A)**Important:** To publish an item, make sure the following fields are completed:

- **Category**

- **Supplier**

- **Total amount**

If any are missing, the item stays in **To review**. **[Learn more here](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/416750-what-does-to-review-and-ready-mean)**. 

 

# **Publish items to your accounting software**

**[Connecting your accounting software](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/339551-how-to-connect-dext-to-an-accounting-software)** to Dext enables publishing - we send the item’s extracted data along with the original document image to the selected destination in your accounting software.

- Open an item in the **Costs** or **Sales** inbox.

- (Optional) Update the **Item details **where necessary.

- Select a **Category**.

- Choose a **Publish to** destination. 

- (Optional) If you’re [connected to Xero](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/339548-connecting-with-xero), select a **Publish as** status for the item. 

- Click the **Publish button **at the top or bottom of the page.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81418155/a01690cc3d1b35f9392191564927/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=a450ea3b6ce785b7b7ee9d3cba88aad36b9bdf1633261022d3bc13a4670989f7&req=2NRrxFD7qTJk2hL085ZhoVpW9XVDuRW9ycIQtvqbPR4mJVOwPSdTRRqlFAWY%0AqrnOGcAHCFAaShQt%0A) 

A copy of the published item is kept in **[the](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/416742-the-archive-tab)**[ **Archive tab**](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/416742-the-archive-tab) for easy reference.

 

# **Export items to download or share**

You can export from the **Costs/Sales Inbox** or **Archive tabs**.

- **Export selected items:** Select via checkboxes, then go to **Tools > Export**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81417379/9bd2fbafefa7c47074c008d1248b/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=902913a388bb478cdffbac0fd529cc7803e0556112e06037671dabb3e55c22aa&req=2NRrxF%2F5qz5k2hL085ZhoYUT9sYar0fy3L8SrJRjsz%2BlS24CzA8EIt9HvHAy%0A%2FnKrIYKGoqjoUCkT%0A)
- **Export everything in a list:** Leave all items unselected, open **Tools**, and choose **Export all**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81417707/b956820fc1e1465169a50a9c496b/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=541e2211ae4f2c53a8c1d1068b804cde7f117b05591db40ce759a11f420073ea&req=2NRrxF%2F9rDBk2hL085ZhoYF%2FUtbg6h7LQdfFOwaBoQ86%2BJweGd7optkAjBCI%0Ax5neRAKQ1SiGnoP8%0A) 

Choose your format in the export window:

- **PDF** – up to 250 items.

- **CSV** – choose a default or custom format.

- **ZIP** – ideal for large batches; includes original file formats.
