# Use Fetch to automatically collect costs from your Amazon Business account.

 

 

This article is relevant to the enhanced Dext experience. You can access the article for the legacy Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/setting-up-a-fetch-connection-for-amazon-business).

 

Before setting up the connection it is essential to ensure you have an Amazon Business account. If your Amazon account is a private account you can either convert your account to an Amazon Business Account (For Non-Prime Subscribers) or sign up for Amazon Prime Duo (for Prime Subscribers) if you wish to keep your personal Prime account.

 

Once this is done you’ll be ready to set up your fetch connection.

 

- **[Convert an Amazon Personal Account to an Amazon Business Account (Non-Prime Subscribers)](#convert-business)**

- **[Sign up for Amazon Prime Duo (Prime Subscribers)](#prime-duo)**

- **[Setting up a Fetch Connection with Amazon Business](#connect)**

 

 

# Convert an Amazon Personal Account to an Amazon Business Account (Non-Prime Subscribers)

 

If your Amazon account isn’t a Prime account you can convert your personal account into an Amazon Business account.

 

If you prefer to just create a new Amazon Business account separate from your existing Amazon personal account, just follow the same instructions below and be sure to use a new email address as opposed to the email address already associated with your Amazon account.

 

- To begin, sign in to your personal Amazon account.

 

- From the top **Account & List Menu** choose the **Register for a Business Account** option.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668321/194c838469ed7deb06fa0c3b5987/51609_7cb7c3d3c319a16ee0f506484bb6d299.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=0f8502fbf3ba6e9db71f56cbf2ba24a44fb6bc57c35fab08f686655f2ae0f0ed&req=1dxpw1D5rjZk2hL085ZhoVCqAceOsAm8WKvJB3f8Md4LmxTOaYLfxlEVwoJA%0AvkEo%2BhY6J4pW7gxp%0A) 

- Amazon will ask you to provide a business email address to use with this new account.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668339/17706d13efa7442014d3c920c31a/51609_272b5c78e8cda36ffc15a994a3f090b7.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=a544e779927888167a7789b4a11e88d9b2fca5a8794459f38cbfba41ddfe62a5&req=1dxpw1D5rz5k2hL085Zhobs9mu7vsrsJAixWmwmAhhcLQn20uNMmhZfproQc%0ANcX2dtt2ep%2Bd0jSt%0A)

# Sign up for Amazon Prime Duo (Prime Subscribers)

 

If you have a Prime account you can sign up for Prime Duo, which is designed for single users, small business owners or sole proprietors who want to separate their personal shopping from their business purchases. Business Prime Duo is free with your Prime membership.

 

Once you create a free Amazon Business account, if you’re an Amazon Prime member, you can sign up for a free Business Prime Duo plan.

 

- [Learn more and sign up for an Amazon Prime Duo Account - Amazon.com Users](https://business.amazon.com/en/find-solutions/business-prime/duo)

- [Learn more and sign up for an Amazon Prime Duo Account - Amazon.co.uk Users](https://business.amazon.co.uk/en/find-solutions/business-prime/duo)

 

 

# Setting up a Fetch Connection with Amazon Business

 

 

Important note: Supplier Rules currently do not work for items we pull from this connection.

 

- Go to **Business Settings** in the sidebar, then **Connections**.

- Then **Cost Connectors**, and click **Connect**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668355/04fe3730222a46e38089641b1c66/51609_bf0368f6de3f20e1dd41e47283a58f99.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=e68690baabcc6406856885f0d8175505cad474592718ef2589528ff63ffdb032&req=1dxpw1D5qTJk2hL085ZhoWetYLiAn8keWOA8hjNfPWNWYfXPgg1cr%2FC5up%2FV%0AdurJL9SA2bAyNQUv%0A) 

- On the next page, click the **Add connection** button in the top right corner.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668360/d2d5d0dd0d04c0d8fc8cc729df97/51609_aabeffea58cfc85bbcf967a11fef0fd2.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=087007ac6400dbefe50e50fa188ef567f1b3c05d0e92e188a5c5407e463eb453&req=1dxpw1D5qjdk2hL085ZhoVW2srhoEDQvIYyeTULJZ0p8py52h8DZzMlj3wP2%0AtqvKabI4i5MlCbvH%0A) 

- Find the relevant Amazon Business and click **Connect**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668370/31cf41eca9df508f6824c0e053a3/51609_a478522e2f2bdbbbb0e4649fd99a572a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=1c434ba5475a075d5f2781f6da2b622aebf32ebc1ea0e1f2728a071e30c2169c&req=1dxpw1D5qzdk2hL085ZhofF3saYh5vtfMs96UhUBT0ypAsVbTSKvoxeCs8ba%0AHauPozn%2FBKnv68qZ%0A) 

- Select the **Document Owner** for the costs collected from your Amazon account.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668386/db2e54fdb479e945aa007f2aa322/51609_6bb3bbbf6e89409d60681e248a4fc001.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=208bc2c4fb8e46333b393c99ea79706e90c78fe80b30932c1384097d8b67eb56&req=1dxpw1D5pDFk2hL085ZhoSaMawBXU3GeorV22fObaoFRan2Fkore%2FcZz%2BYro%0Ap%2FysdMTDj6tzxRv2%0A)
