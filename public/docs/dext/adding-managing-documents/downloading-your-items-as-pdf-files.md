You can download your documents from Dext as a **PDF**, **CSV**, or **ZIP** file. This makes it easy to share records, keep backups, or upload your financial data to an accounting platform.

 

## **Before you download**

PDF exports have a **250-item limit**. To stay within this limit, use **advanced search** and **filters** to refine your selection.

 

If you need to export **more than 250 items**, choose a **ZIP export**. ZIP files include items in their **original format**.

 

# **Where you can export items**

## **From the Costs or Sales inbox**

You can export items directly from the inbox:

- **To export selected items:** use the checkboxes to choose what you need, then open **Tools > Export**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81247923/5b7a8262fb369ffce6daaf6b3223/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=5de8f8f56320d2bd3c297b9158387f2e8daf32659a7f3b1e704fc28699c3b7ed&req=2NRtwV%2FzrjRk2hL085ZhoVK8iYPhZMhggoWmyYWg1pLhr9AQn0YidMlaDtUJ%0AFByy%2F9HHRvKVEX9e%0A)
- **To export everything in the inbox:** leave all items unselected, open **Tools**, and choose **Export all**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81249307/46643cae2121972e6bfb08926bed/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=f7461501235c1d043671c42cd62c938028a7a077f217a443dc7e5c1ba3124e49&req=2NRtwVH5rDBk2hL085ZhoepYkKFzb7wQiMCfS7kZbcIOy6s9Mu38H4Cd2dNU%0AD16DAWXjpgCDvlp5%0A)**Note**: Both options let you choose PDF, CSV, or ZIP formats. The 250-item limit applies to PDF exports.

 

## **From the Archive**

Items can also be exported from the Archive:

- Select specific documents and click **Export**, or

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81249749/a2a42af2f304a4abad731b335356/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=744817f351d45098f8a9207a901863eb49e775b4600556f2bfd805418196d6e6&req=2NRtwVH9qD5k2hL085ZhoeLhBT1FUthX4ITqmlzLuy2wCIpV%2FPZnGmJEbOte%0AXrJYJ71y0%2BRjtFGg%0A)
- Choose **Export all** to download the entire Archive in one go. 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81372160/642c91bf3849a40ab13fd9f21cc0/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=efdf9dcb2c541e6f77ef51a902fdef63ee3f37d85a09f6258d0cbd8cd31118d5&req=2NRswlr7qjdk2hL085ZhoYCje7CrM%2Bt5CEOOBadyaWUUTpiIb%2F8p9ug%2BTJIB%0AfM0okjp2rT7flB6B%0A)**Note**: The same export formats and PDF limits apply here.

 

# **How to download items as a PDF**

You can start a PDF export from either the Inbox or Archive. Once you open the export window, follow these steps:

- Select the **PDF** tab.

- Choose whether to move exported items to the Archive.

- Click **Export**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81251396/5de27c898e545ec39c9fad713dbf/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=58841bdbe1cbc61954ba2c0f65184dce5bfa6cd93591e1aeb8bf11cdbc87f5cf&req=2NRtwFn5pTFk2hL085ZhofS%2BBcAdslsUhK1alAIJbbCN9eHdagwob%2B83fmn1%0A17P88ybd22aYNZhu%0A) 

Dext will start generating your file and scan it for viruses. When the file is ready, click **Download** to save it.
