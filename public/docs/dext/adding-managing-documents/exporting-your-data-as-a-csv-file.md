You can export documents from Dext as a **CSV** (or PDF/ZIP) to share, analyze, or upload to your accounting software.

 

## **Before you export**

- Use **advanced search** and **filters** to refine your selection.

- You can export from the **Costs** or **Sales inbox** and from the **Archive**.

 

# **Where you can export items**

## **From the Costs or Sales inbox**

- **Export selected items:** Use the checkboxes, then go to **Tools > Export**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81368067/5d15fcdd29efb22109fcf4a0eab6/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=28edc33b4f9518897df717760634eb0919fa3646a4961c35a486e96f4eeff59a&req=2NRsw1D6qjBk2hL085ZhoWElWt1dRygb6susGtZ8ZzwJyqG%2B8YtJB5dZ%2FJYG%0AA5Pis5MDfgT0Mf1H%0A)
- **Export everything in the inbox:** Leave all items unselected, open **Tools**, and choose **Export all**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81368393/0fb38433c8ca6ec6c6bb3c594473/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=6ad2d7aba0fc1b96af6e5fcbb493647a4893516ab926a36086bdfb3362a25f4e&req=2NRsw1D5pTRk2hL085ZhoX7I2QMRbEeVcDMOUe5MYi61%2BJutnJeSEc7HU6BK%0AfWe9DDBOxyLWPzd4%0A) 

## **From the Archive**

- **Export selected items:** Select the documents and click **Export**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81368497/d069c047ff580b5f85a124258f86/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=daba187caae89e23dfa75dd8757b5ed033e41907061ffef97b0049c5742d1381&req=2NRsw1D%2BpTBk2hL085ZhoRG%2BNXZC5ZXaJcdPKXWsjaXZLLY9hEdTS%2FIhLszV%0AltJ6tWBXXAcxfIq9%0A)
- **Export everything in the Archive:** Click **Export all**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81368725/3c99ff458c8b7e53b6a06c001920/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=aa6e45e57cfe5ea8d9e26f7cebf274c9c2676348993f4329bb69773321419d57&req=2NRsw1D9rjJk2hL085ZhodDStbW5nW1FGxCvKl1CdxZTX6R2VLh9zuOpIRwV%0AnR31Rive2tnAkFfY%0A) 

# **How to export items as a CSV**

You can start a CSV export from either the inbox or the Archive. Once the **export window** is open, follow these steps:

- Select the **CSV** tab.

- Choose your **CSV format** from the dropdown.

**Tip**: **Admin users** can create a **custom CSV format** in **Business settings > Exports** and then **select it in the export window**. **[Learn more here](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/425561-creating-and-using-a-custom-csv-export)**.

   3. Choose whether to **move exported items to the Archive**.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81368947/20a202c82faaa1a86996d2754796/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=3e2e083f519f07eefc863d44f2fd78d9929e1090ff8a1995fac556278e49b304&req=2NRsw1DzqDBk2hL085ZhofOQsdzeF%2FwnpWZlB49ON8R%2B%2BF9l%2FV3ruyhJAPZ9%0ARXrEwT0NZj%2F6bRc%2F%0A) 

   4. Click **Export**, then click **Download** when the file is ready.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81369214/d26954d040b6df95229a11df5b00/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=6ef3e54b6515adc12eeb3d66072c86e2278ced817effa175d5e8d2c53de16017&req=2NRsw1H4rTNk2hL085ZhocGln5qpvq1Xn1kWwuwR4DQHp8Hx%2Be5QAVVlgPpx%0Al9Je8c%2BmEIYecEZP%0A) 

# **Troubleshooting CSV exports**

## **Error: “Please set total amount for each of the items in the export”**

This means there’s at least one item in your selection with **no total amount** (not even 0).

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81369589/cfe0e9cc5800a0dbd7ba49b42df5/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=d979accbc4f1ad948849268b944b323a04bc12111965e7b6c6f367e70b38797c&req=2NRsw1H%2FpD5k2hL085ZhofJs00wP4s117ryI3qOTefofgNdrxAbtGStf8FQZ%0ADQRIgDys%2FRBRiJWx%0A) 

### **To fix it:**

- Go to **Costs > Inbox** or **Costs > Archive**.

- Click the **Total** column to **sort by descending** total amount.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81369880/f5af6823b4b674fbeced9d993791/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=e07ab09e54c546cb1b0713dbdb68619efd4d9c6f71cdb56060feca287e1c172a&req=2NRsw1HypDdk2hL085ZhoXdHrV2M990zClrdm88d6WZcOcTg4DH99XYB%2Bm6J%0AGDQYpCSZMiqEK1ZA%0A) 

   3. Find the item with **no total**, open it, and enter a value (**0 is allowed**).

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/81370147/8d7832b970d2a87023e02fa757da/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=e8f827b1052d17f8617296abf2551da960b274da0337d48b00acd75ecaf16877&req=2NRswlj7qDBk2hL085ZhoQStgVaU1WZsWgKOxDcQm4h8ameSyDR%2FtD2HqKpE%0AsCM%2FPhq1fPbboxvm%0A) 

   4. Click **outside the field** to save the change.

   5. Re‑run your CSV export.
