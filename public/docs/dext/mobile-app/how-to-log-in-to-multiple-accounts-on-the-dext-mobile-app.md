# If you have more than one Dext account to manage multiple businesses, you may need to swap between logins while using the Dext Mobile App.

 

**If you own multiple businesses**, follow the instructions below to add a separate account to your login page.

 

- [iOS App](#ios-app)

- [Android App](#android-app)

 

 

**If you&#x27;re an accountant or bookkeeper**, follow [these instructions](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-switch-between-different-client-accounts-on-the-apple-ios-mobile-app) to learn how to view and manage your different client accounts on the mobile app.

 

 

# iOS [Apple] App

 

To add a separate account:

 

- Click the **Settings **button in the bottom-right of the screen.

- Go to **Switch Logins**.

- Tap **Add login.**

- Select **Sign up with email** to create a new account or **Log in** to add the login details for another account.

 

Once this is completed, your accounts will be listed on the Switch Logins page and the account you&#x27;re currently logged in with will be indicated by a green tick.

 

Switch between accounts at any time by tapping the desired account from the list.

 

 

# Android App

 

- Click the **Settings** button in the bottom-right of the screen.

- Select **Logins**.

- Click **Add Existing Account** and fill in the login details of the account.

- You can now switch between accounts by pressing **Accounts** and selecting your desired account.

 

Once this is completed, your accounts will be listed on the Accounts page and the account you&#x27;re currently logged in with will be indicated by a green tick.

 

Switch between accounts at any time by tapping the desired account from the list.

 

If you need to remove a logged-in account from the Mobile App, read [this article](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/How-to-remove-accounts-from-the-mobile-app).
