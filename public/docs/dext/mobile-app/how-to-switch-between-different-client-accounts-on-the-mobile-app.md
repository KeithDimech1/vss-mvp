# Switch between your list of Client Accounts or Multiple Dext Accounts on the Dext Mobile App, and easily access and add to the financial records of each of your businesses and clients.

 

To switch between clients on the Mobile App:

 

- Log in to the Dext Mobile App using the same username and password that you use to access your partner account on the Dext web app.

- Click the initial in the top left corner to be taken to the client list.

- Tap the name of the Client you want to view or submit on behalf of.

You&#x27;ll see their inbox, which includes their name at the top of the screen.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59662769/3285f0c331853cf0be8bd2533453/2894_c89b9bddffc5140aa01dd117c36a6cd0.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=1cda06edc7b521b935a8d3c0b67368f3a2e9ff039682672e8b83106a0d26ac22&req=1dxpw1r9qj5k2hL085ZhoTLTRwvkBRVXs6%2BdqjfV3X%2FTblToi4opAWPmzku%2F%0AKhdXv%2FdKzE4nOtj%2F%0A) 

- Submit your clients&#x27; documents as usual by tapping the camera button, selecting who you are submitting on behalf of, taking a picture of the document, and adding any necessary details to the image.

- When finished, tap your initial in the top-left of the screen to return to your **client list**.

 

**If you own multiple businesses**, follow the instructions below to add a separate account to your login page:

 

- Press the **Settings **button in the bottom-right of the screen.

- Go to **Switch logins**.

- Press **Add login** and then fill in the login details of your account.

- You can now switch between accounts by selecting the desired log-in.
