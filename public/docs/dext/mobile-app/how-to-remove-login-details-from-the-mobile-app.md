# If you’ve previously logged into multiple Dext accounts on the Mobile App, you may want to remove logins that are no longer needed.

 

To remove login details from the mobile app:

 

- Open the Dext mobile app.

- Click the **Settings** button in the bottom right corner of the screen.

- Select **Switch logins**.

- Swipe left on the name of the you want to remove.

- Press **Remove.**
