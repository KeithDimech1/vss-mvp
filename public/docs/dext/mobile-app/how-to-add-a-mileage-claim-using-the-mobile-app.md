# **Supported Regions**

Mileage claim calculations are only available in the following regions:

- Australia

- Canada

- France

- United Kingdom

- United States

Dext accounts that are not set to one of the above regions will not be able to access the feature. This is due to providing a regionalised and compliant experience for the countries we support. If you would like to request support for additional countries, please raise a UServoice ticket.

# Creating Mileage Claims

 

To create a mileage claim:

 

- Click the up arrow next to the camera icon and click **Create mileage claim.**

- Select **Log trip manually.**

- Swipe up on the **Your trip **section to allow you to enter details of your mileage claim.

- Select the vehicle the trip was made in, you can also add a new vehicle in this step if needed.

- Select the Document owner and the date of the trip.

- Enter the destinations you’ve travelled from and to. You can also add in additional destinations if you made extra stops on your journey.

- Select whether it was a round trip using the toggle and once happy, click **Create**.

 

 

**Note**: Once created, you&#x27;ll find your mileage claim in the Cost Inbox.

# GPS Mileage Tracking

 

You can now capture and submit mileage claims using GPS!

 

To create a mileage claim with GPS tracking:

 

- Click the up arrow next to the camera icon and click **Create mileage claim.**

- Select** Start GPS tracking. **

- (Optional) You can pause your tracking mid trip if you’re making multiple stops throughout your journey.

 

If you navigate away from the Dext app, we&#x27;ll continue to track your trip whilst in the background. We&#x27;ll also save all recorded trips so you don&#x27;t lose them until you&#x27;re ready to submit them.

 

- Click** Finish** once you’ve completed your journey.

- Swipe up on the** Your trip **section to allow you to enter details of your mileage claim.

- Once happy, click **Create.**
