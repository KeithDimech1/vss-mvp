# How To Submit Documents Using The Mobile App

 

1. Open the mobile app and sign-in using your Dext email address and password

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608694/1032ee73c71bb017e13ee97acf08/352_190381faa528e009442f97c923cbea74.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=b061ea4e1ff4160f9dacb868e266f0fcd131e004b904d3c1903deed23b07343b&req=1tBpxVD8pTNk2hL085ZhoSB6xRSSmRRZO3YE2aM%2FLO58udxQ8u6kyE%2BDghEc%0A9c26woCfYZwCeY5D%0A) 

2. Go to the **Costs** or **Sales** inbox.**If you have not yet added any documents the inbox will be empty.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608700/7a908cf70609113918b1a9a23e79/352_9a5f70484e51e92e0fa6c25b4c695dfc.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=4335ef6170533f8abaffc9f18fca6b36727810fe4bc467384909b069764bdb73&req=1tBpxVD9rDdk2hL085ZhoS0yoZZxKBcK8pGrmqLCDr%2FK3oWgD5AuO0JzMTsb%0AY4KEJMMsbK3OIYKj%0A) 

3. Capture documents by tapping the green +** button at the bottom of the screen. This will open the camera.**There are multiple camera modes on the Mobile App that allow any type of paper document to be captured easily.

- **Multiple Mode** is for when you have several documents that you would like to capture. You can take up to 50 photos of individual documents before submitting. Dext will then separate each photo into a separate item.

- **Combine Mode** is for when the information you need to capture is spread over several pages (up to a maximum of 50) or you have a very long receipt and all the information will not fit in one picture frame. Dext will then combine your photos into one item.

**Write your description here** is where you can add any notes about the document.

 

**Customer** is where you can include if the receipt or invoice is for a particular customer.

# How to share PDFs to the Mobile App

 

You can submit PDF documents using the mobile app by:

 

- Navigating to your **Files**.

- Selecting the PDFs you wish to upload.

- Clicking on “**Share**“ > “**Dext**”

 

You can also add PDFs from your gallery by:

 

- Logging into your Dext Mobile App.

- Clicking the green add button and selecting the image icon in the bottom left corner to open your gallery.

- Select the PDF image from your gallery.

 

Your files will then be uploaded to your account.

 

 

# How to Whatsapp your Items to the Mobile App

 

If you have the Dext Mobile App downloaded, you can share files you receive on Whatsapp with Dext without leaving Whatsapp!

 

To share a file from Whatsapp to Dext:

 

- Open the invoice in Whatspp by clicking onto it.

- Click the Share button and select Dext.

- Select whether it’s a Cost or Sales item, the Account it’s going to and who owns the document.

- Once you’re happy click the share button and the item will be sent to Dext!

# How to Create an Expense claim using the Mobile App

 

To create an expense claim, you’ll need to:

 

- Log into the mobile app

- Select the items you’d like to add to the claim by:

 

- Pressing and hold on an item to see check boxes

- Click the **Checkmark** button in the top right corner

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608812/747e0792dd948183248bb21164df/352_5f09032ee158da541aca1375c5020965.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=579b64752683eb2de2356b7abedce9e2fd6c00be372a013f92f3b4631103fffb&req=1tBpxVDyrTVk2hL085ZhoeD%2BsgAWO7fnAkp2Dtx1hzfBuZa94wyM9WYZCffn%0AISQMxOFuWdY%2Fi9FF%0A) 

- Click the three dots in the bottom corner and select **Create Expense claim**

- Enter the requested details for the report and click **Create**

 

 

# How to create a new Expense claim in the Expense claim Space

 

You can create expense claims in the mobile app before selecting the items you’d like to add to the claim! To do this:

 

- Log into the mobile app.

- Click the **Expense claims **button.

- Click the green **+** button and select the items to add to the report using the check boxes.

- Click **Create.**

- Enter the details for the expense report and click **Create. **

 

**Note: **If you don’t have any expense claims in your workspace, you can also click the **Create** button to select the items you’d like to add to the claim.

 

 

If you’d like to add items to an existing expense claim, take a look at [this article](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-edit-an-expense-report).
