# The Dext Mobile App is available on all iOS [Apple] and Android devices.

 

There are a number of ways to access the Dext Mobile App:

 

- Press the link in the invitation Email or Text you receive from your Bookkeeper/Accountant

- Enter the Username and Password you&#x27;ve previously set up for your account

- Single-sign-on with your Intuit credentials

 

Download the [iOS Mobile App](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/app/receipt-bank/id418327708) or the [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.receiptbank.android&hl=en) if you haven&#x27;t already. Open the app and login using any of the previously mentioned methods.
