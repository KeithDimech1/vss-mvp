**When you delete your account from the Dext mobile app, all the data in your Dext account will also be deleted. **

 

Before deleting your account, we recommend that you save all your account data, including item images and line items.

 

Only the user who owns the account (the creator) has the ability to delete their Dext account from the mobile app.

 

 

**Note:** If you&#x27;d like to delete your account and you don&#x27;t see the option to delete your account under the settings section of the mobile app, you&#x27;ll need to reach out to our support team to request an account deletion.

 

- [Backup Item Images](#image)

- [Backup Your Data](#data)

 

 

# Backup Item Images

 

There are three ways to save your item images:

 

**1.** Backup to an online cloud storage service. You can connect your Dext account with four cloud storage services:

 

- Google Drive

- Dropbox

- Everial Digital Expert

- OneDrive

 

To connect with any of these services, follow the steps in [this article](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-back-up-your-paperwork).

 

**2.** Export images as a PDF

 

To do this, go to your Inbox, select the items you want to export using the checkboxes.

 

Once you&#x27;ve selected your items, click Tools and then Export, choose PDF, then click Export to download your invoices in one PDF file.

 

If you&#x27;d like to export all documents, you can click the Export All button under the Tools button.

 

 

**Note**: You can only export 250 items into a PDF at a time. If you’d like to export more than this at one time, we’d recommend exporting your items as a ZIP file.

**3. **Export images in a ZIP file.

 

To do this, go to your Inbox, select the items you want to export using the checkboxes.

 

Once you’ve selected your items, click Tools and then Export, choose ZIP, then click Export to download your invoices in a ZIP file. The invoices will be downloaded in their original format.

 

If you’d like to export all documents, you can click the Export All button under the Tools button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608615/9519f9b996656d9867774e249be5/878_dd035b8d1034f1e9434783d45520d40e.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=03e5a0ac0a2d41459d34655b966d621d31ab48c7c4971dfefbf683810d249584&req=1tBpxVD8rTJk2hL085ZhoQNs%2BWbayeRZguGiKe01yaA7QOhBt7DKlzAX3wYx%0AIaycHYcl1PjXZmZ3%0A) 

 

This will not export items that are already archived. You&#x27;ll have to do these steps from the Archive tab of each of your workspaces. You can also archive the items in your Inbox and export them all in a single folder from the Archive tab.

 

 

# Backup Your Data

 

Before deleting your account, be sure to backup your line items.

 

Just like with exporting as a PDF, select the transactions you want to export from the Inbox and/or from the Archive.

 

Then click Export and select your preferred format.
