# Insert your company logo into the Dext Mobile App and make your partnership with Dext immediately visible to users on your account.

 

 

**Note: **This functionality is only available for Accountant/Bookkeeper accounts. You must also have admin privileges to make this change, see more about user privileges [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions).

 

To insert your company logo, you&#x27;ll need to:

 

- Log into the [Dext web app](https://app.dext.com/login).

- Navigate to your **Internal Practice account**.

- Click **Business Settings**.

- Under the **Business profile**, upload your logo.
