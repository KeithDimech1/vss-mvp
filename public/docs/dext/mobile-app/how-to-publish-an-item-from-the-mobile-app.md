# If a Dext Account is integrated with an Accounting Software, you can send submitted documents directly from the Costs or Sales Inbox to a specified location in your Accounting Software.

 

 

**Note: **You must have publishing rights as part of your [user privilege](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions) to publish items from the Mobile App. Reach out to your account administrator to get access.

 

To publish an item from the mobile app:

 

- Go to the **Costs** or **Sales** inbox and open the item.

- Swipe up to check the Item details are correct and view the publishing destination.

- Click the **Publish** button.

 

Once published, you can find your document under the archive section.
