# Account Management

 

# How to invite your Accountant or Bookkeeper

 

You can invite your Accountant or Bookkeeper by navigating to the Accountant section under Business Settings.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665927/2d8c19ddacb4e0aa641d4219562e/46604_d0cec81254e4efb637498651921796eb.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=953a4ea860a2afa5a34a48a0eeadd0b15b42df4af80e9aa223ee4168bd6958a7&req=1dxpw13zrjBk2hL085ZhoRbqNuNJLy0BtedS6R5w%2FcsmEuIjodQDf%2BQLWNpP%0AgIAhri4ODmOSZZt3%0A) 

You’ll then be prompted to enter your Accountant or Bookkeepers email address.

 

 

# How to remove your Accountant or Bookkeeper

 

To remove your Accountant or Bookkeeper’s access:

 

- Navigate to Business settings **and** **click **Accountant**.

- Click **Disconnect**.

- Review the option on the panel that appears and then click **Disconnect**.

 

 

# How to add a new Accountant or Bookkeeper

 

To allow another Accountant or Bookkeeper to access your account:

 

- Navigate to **Business settings** and click **Accountant**.

- Select the **Invite someone else **button and add your new Accountant or Bookkeepers email address.

- Click the** Send invite** button.

 

 

# Subscriptions

 

# How to modify your plan

 

If you need to modify your plan, you can do so by:

 

- Clicking into** Business Settings** then **Subscription**.

- Click the** Change plan** button.

- Change the plan by adjusting the amount of users and click **Upgrade.**

- (Optional) Add any extra add-ons you’d like and click **Continue**.

- Confirm you’re happy with your new plan by clicking **Upgrade**.

 

 

# How to view your Item Extraction limit

 

You can get an overview of how many items you’ve processed and how many are remaining by navigating to **Business settings**, **Subscription **and then **View usage**.

# How to deactivate your Dext account

 

To deactivate your Dext account:

 

- Click into **Business settings** and then select **Subscription**.

- Navigate to the **Billing** tab and select **Cancel subscription**.

- Follow the on screen prompts to confirm the cancellation.
