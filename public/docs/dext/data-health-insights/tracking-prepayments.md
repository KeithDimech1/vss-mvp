**Maintain visibility on prepayment activity with the Prepayments tracker. **

**Important**: This feature is currently only available for accounts connected with Xero.

To view prepaid expenses, in the client’s account go to **Data Health** then **Prepayments**.

The tracker:

- Groups manual journals using a naming convention:

Prepayment - SupplierName - DocReference - ItemID**​

- Displays:

Total amount prepaid

- Amount recognised to date

 

## Reconciliation Check**

A system check matches initial prepayment journal entries with the associated monthly recognition journals. It will:

- Identify and group entries using narration

- Flag errors such as:

Missing journals

- Date misalignments

- Totals that do not match the invoice amount

Users can **retrofit** historical journals into the tracker by editing narrations to follow the naming pattern, ensuring backward compatibility without needing to reimport or recreate data.
