❗ **Important: Transaction cleanup is available for QuickBooks Online clients only.**

 

 

# **The three cleanup checks**

Each check can be expanded to show a list of matching transactions.

 

## **Parent Coded Transactions**

Shows transactions coded to **parent-level accounts** when **sub-accounts** exist. Updating these improves reporting and keeps your chart of accounts tidy.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/63992766/c5d15012ba56d2b6ef45f397b3f2/0da68358-7e89-4b18-9192-65c52c6eb81f?expires=1765161000&signature=b68559b12c352e88fc6ce3c4f579df60737c797a92dbc00ac29878d77d8ae665&req=1tZmzFr9qjFk2hL085ZhoTZ8hDegZjCEeWPR%2BQpxI40XeuJg1YrEukH%2BnS8%2F%0A4TnZEG6Pk0CnSjVT%0A) 

## **Uncategorised Transactions**

Displays transactions posted to the system-created **“Uncategorised”** account. These likely need to be reviewed and properly coded.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/63992770/9f44c79860780c60e2b6d1c53a9e/83ae61e4-17fa-4b27-a8a3-1d812507468e?expires=1765161000&signature=7c4fa1fa85b3665105fe969f10d81b64e532c472ac1f29d28af0a179070a4c89&req=1tZmzFr9qzdk2hL085ZhoWbGppBOkJWfBTOvVtsYnZReC2ga7hW3qttwg99D%0AZvR6CtNUc5sF9flI%0A) 

## **Transactions Without a Contact**

Lists transactions missing a **customer or supplier**. Filling in this info is key to better AP/AR tracking and reporting.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/63996562/f5eea7a56feaac580e81e2a7d1b3/7a72bb64-5187-48bd-aa63-0e01643a6c53?expires=1765161000&signature=2487286837fcab57a9f95cc0e07da6e0fd178d1dcad007fd39b11297d6f7b26b&req=1tZmzF7%2FqjVk2hL085ZhoZUUrIzMw7NVQ38R4qNK51AYAODDEarw0UV%2Bu5NC%0AYz1E0NAQVkSNGXhx%0A) 

 

# **Why cleanup checks matter**

Cleaning up your transactions helps you:

- Keep financial reports accurate and reliable

- Make reconciliation smoother

- Maintain clean and complete records

If any issues appear in the checks, it&#x27;s best to review and update those transactions.
