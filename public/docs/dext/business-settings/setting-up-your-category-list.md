**Applying the correct nominal code to your purchases and expenses is an essential part of accounting. Dext makes it easy to set up your Chart of Accounts, whether you’re working with a cloud or desktop accounting solution.**

 

 

This article is relevant to the enhanced Dext experience. You can access the article for the legacy Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-set-up-your-category-list-chart-of-accounts).

 

In Dext, we refer to each section of your Chart of Accounts as &#x27;Categories&#x27;. You can set them up differently depending on whether you&#x27;re using the application with an [integrated accounting software](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/339551-how-to-connect-dext-to-accounting-software).

 

Learn how to set up your Category Lists:

 

- [With an integration](#h_32870f5a-c367-4f27-a06e-9b4a0c8b7d6e)

- [Without an integration](#h_f344deab-a2c5-4b22-8ea9-93be661f5bb2)

 

 

# With an Integration

 

When you integrate a Dext account with cloud accounting software, we’ll automatically pull across your Chart of Accounts. To view these, click on **Business Settings **in the bottom left of the screen, then **Lists**, and finally **Categories**.

 

Any edits you make to your Chart of Accounts within your accounting package will also take effect within Dext. We check and renew your Chart of Accounts every 48 hours, but you can also manually update these lists by pressing the green **Reload **button.

 

If you don&#x27;t want a certain nominal code to be available to view or assign to transactions, you can hide specific Categories by turning the toggle in the **Visible** column to **OFF**.

# Without an Integration

 

There is one default list of nominal codes: **default list - simple**. You can use it or choose to import or create your own Chart of Accounts:

 

- **Import your Chart of Accounts**

 

Download your Chart of Accounts from your accounting software as a .CSV file.

 

To do this, click **Import from CSV** at the top of the page.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665614/d7fe4424f404f999a6a123bb3cca/45790_fa3011d2830e1d6a0a5a718d3f353669.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=3b12108c3b77ad04dd419a882b77785bdd4e13b1b67ce55b049f9080985e3a75&req=1dxpw138rTNk2hL085ZhoQznNXcpdKhh%2Fw83W9%2BCBk9uVnFj1kCJVypGj9fX%0A5La1k6wqFUKvIbag%0A) 

From there, simply select the CSV file you wish to upload.

 

 

- **Manually Create your Chart of Accounts**

 

To manually create your chart of accounts:

 

- Go to **Manage groups** at the top of the page, then click **Add group**. You can also duplicate an existing category if desired by clicking **Duplicate group**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665626/b2f0a35e6a3331a4d08acdd55208/45790_13e6173e5aba5c03718094917722eae7.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=1e90f078d06082ce4d6847d2ffd7e2e3fc7e7d82af37abdd4adaff1a5d3788a9&req=1dxpw138rjFk2hL085ZhoXSA2CgiPYxpv1ohcq%2FfOWN5IfKFzFGgSxgCTuBG%0A66jPQZtBlOjPpF79%0A) 

- Enter the names of the categories and their codes, then click **Save**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665642/4c1d878f8eeeb09d8d5e2b3f16aa/45790_047a0fce666caefda7a4b6918d73f2ed.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=3051b9801f6f2a5f2f6af2106cc34046336547f98941d3a006f1b606de976bfe&req=1dxpw138qDVk2hL085ZhoSCULaSCmxJGFAOe2StF7D%2BgdhvmIaNr356YxfKM%0AMbo5depJnxgSdU%2Fu%0A) 

You can edit your categories at any time from the Category page by clicking on the category **Name** or **Code** and making the necessary changes.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665653/4d093c48928e6d4bfa4d60c03fa2/45790_a77d6f6eac040b9ff35ec392393713b6.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=6ca2ce0553202b420b99d9b58aafba3a72017de5e4b5b23e04e784142d730a0b&req=1dxpw138qTRk2hL085ZhofatEA%2FrqRjQ9NCnw3bPz7IQ25zjCGeI9ARtTogW%0ARws0TN5XvBTxfBEG%0A) 

If you don&#x27;t want a certain nominal code to be available to view or assign to transactions, you can hide specific Categories by turning the toggle in the **Visible** column to **OFF**.

 

You can also remove a category if desired by clicking **Delete** next to the category.
