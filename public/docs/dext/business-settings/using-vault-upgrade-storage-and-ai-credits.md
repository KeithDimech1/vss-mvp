With **Vault**, you can purchase additional **storage** and **AI credits** as part of your **Dext Business subscription**. This will allow you to store and organise more important documents all in one place.

 

Every Dext plan includes a standard free allowance of **5 AI credits** **per month** and **100 MB of storage**.

One AI credit equals one processed document - this includes extracting the summary, smart tags, file name, and due date from files uploaded to Vault. Without an AI credit, this information won’t be extracted automatically.

 

## **How to Upgrade Your Vault Plan**

When you reach your **storage or AI credits limit**, Dext will show a message prompting you to upgrade. 

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/68768129/5fe772aed9b65032f384db279281/Screenshot+2025-10-09+at+13_43_47.png?expires=1765161000&signature=227f6261bee95dd4a3d695c2ed80206d491fca8f792fd3be66dd30a4fe7f4c6d&req=1t1ow1D7rj5k2hL085ZhoVJyN%2FVTmnYwKOYzeXG37Zts0M%2BqMNGoggLmkPwR%0Ab%2Ffsgq6Vk9SAZx0m%0A) 

**Follow these steps:**

- Go to **Business settings** > **Subscription**

- Go to the **Vault** section and click **Change plan**

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/68768368/a665d9c9a40d8b74d0b6b8088897/Screenshot+2025-10-10+at+14_17_07.png?expires=1765161000&signature=dcd702b8af1a748751d0f2c33a2197fe18d57f27151bca69312941b3ff1dd188&req=1t1ow1D5qj9k2hL085ZhoQSGghRKfmmRTw2ASv3QgDoQZmToh5laxxVijTzI%0AHIUPebRgDAwUewTW%0A) 

   3. Dext will suggest an upgrade based on your previous usage.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/68771051/5893a5f22019bc83f202f516d50e/Screenshot+2025-10-10+at+14_33_10.png?expires=1765161000&signature=52f6ab86767ab098e20787394c18aa90971fb29644b6156d2f102c5eff80b32e&req=1t1owln6qTZk2hL085ZhocZo%2F%2FKVfhD%2BWuVuTNLchVpl%2BLyynZcnlWyVTcnn%0ALmGynNIxcecFt8dz%0A)
- You can accept this recommendation or select a different number of bundles.

- Each **bundle** includes **50 AI credits** **per month** and **1 GB** **of storage**.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/68768822/82bd8a67557c0cacce46dfa114c8/Screenshot+2025-10-10+at+14_20_06.png?expires=1765161000&signature=25a033ebfc3d5367caeb279dc656cb3ce8a32fab52d9efafd0b8aa86bfcc22e9&req=1t1ow1DyrjVk2hL085Zhoeb1T9RCU3ej9v5xQr8AJnKHCqC2IpVdGxexLgqb%0ADKQyg2JorUOcCDtM%0A) 

   4. Click **Review upgrade**

   5. Select **Upgrade** to confirm your purchase. The cost of your upgrade will be applied starting from your **next billing date**.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/68769316/7c0a66fb39ecd8bdff6f6d14812e/Screenshot+2025-10-10+at+14_22_36.png?expires=1765161000&signature=79d0a194b6fa90ea136ca5cdc0deb55c90d37beab0d7881a1f8fa96ceb7c9726&req=1t1ow1H5rTFk2hL085ZhoZsxNSK2cQI6ZYOGHPVH5BQ79jnYOSv%2B6QFXwkMW%0ARFOjhWYD2PDUH%2BCN%0A) 

You will get a confirmation once the upgrade gets applied.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/68769420/086d93b440515abf32896cad70e0/Screenshot+2025-10-10+at+14_23_03.png?expires=1765161000&signature=209be7bef0b660a386aeb4c4b8837f07b3e4153949570e87d8b1269d9a084535&req=1t1ow1H%2Brjdk2hL085Zhod9JvgQ%2FsQJQ64sFfKeXediZ6wB52DE6tCKorexA%0ApI5%2FwUgqBvMkkRKU%0A) 

**Note**: If you reach your AI credit limit, you’ll still be able to upload documents, but AI-powered features such as summaries, smart tags, automatic extraction of file names, and due dates will be unavailable.
