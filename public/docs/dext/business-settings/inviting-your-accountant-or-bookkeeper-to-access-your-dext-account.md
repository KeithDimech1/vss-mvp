By inviting an external accountant or bookkeeper to Dext, they’ll be able to view, manage and collaborate with you on the paperwork that you’ve submitted to your account.

 

 

**Note: **No changes will be made to any account data when access is removed or granted to a new accountant.

 

If you’re currently connected to an accountant and wish to change to a different accountant then you can simply reallocate the access to the new accountant.

 

Depending on whether or not your accountant/bookkeeper uses Dext already, they’ll either be added to your account immediately, or contacted to see if they’d like to join Dext

 

You can only invite your accountants/bookkeepers to access your account if theirs is an accountant/bookkeeper account. If their email is linked to a business account you will get an error and won’t be able to invite them. In this case, you’ll need to contact your accountant/bookkeeper to set up an accountant/bookkeeper account or, alternatively, set them up as an Admin user in your own account.

- [How to invite your Accountant/bookkeeper that already uses Dext ](#already-uses-dext)

- [How to invite your Accountant/bookkeeper that doesn&#x27;t currently use Dext ](#does-not-use-dext)

 

 

# Where to go to invite your Accountant or Bookkeeper

 

- You can invite your accountant or bookkeeper from the **Accountant** page from within the **Business Settings**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608730/9dc3cf0799b3be9b8c5b831fa826/46897_45bd1e7cfaf749d226f3b2dcd34aa376.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=0a4401c369fdf605393fbe5f5a2d056b7280340aa4baf01bfe6baee93df0692c&req=1tBpxVD9rzdk2hL085ZhoURwDQXhRxgkdLIHEn0wbGkUuUD9%2FKaVWHyNB4x6%0Aak8uk7O3KHxAqzk6%0A) 

- Once on the Accountant page, enter the accountant’s email address, then click **Send invite.**

 

From here there are two flows:

 

- How to invite if your Accountant/bookkeeper already uses Dext

- How to invite if your Accountant/bookkeeper does not currently use Dext

 

 

# Accountant/bookkeeper already uses Dext

 

If your accountant/bookkeeper is already using Dext, you’ll see this message:

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608742/c423032ea96a9a9deac33a704e0c/46897_ba3e4247e95b4ee8fc7e388b3582f831.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=4ed089766f448a9dd8734ec2845ae71b05f3ab7600c59a9c1f2683afd268596e&req=1tBpxVD9qDVk2hL085ZhoZzYACCfEp%2FyHeQa06hW9q4D5gImCNhMIAzOkU4K%0AunkiH7v%2BPK%2B0K2W5%0A) 

- Click **Allow access.** They’ll be given permission to access your account and view, edit and publish your uploaded paperwork.

- When the connection is successful you’ll see a notification to confirm this.

 

You can manage this connection at any time by going to **Business settings**, then **Accountant**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608747/ef888ac296fd1a7f02f950fb7ef6/46897_f945ef4a158c8b2f28e27248d8707841.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=d9b2ac9e4bbf010adfce3662228b860069c9723b4ec8177147bff5661e5f77ad&req=1tBpxVD9qDBk2hL085ZhoeHD1Q%2B8Y2nfg%2FugplfaRIBKNrZ0ipElqe6iflg1%0A7Or1dup0HvtWZgXZ%0A) 

 

# Accountant/bookkeeper does not currently use Dext

 

If your accountant/bookkeeper does not subscribe to Dext, you can invite them to use Dext:

 

- Enter your accountant or bookkeeper’s email address, then click Send Invite.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608758/89a367b45080f897df90374df363/46897_45bd1e7cfaf749d226f3b2dcd34aa376.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=15b903e82cef0615481c85ca558f19e600dcd1bef9831cb268fa2fa3ad2c4562&req=1tBpxVD9qT9k2hL085ZhoRCJxWi9PpjgH3PyDFS5pY1o1iP8XJImnUNrYBw9%0ACpbVDEATX3f5%2BrjQ%0A) 

The accountant will be sent an invitation to start a free trial for accountants & bookkeepers.

 

If you want to see a preview of the email they will be sent, click **See how the email will look**.

 

- You can see the status of the invitation by going to **Business settings**, then **Accountant**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/65608768/a1972259cb0d3c8c99019c7ddead/46897_fb23b855d197d87d0457a60018208fea.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=631e48e9b2654635985aa97546f2b67553f30c158954d96461dec8d86c2fbf55&req=1tBpxVD9qj9k2hL085ZhoVCBfH92JfsqAjwx%2BILbkUp4P%2BavNrLtAqfv4aeC%0A%2BgAptw%2FrxTR5Tz0V%0A) 

- When your accountant accepts the invitation a pop up will show on the screen notifying you of the successful connection.
