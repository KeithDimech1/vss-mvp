# If your Dext account is integrated with any Accounting Software, we will check every 48 hours for any updated information and sync these to Dext.

 

This article is relevant to the enhanced Dext experience. You can access the article for the legacy Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-refresh-your-chart-of-accounts).

 

However, you might like to speed up this process, especially after creating new categories or adding new clients to your chart of accounts. Follow the below instructions to trigger an immediate sync.

 

- [**Refresh all lists**](#refresh-all-lists)

- [**Refresh only a specific list**](#refresh-one-list)

 

 

Important for businesses: You must be assigned the Admin level [role](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions) to be able to access the Connections page to refresh the chart of accounts. Reach out to your account admin, accountant or bookkeeper about getting access.

 

 

# Refresh all lists

 

Update **all the lists** at once (Categories, suppliers, customers, tax code…) by refreshing integration data. This is useful when you make several changes in your accounting software that need to be displayed in Dext.

 

To refresh integration data:

 

- Go to **Business Settings**, then **Connections**.

- Click **Manage** next to the connected accounting software, and in the drip down list click **Refresh integration data**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59666139/01af7fe8ee5a592ce360fb4f8891/47144_62a17cf25a49cbba3b4fe5b6d76d2416.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=da33dedeb5f554e1e2735d28d0b0280d4ee76c2fc04d33586c22a0fbebfebe7f&req=1dxpw177rz5k2hL085ZhoVhUAGgqUKrmrejGSS9zlo3NEb4YhW2W8o5K%2F99h%0ALbaIGMMGyTOqEytv%0A) 

**Note**: The **Reload** button will also re-sync the Payment Methods, Customers and Projects Lists.

 

The update of all your lists will start, and a confirmation message will be displayed when complete:

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59666143/bb8834ff588960045086f1ae6470/47144_96d1edc4c10f2303cdd79f58ca549ef1.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=8e0efa37b6d0e47e68c1889f9a14f6340afab7311d19bc31095f0e90f9d20429&req=1dxpw177qDRk2hL085ZhoabF8T%2B0nTNg1NuCEDxjF7HaiKYDXjewkzjhRosP%0ALDwaMSUZsm3H0idA%0A) 

 

# Refresh only a specific list

 

You can also refresh a specific list if needed. For example, you want to edit a supplier or customer in your accounting software:

 

- Go to the **Costs** section, then **Suppliers**. For customers go to **Sales**, then **Customers**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59666146/771a3ab143123808716604839aef/47144_c1d8fd9031d0dabbbb0911db3b64b4e9.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=1ec42c0ca9d18abd8ad01e0376122ec8a383750a267ffb18e26d80c7d1165022&req=1dxpw177qDFk2hL085ZhoSsaT2SGFhANW49ld0bBbe2K8LTwoeK0C4wC8N7s%0A%2Fi5gidEcXnnt%2FKlm%0A) 

- And click **Reload** at the top of the page to update the list.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59666150/0d783279b15d2546c51092c2f0d4/47144_900abd235d660aafb53c3750ca2c25a5.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=64ae3daa87c03ea2f31ae97bdc868eb7cd9971cf4902f130f74eb94a3e7495c9&req=1dxpw177qTdk2hL085ZhoVlKX6rUWFBr2AUGtfTdmsIXUXDhaFtYW%2BLfkwBL%0AThZcoIT01Sy9onsg%0A)
