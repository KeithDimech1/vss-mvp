**Submit expense claims for approval.**

 

 

# Submitting an Expense Claim

 

When you have completed an expense claim and want to submit it for approval:

 

- Go to **Costs**, then **Expense Claims**

- If an approval workflow has been set up by your approver(s) to manage your request for approval, there will be a **Request approval** button next to your expense claim. Click the button and your request will be sent to your approver.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668737/4897de6a8862bf97da459e6b3a56/56275_8d7d05fe34c83a96651f5ecc2cd95111.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=4d3cae3c00d149fab28bda7975e3b3810a42b74e518cfab29dae0e06be799f35&req=1dxpw1D9rzBk2hL085ZhoW9A4B4jfiRDq6H7Pikb7D%2BNTrklptef0vFxhYpW%0AhRveO9EFEl7Ohzfg%0A) 

- If the request approval button isn&#x27;t showing, check the box next to the expense claims you wish to request approval for (you can select multiple if needed), then click **Submit for approval** at the top of the page.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668746/bf32c35d6cd72e7ad2146452374a/56275_728985130d7d6c37ce31da5aefbed0e2.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=71abfa0ee846e494fa67ab6ef26013a20e1441fdf8f9dc7d429273a90afc464d&req=1dxpw1D9qDFk2hL085ZhoTMnQgFPCbtzKDzoFnlyKLYIXcydUJH6qLO3ntIJ%0Aus8MDjVOG1rh86fN%0A) 

- Then choose an Assigned Approver. The Assigned Approver can be; a user set up as an Expense Approver, the Admin User for the account, or your Bookkeeper/Accountant who can access your account. When ready, click **Submit**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668755/a030156a965237ddbc9ffbc13e3d/56275_79efc5f98600a1b95b8e36ebc9010dfe.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=b131f335de59049aa92568dcd5fd2287ea11815ae8d874ae9156650a59eb8445&req=1dxpw1D9qTJk2hL085ZhoR4imVRWgddFU%2F5KHyW7ntqLfThvrAWGP8V%2BuhSZ%0AxV2SajkG5GLYHm%2FO%0A) 

- The submitted expense claim will be marked as **Waiting** in the &#x27;Approval status&#x27; column.**​
Once approved, the claim will be marked as Approved**.
