**Supplier and Customer Rules** help automate how documents from specific suppliers or customers are processed in Dext. 

 

Once a rule is set, it’s automatically applied to every new document uploaded from that supplier or customer - reducing manual entry and speeding up review.

**Important**: You need to be **[an](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/215320-roles-permissions)**[ **Admin** **user**](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/215320-roles-permissions) to create and manage Supplier/Customer Rules. 

 

# How rules work

These rules are useful when documents from a specific supplier or customer consistently follow a pattern. For example:

- Uber receipts are always categorised as **Travel**

- Adobe, Google, or Apple invoices are categorised as **IT Costs**

- Utility bills are categorised as **Utilities**

Rules eliminate repetitive categorisation for recurring suppliers or customers.

 

# What you can automate

With Supplier or Customer Rules, you can set default values for:

- Category

- Invoice due date

- Tax rate

- Payment method

- Paid/Unpaid status

- Description

- Currency

**Note:** If your account is connected to accounting software like **QuickBooks Online**, **Xero**, or **Sage 50**, additional fields may be available. These integrations also support suppliers in multiple currencies (e.g., CAD and USD).

You can set rules either from the item itself or from your Suppliers/Customers lists.

# **Supplier-specific workflow notes**

You can now add a workflow note to a supplier to help standardise the coding of their documents. 

 

This note appears at the top of the[ Item Details page](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/105676-the-item-details-page) for every new document from that supplier, giving your team a clear reminder of what to check or fill in.

 

## **How it works**

- Go to **Costs** > **Suppliers** > click on a supplier to open the **Supplier rules** page.

- Alternatively, open an item from the **Costs inbox** and click on **Set supplier rules** under the** Supplier **field.

- Find the **Specific workflow** field at the bottom.

- Enter any short instruction you want users to follow, such as “Always fill in the invoice number” or “Verify tax rate before publishing”.

- Once saved, the workflow note automatically displays on the **Item Details** page for all items from that supplier.

# Setting rules in bulk

You can apply rules to multiple suppliers or customers at once.

 

### For Suppliers:

- Go to **Costs > Suppliers**.

- Tick the checkboxes next to the suppliers you want to update.

- Click **Bulk Edit** in the top-left corner.

- Select and apply your rules.

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/71669425/24a02a8a7383dd7a2eab3845bb72/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=7cd4b00193a507cd4caff5d6e42faef650092ceef9efaf1fcd8d46e502841f01&req=19Rpw1H%2BrjJk2hL085ZhocabaFUiY%2FU025heYB3ZT7bktBW1aPWFpyBvKIem%0AzrvtkVvSgoDQgCYU%0A)

# Rule prioritisation and auto-publish

- **Auto-Publish Rules** only apply to documents uploaded **after** the rule is created, regardless of where you set the rule from. They’re triggered automatically after data extraction.

- **Payment Method Rules**, **User Rules**, and **Auto-Publish Exceptions** will override Supplier or Customer Rules if set for the same field.

- Supplier and Customer Rules will override **account-level defaults** if set for the same field, for example, default due dates.

 

# Frequently asked questions

### Do rules apply to existing documents by default?

No, only to new documents. To apply rules to existing inbox items, edit them directly from the **Rules** page or use the **Apply to all inbox items** checkbox when editing from the **Item Details page**.

**Important**: Rules like **Auto-publish** and **Extract line items** can&#x27;t be applied to items already in your inbox. 

### Can I edit all fields in bulk?

Not all. Specific fields linked to accounting integrations may be unavailable to edit in bulk.

 

### Do rules support multiple currencies?

Yes, but only if connected to [QuickBooks Online](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/106082-how-to-publish-multiple-currencies-to-quickbooks-online?q=publishing+multiple+currencies+to+quickbooks), [Xero](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/219965-publishing-multiple-currencies-to-xero), or [Sage 50](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/105684-how-sage-50-multiple-currency-works-in-dext-prepare). 

 

### Why are my supplier/customer rules resetting or not applying?

If you’re connected to accounting software, check if you’ve set up rules for the supplier/customer there - these will often overwrite the rules in Dext when we sync the data every 48 hours. 

 

If you removed or made changes to the rules in the accounting software, make sure to [refresh the integration data](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/209127-refreshing-your-chart-of-accounts) in Dext to reflect them.
