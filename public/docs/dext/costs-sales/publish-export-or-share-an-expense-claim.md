**Expense Claims can be published to an integrated accounting software, exported in CSV and PDF formats or automatically sent via email.**

 

After completing an Expense Claim on Dext Prepare, you can process it onwards in a number of different ways:

 

- [Publishing to an integrated software](#publish)

- [Exporting as a PDF or CSV](#export)

- [Sharing via email](#email)

- [Sharing via Image Sharing](#image)

 

 

# Publishing to an integrated software

 

When the Expense Claim is complete, and each transaction in the claim has been assigned a nominal code, you can publish the claim by checking the box next to the claims you wish to publish, then pressing the green **&#x27;Publish&#x27;** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665719/d2f670de50aa239cdb830578e5ee/45958_09a1b567afd14080f46b15b86186249a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=1bf32e5e0a4621febbeb4ca1ed38022a550bf51ef034307f9f30b81b3de35d6f&req=1dxpw139rT5k2hL085ZhofHX28yqscaoH6VxxiHpR1VTT2ola5gWEW0cjBYO%0AuO00Q%2FrmkLzohhfb%0A) 

This will be published to general ledger as a purchase invoice (as if the employee was billing the company for their expenses). Each entry within the claim will be listed as a separate line item.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665742/55e95a4f5052f98e9e6d86e90abc/45958_944a7479f30b8729ef0921a12b1f896c.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=210cc287e751513ab0f743616268b32ef17bd5cb04a0b64824a1a4356396a1d1&req=1dxpw139qDVk2hL085ZhoXMXRVCJvcSIRYQfJtk0J9ERpd5xAPq%2BTls%2BbZRX%0AHi9hEHKgfYHS7rk%2B%0A) 

 

# Export as a PDF or CSV

 

- When the Expense Claim is complete, at the top of the Expense Claim&#x27;s page go to the **Export** button, then click **Export**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665753/078850a8fc34280d96b514faa9f5/45958_c7cb478ba73d39e1cc110d464d44944d.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=fc73eb38e9e7cf371abad8591625f57b6aeeff3dcef56947fcc88d32a5425f93&req=1dxpw139qTRk2hL085ZhoYjUuxGTc4L5iXR%2BgfBEEaj%2BfsCIEPgp3S%2BBh7Z1%0A8ql%2BobY1a%2F9%2BLkZr%0A) 

- To export a CSV, click the **CSV** tab and select the detail level and CSV format.**To export a PDF, click the PDF** tab and select the level of detail you wish to include in the file; just the expense claim summary or including receipts. You can also choose to archive the items once the claim is exported by ticking the box.

 

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665763/1b3c160869e5c1d700359f5203aa/45958_2eaa95a61e75e11020a8bf938f2803c1.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=8558e7520a0d7ae02a3a964bc3c95ba95dabb1b3af1d85cc5817ecd36e54a326&req=1dxpw139qjRk2hL085ZhoWe97Lq2Gf9I94zR0776sfu%2BPcXPuWXY58ssbkNq%0Aob1NkKHMgux3RzCJ%0A) 

 

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665772/c8df9156e696283157ee873abf51/45958_88de3d2085c9748efa4753ca474200f4.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=e9a33f61d2f9096c6ae7d1caaf03a63ac478a9c434bcc7d705c6938ccc0e0602&req=1dxpw139qzVk2hL085ZhoVnu2WZXOMmv8nKFw9hhFtKil5pdM7jx5TapLqQ%2B%0A2v8pGARSk8UAZHHa%0A) 

 

- When ready, click **Export**.

 

Clicking **‘Export to CSV’ **will create a more detailed claim that also displays the sub-totals of each category.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665781/db673fa47f00fe8382c576b15f42/45958_d64e1424ad24d3ae96f05f91ac16a7bf.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=c8c362a82783848cd399ac03419b69b1cd739e1b23466916b62c85922bb4074e&req=1dxpw139pDZk2hL085ZhodpCZgoOLY1uMd55%2B9FfymzVagobe47fYlRFZmOn%0AB8CkWhm1f4jzS6DP%0A) 

 

**Note**: You can now export an Expense Claim with the source images if Image Sharing has been turned on. You can also change the order your items are displayed from within the System Settings.

 

 

# Sharing via Email

 

You can share an Expense Claim via email at any time. This will send a CSV and PDF of the claim to an email address that you specify. Links in these emails will expire after 30 days.

 

To share via email:

 

- When the Expense Claim is complete, at the top of the Expense Claim&#x27;s page go to the **Export** button, then click **Send by email**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665789/310a57b91f9eb558d93c12a2a911/45958_9a701f60ace1906bdff177ccbb01bde0.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=86c80c12f27fdaafe0855ab0de8d088ad8026c6abd7a06b4cce980dbc12906e4&req=1dxpw139pD5k2hL085ZhofP%2Fm1gAGttuMO4%2Fq%2F1EQJo%2BID7eGSgjy4o5TzxF%0AaFbEQHZNSXmQW3L9%0A) 

- Enter the email details and select the export options, and when ready click **Send**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59665798/3965a801d90189514e29720bf61e/45958_a530459ebb067b9d47f5aba1c22a4369.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=c7ae109cfee14bec12da68ebe94e35476f5d4f7037d7fe2b8518cf70861f6b3b&req=1dxpw139pT9k2hL085ZhoX%2FSNG6JtrT6mdElNMl5rqlUEQYvNKqz5A23DaUi%0AKxqKxtaZK2DanAhw%0A) 

 

# Sharing via Image Sharing

 

Use the &#x27;Image Sharing&#x27; setting to give permission for other people to view images of your receipts, bills, invoices and other financial paperwork, even if they don&#x27;t have login access to your Dext Prepare account.

 

This will create a link inside your Expense Claims (and CSV downloads of your Expense Claims) where you can view the original image that was captured of the document.

 

To turn on Image Sharing:

 

- Go to **Business Settings**, then **Exports**.

- Then go to **Image Sharing** and turn image sharing **ON**.
