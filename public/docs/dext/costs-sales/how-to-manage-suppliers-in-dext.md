Dext automatically detects supplier names when you submit Costs receipts, invoices, or financial documents. But sometimes the supplier may:

- Not yet exist in your Dext account

- Have a slightly different name

- Appear under both a trading name and a legal name

In these cases, you can easily **create**, **merge**, or **manage aliases** to keep your supplier list clean and consistent.

 

# **Create a new supplier**

If the correct supplier isn’t listed, you can create it directly from the **Item Details** page.

 

## **To add a new supplier:**

- Open the **Item Details** **page**

- Click the **Supplier** field and search for the supplier

- If it doesn’t appear, click **+Add "[supplier name]"** to create it

### **If you&#x27;re connected to accounting software:**

New suppliers created in Dext will also be created in your accounting software when the document is published.

 

**To get a warning before this happens**:

- Go to **Business Settings > Connections**

- Click **Manage** next to your accounting software

- Select **Settings**

- Turn on the toggle for **Warn me when creating a new supplier** under the **General** section

# **Merge duplicate suppliers**

If a supplier was incorrectly extracted or published, you can merge duplicates to clean up your list.

 

## **To merge two or more suppliers:**

- Go to **Costs > Suppliers**

- Use search or click **Supplier duplicates**

- Select the checkboxes for the suppliers you want to merge

- Click **Merge** at the top of the page

- Choose the correct supplier name to keep, and click **Merge**

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/78385128/6e3e1775f43ba1bb0c1cb01efefb/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=d74b9b005cb18fe11c8b293f98363101fc37867cd6672213ffee0a848a23221d&req=191szV37rj9k2hL085ZhocPAnxHdQ96gFBgharxMoxFy0kURKHpF8QniVU%2B1%0AjS7mlMWWY7khsBgo%0A)**Important:** Merging suppliers **cannot be undone. **If you’re **connected to accounting software**, make sure you **merge the suppliers in it as well.**

 

# **Delete a supplier**

You can delete suppliers **that only exist in Dext.**

 

### **To delete a supplier:**

- Go to **Costs > Suppliers**

- Tick the checkbox next to the supplier name

- Click **Delete** at the top of the page

If the supplier also exists in your **accounting software**, **delete it there first** - otherwise it will **re-sync into Dext**.

**Important**: Once deleted, the supplier’s name will be removed from associated items, leaving the **Supplier field empty**.

 

# **Use supplier aliases**

Over time, you might see variations of the same supplier (like “Acme Ltd” vs “Acme Inc”). Use **supplier aliases** to group them under one correct supplier.

 

When a document matches an alias, Dext automatically assigns it to the main supplier.

 

## **Adding an alias**

**From the Item Details page****If we detect that the item’s supplier name is a potential alias of an existing supplier, we’ll prompt you to confirm it under the Supplier** field. 

 

If you click **Yes, they are the same**, you can add the supplier name as an alias, and we&#x27;ll start assigning new documents from it to the main supplier. 

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/78386479/69fa775674dec0dbe116ff0a3daf/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=1d4b8b3b94c1a01acf81958ca904b87cf41f6915d19bc3b67c7ef5d045ff1dfa&req=191szV7%2Bqz5k2hL085ZhoUM4pX61SryONxVGL5Wm5L0zwshOV7LzsMuxwWU9%0AEIKCTguh1P5iGlE5%0A) 

**From the Suppliers list**

- Go to **Costs > Suppliers**

- Click on the supplier name

- Scroll to the **Aliases** section

- Enter the alias name and click **Add**

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/78388134/45050fe829e5eefefc0ae0779a5b/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=c524082feda2bb12e204c50b9ce0c2a7cbeef396fb44f1bd23d680f8577ba5ae&req=191szVD7rzNk2hL085ZhoX0oj66ijeIbiXLHhhB%2BKK4gtI7zi32qsgFuDUJw%0AUgXNMJuCAMiY4aDj%0A) 

## **Removing an alias**

To remove an alias, go to **Costs** > **Suppliers** > click on the supplier name > go to the **Aliases** section > click the red ❌ next to it. 

 

When removing, you’ll have the option to turn the alias into a new supplier if needed.

 

[](https://downloads.intercomcdn.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/78388973/9d55038ab7cf8090d3eedafa83d8/image.png?expires=1765161000&signature=05378dc350487efcdf0d990d727621aba9eddb930083c357c03a602fecd32828&req=191szVDzqzRk2hL085Zhodz7WLEEWPJY5agshmCCnLw3q8QdJGyeq5o%2F9luG%0AFph5ejgtYchAw7MA%0A)**Note: **When you remove an alias, your processed items will remain under the original supplier name. If you create a new supplier from the alias, it won’t be assigned to past items, but Dext will assign it to new submissions.
