# Connecting your Dext and Twinfield accounts will enable you to send your Dext items directly to your accounting software with all the relevant item details applied.

 

 

This article is relevant to the enhanced Dext experience. You can access the article for the legacy Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-integrate-with-twinfield).

 

 

Important: To be able to connect with an accounting software you must be assigned the Admin [role](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions). If you don&#x27;t have this access, reach out to your account administrator or accountant/bookkeeper to request access.

 

- [Setting up the Connection](#setting-up-connection)

- [Publishing](#Publishing)

- [How to Reconnect](#reconnect)

- [How to Refresh Lists](#refresh-lists)

 

 

# Setting up the Connection

 

To connect your Dext account to your Twinfield account:

 

- Navigate to **Business Settings** in the sidebar, then **Connections**.

 

- Under Accounting software, click the **Connect** button.

 

[*](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668227/79ecf4ea739fdea5ea1e63af7372/51257_2eb95af494cc3e112e97556e6588213a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=0351e9074f83590db380a5626392c2fcaa4c74b8b6e2c7b6be962b7b1e9d00c3&req=1dxpw1D4rjBk2hL085ZhoV9B5sqACzjq2XiKAumsAll4R5YARI69vTc9VYiV%0A9oTDq%2B1p3XfbDlfE%0A) 

- On the list of available integrations, find Twinfield, then click the **Connect** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668235/cb0eded5d6158eaf7a1a4c8252c3/51257_e2cb097990c040385ccfddd78991a4cd.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=a4a9ce6e37a0ad1d0369494c364494a8ae38b656d0882b8b24a5bdc73c86403c&req=1dxpw1D4rzJk2hL085ZhoZkdkS86SeXDIpee3spr1I0TOdlTTP7S96vpiHFx%0AHrjO1CbwVlyR3waQ%0A) 

- Click the **Connect Software** button - you&#x27;ll then be prompted to log in to your account.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668243/d739f435d8ce271453d6b774d156/51257_e77316563234a33feb9fffc2f6c8b047.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=ccedd6d78065815a93fa250cbb609dc6861a18d5a96600d9ea1885809a290ef9&req=1dxpw1D4qDRk2hL085ZhoXlxvjFgbrgt9dTGQfXx32mN0e%2FwMqYzrQ9%2BDPL%2B%0A4cg1yVBh2%2Fwmc8tm%0A) 

- Follow the steps provided until you return to Dext.

- Finally, you will see a confirmation that the connection has successfully been made. Click **Finish**.

 

After integrating, please allow a few minutes for all the category codes and lists to synchronise.

 

 

# Publishing

 

Before you publish any items, ensure you assign each item a category and configure your tax settings correctly. This will save you time and reduce the risk of errors when you publish to Twinfield.

 

On the Twinfield connection page, in the **Link Expense Types for Publishing **section, select where each expense type should be published in Twinfield.

 

 

Note - If the wrong expense type has been selected then the error "Incorrect XML error - &#x27;the code has not been filled/this transaction type does not exist for this company" *will appear. To correct the error, return to the Integrations page and select the correct option for that expense type.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668251/9bfc16e0e65c29bef217d35bee27/51257_2127eeefae970a1689885df19bbed81b.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=6b2ff9ee2a1c5f3cad9c5a7421db64a31ff7e60bbe8081e3b1458a19a90db2b0&req=1dxpw1D4qTZk2hL085Zhodh75llkQ9On%2BlXbsLod%2BEp%2FYYe9IEPKQEhH4A9a%0A872vZ5SKqxAl9Rhz%0A) 

In the **Link Expense types to Tax Rates **section, confirm which tax rate should be applied for each expense type.

 

Once you&#x27;ve selected a category and set the correct tax settings you are ready to publish your item.

 

See [how to publish here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/editing-publishing-and-exporting-items).

 

 

**To Send Images To Twinfield When Publishing **

 

Sending images to Twinfield when publishing allows the user to see the specific item that was uploaded to Dext within Twinfield.

 

To enable image sharing complete the following steps:

 

**In Twinfield:**

 

- Within the ‘General’ tab select ‘Transaction Types’ option (located under the ‘Company’ menu)

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668261/5c28be5c6ad98411e18d6257dea2/51257_4429fa4cf30c86b15f6fb36694302bf9.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=4f04b5a9a85dc7d5c25e3d45a20fa71830e87e3ce24704293490503ccc9b1e73&req=1dxpw1D4qjZk2hL085ZhoeqP1CGa%2BAbi1z9PhPuxsQDGSFQLv2ghJd8XH03P%0AJHpIu0mgQhkvxYow%0A) 

- Select ‘Purchase Invoice’ and hit ‘Next’

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668269/083528e51cbaa5ed872ddcd6b6ca/51257_245a50ded5a9abeac39ad8bed42b56d7.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=fd152de4ff174f33de5d8481a6541ddb18f5eeacbc3094540a602556924a8ebc&req=1dxpw1D4qj5k2hL085ZhoZNt0jrh7igmBBrh6xVqB61oBI8nGbUYE9vrMQ3c%0AwsxjL3kqJNVmgrxR%0A) 

- Under ‘Free Text Fields’ section for ‘Free text field 3’ select ‘Allow’

- Under Document imaging section fill the name field (e.g. ‘Transaction Images’, ‘Scan’, etc.)

- Then for ‘Link’ enter ‘http://$FreeText3$’ and save.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668279/0876431c88b5c9e1ad725eb2092c/51257_cb5ecdf29105f3cc9b908cd08ef9ae45.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=92cd8c1883dced75c5f595467c92dcfdb4f9a6a8edc433146cefc3324ed56a97&req=1dxpw1D4qz5k2hL085ZhoaMwyuN%2B%2B4B8Ok4XjQ0rdl8mdVJx3iyU4cknllHF%0A5zie%2F2idFnmdNTup%0A) 

**In Dext Prepare:**

 

- Navigate to the **Connections **section and then click on the **Integrations **tab.

- Select the relevant free text field from the &#x27;Image field in Twinfield&#x27; dropdown.

# How to Reconnect

 

Reconnecting will re-establish the connection with Twinfield. You may need to reconnect because your login credentials have changed or your connection has expired.

 

If you would simply like to refresh your chart of accounts, see how to refresh the connection section below.

 

To re-connect:

 

To reconnect:

 

- Navigate to **Business Settings** in the sidebar, then **Connections**.

 

- In the Accounting software section, next to Twinfield click the **Manage** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668303/84b2c612b02add232b94f42c1e71/51257_3d60b23deb7bcbf6a9f601a1363d3105.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=669a4faf989875033e3c634e62026dbab3cd3b7536ea2a95dc3915eed98132fb&req=1dxpw1D5rDRk2hL085ZhobFsNZ5P6Ok40zD7a%2FhDe7aJ8%2B6GckDRuJQbEOil%0At7QghR0vZjUbE2xQ%0A) 

- Click the **Reconnect** button - you&#x27;ll be redirected to Twinfield and prompted to log in to your account.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668312/ea15d9a7e7141a0b53b963ead6e2/51257_7f6a92ce45cabc813bf6891b221bb979.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=994b96b1a572c29098f6526d9c38f2034d77eb53f263267679c6761e8f0826ec&req=1dxpw1D5rTVk2hL085Zhof%2B2k5NC7PhzhMqve4f5FCv5ulMJRdPsYuKpwPMz%0AbYDJm5qbhR6YQAXN%0A) 

- Follow the steps provided until you return to Dext.

 

- Finally, you will see a confirmation that your organisation is connected. Click **Finish**.

 

 

# How to Refresh Lists

 

Refreshing your connection will only refresh the charts of accounts (all the lists pulled from the integration).

 

 

Please note: No data will be lost when the connection is refreshed.

 

To refresh lists:

 

- Navigate to **Business Settings** in the sidebar, then **Connections**.

 

- In the Accounting software section, next to Twinfield click the **Manage** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668319/93dab18f11f895c12ee55b278144/51257_3d60b23deb7bcbf6a9f601a1363d3105.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=cdbbf258c1dd7b8a80a2065eb529ae01452a329d0b8e2cce6c7d2da1dc9c6db9&req=1dxpw1D5rT5k2hL085ZhoVOlDoGJzshUspeKIwEO7WXJTXIt%2F%2BlYWuhv0Bs2%0AjrAJMjz59XnTkg5g%0A) 

- On the next page, click the **Manage** dropdown, then **Refresh integration data**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59668330/785f022afab0f2964702044d024a/51257_933275b9a678c29aa13b6087a4a31849.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=ce3f73e1c829b60ac1e92645bda2c44cd66622d9e25a755acfb2abb17cd42f86&req=1dxpw1D5rzdk2hL085ZhobHkr5PV5X47DEsCBWbDsqRjvzfGA795ZEKX0ISx%0ACD1sbykA9jmAaUAi%0A) 

- Finally, you will see a green banner confirming the refresh is in progress. The process should only take a couple minutes.
