# Connections between Dext and an accounting software expire once a year. You&#x27;ll receive a publishing error when this happens, and will have to complete a short re-connection process to continue publishing items.

 

 

This article is relevant to the enhanced Dext experience. You can access the article for the legacy Dext experience [here](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-re-integrate-after-an-integration-expires).

 

 

Important for businesses: To be able to integrate with an accounting software you must be assigned the Admin [role](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/roles-permissions). Reach out to your account administrator or accountant/bookkeeper to request access.

 

- [How to Reconnect](#reconnect)

- [How to Change the Connection](#changing-connection)

 

 

# How to Reconnect

 

**Good to know**: the settings and automation rules you have already set up will remain unchanged.

 

To re-connect:

 

- Go to **Business Settings** in the sidebar, then **Connections**.

 

- On the Connections page, click **Manage** next to your accounting software and in the drop down list click **Reconnect** .

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667798/e22c7ecf4a7e9a333e9fdd769d08/48652_4626700cba55f107861051193587843a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=e0649654ecdc158394d236923c41afb9c3f0ba1352bc6d647a5f25fa1f0f06e9&req=1dxpw1%2F9pT9k2hL085Zhoe0ilFjSx18quzcQomgtDAFm4v1thotTuo86UNnP%0A1%2FPfJp7mrE2Q3vAe%0A) 

- You&#x27;ll be redirected to your accounting software and prompted to log in to your account. Once logged in, follow the steps provided until you return to Dext.

 

- You will then be taken back to Dext Prepare. From there, choose an organisation to give Dext access to, and click **Next**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667807/535d0ceaf3fa0decfd21ad0b443f/48652_ce8d20569d12194b56f88b0b1daf0278.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=5c07f286e527bdabbbe36beb8919e0178248a92359949e04ba3666ebc04b0e73&req=1dxpw1%2FyrDBk2hL085ZhoZkxTft7SA33xyU6o0vMOIoKFHqHISruMHAsPyfN%0Am%2F3r5R0LmbgTo5oP%0A) 

- Finally, you will see a confirmation that your organisation is connected. Click **Finish**.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667819/46e9b488fd0fb32c164e5db2a6b6/48652_199583367326d225538c0518788cabfd.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=97b9d065f1276aaf0cb452781ce89f1f4d9a74262d690551c69f89e75f4f17d7&req=1dxpw1%2FyrT5k2hL085ZhoYpzEhObfI91c6jEbCI6vvQjqzP5QL5kGfgSAGwh%0ArL6APNE%2FuFmri5yK%0A) 

 

# Changing the Connection

 

 

**Good to know:** in order to not lose your previous settings, such as supplier rules, chart of accounts, etc. After changing the connexion, you may use [the List merge Tool](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-the-list-merge-tool) to merge your old List items with list items from your new integration.

 

To change the connection from one integration to another:

 

- Go to **Business Settings** in the sidebar, then **Connections**.

 

- On the Connections page, click **Manage** next to your accounting software and in the drop down list click **Change connection** .

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667831/edf19961cf21db87ddda61fd8346/48652_4626700cba55f107861051193587843a.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=c5008c3c230006619d05179ab592880b5e20e77764a31982e66831731649ca09&req=1dxpw1%2FyrzZk2hL085ZhoWL0XiCHnob0Q5%2FjPcSgG0RDytHODaoLGvlmILZS%0AsHWWK1wkYBN4buXj%0A) 

- Next select the accounting software you want to connect with, then click the green **Connect** button.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667840/d8f565b7ec1e088118d48cef3373/48652_40f1eca78f322b31e9083a219b8c2782.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=cb43680677cf4008ed4c5f8ecac9bb72a189ebbf11502e808f8a76092ff682b3&req=1dxpw1%2FyqDdk2hL085ZhoUGDIqkBOmQ8QaQ8IyK%2BIZcvt9pDCCIy8VC6gSQG%0AR%2BEhYCeJW4owrqw4%0A) 

- Click the **Connect software** button - you&#x27;ll be redirected to your accounting software and prompted to log in to your account.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667854/fbedaca7682dabda88409478d1c2/48652_8c127229ab6e08ac9adf9fbe38acc574.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=86a08be65052bcac8e119bfd965b4f7772db03e8ce947b41fb5bcd0c73f2e188&req=1dxpw1%2FyqTNk2hL085ZhoV7g4jVALk%2B9E9p1RhztGTZLHgY4cE9WqwfJz2EH%0AF%2BU8egnJeFBMgGDi%0A) 

5. Follow the steps provided until you return to Dext.

 

6. Finally, you will see a confirmation that your organisation is connected. Click **Finish**.

 

If you wish to merge your previous rules setting, use the **List Merge Tool** to match your old lists with the new ones. It allows you to preserve any rules that have been set up for suppliers, customers, or the bank, which will work with your new list items. [Read the guide to know more](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/using-the-list-merge-tool).
