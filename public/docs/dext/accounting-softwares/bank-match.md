**Bank Match automatically identifies Dext items that have a matching transaction in your accounting software Bank Feed or cash coded payments.**

**Note: **Bank Match does not currently work with Sage Pastel / Sage South Africa.

- **[How Bank Match Works](#how)**

- **[Bank Match & Xero](#bank)**

- **[Bank Match & Sage Accounting](#sage)**

 

 

# How Bank Match Works

 

Bank Match identifies an item in your costs inbox with a matching, unreconciled line in your bank transactions within Dext, or in the bank feed from your integrated accounting software.

 

In the inbox, hover your mouse over the **Match** icon in the Match column to review information about your matching transaction.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667738/f7e57e0833555d4418d467d5ae7f/48179_513fd7310bc06b7222d49d9fdd7471ca.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=c4eef3b28d05ef8511b24c9b04e2927996c1293ae4e62bacd7cae764a0471f4b&req=1dxpw1%2F9rz9k2hL085ZhoQ6Zzh%2Fo17vH8GVusxUg3KA5A3ofvHYtHIoG1pOw%0AqEZzy0uoOSNuhIEK%0A) 

 

Bank Match identifies transactions that have been made within the last 6 months of submissions, so any older transactions will still need to be manually published.

 

**Matching items are determined by:**

 

- Matching Total Amount

- Items paid within 30 days of the document date for documents without a due date

- Items paid within 10 days of the due date for documents that have a due date set

You can then select those items with the Bank Match icon and export them or publish them directly to your accounting software such as Xero or Sage (see below for integration specific instructions).

 

 

# Bank Match & Xero

 

To use Bank Match with Xero:

 

- Go to the **Costs** Inbox and find the item with the **Bank Match** icon and click on it.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667750/c3ff8a2aa618ae0befbefdd1ac06/48179_cc1c2eacb20bde20d1639cae6c6b9685.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=6f2d90b6ac1141f6f4f75e991a8883c7a96efa14dd5fa2de99d1784edfd53220&req=1dxpw1%2F9qTdk2hL085ZhoZeN6DWEL02OgaUVGNHSD4meAjh1rzn%2BkFqz7dQZ%0AFo3%2BulJeUex%2BmZsB%0A) 

- On the **Item Details** page, check the item&#x27;s category, then go to the **Payment** section and click **Autofill Payment** to complete the Payment details for this item. This data is pulled through from Xero.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667759/6f5e93b5dd52417a2fd93b987b18/48179_6fd52e9d11cc0a6736f7830b810f3345.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=ad5ed9914b22e7a8dbaf8d47add5b380188d62b3ce647c6512f43a944adf89f2&req=1dxpw1%2F9qT5k2hL085ZhoUxrDpWnVr1XHmG2J6XtiAee2iT2RSm3y8yTB5tm%0AvsnQowORnHTmoFG8%0A) 

- When ready **Publish** your item.

- You can then find the item on the **Reconcile** page in Xero.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667773/fba9a35eeab01cc3cd4f9338d9e9/48179_43276e7dcbb4ecedf66c1e7789b804dc.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=728a5e1e01199c7831aa265745254024376800b99a7f5c865090c094be47a1f2&req=1dxpw1%2F9qzRk2hL085ZhoUjlzkXOO%2FPms6Rm%2FgyA67qjRTjsfMQVMTfgH2Ww%0A1pp%2FU83zglM7k9BO%0A) 

 

# Bank Match & Sage Accounting

 

- Go to the item with the **Bank Match** icon and click on it.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667782/5b65e39500ac015b1066e0f9911e/48179_f8476ec7052996614aab616de2cd9138.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=71b0ddcd92ade5ca3c4f83faa98f45a017807089459d3e7b6af01bd20000fb33&req=1dxpw1%2F9pDVk2hL085ZhoRVELYrjKltlRzDrdS6CSrjHc6F1JoSp4qTdNrhA%0A52q3YF5a8Ox18Llf%0A) 

- On the **Item Details**, as the data already matches the Sage Bank Feed, you just need to review the Category field and any other tracking information.

- Next go to the **Payment** section, click **Autofill Payment** to complete the Payment details for this item. This prompts you to autofill the Payment Details so that the data exactly matches the Sage Bank Feed.

 

[](https://dext.intercom-attachments.eu/i/o/weauzzxo/59667790/d535ad0f5c99dd759f77d27020c0/48179_4f0fa3466f2466bc252352e26386cd1b.jpg?expires=1765161000&signature=58368fe85a397a70bd819941dba7e4cd1e227650fb97f3c72c450db6aadfd8f0&req=1dxpw1%2F9pTdk2hL085ZhoasRXMNBy2MgDBCAqfPZnOgN02Udd34GiyMAvW1X%0A2KMGSvfF47y5%2FbLC%0A) 

- Make sure all other details are correct, then **Publish** your item.

- It will now be present in the **Banking** section of Sage, ready for reconciliation!
