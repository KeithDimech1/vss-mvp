**This error occurs as a result of the integration with Sage Accounting expiring. It can easily be resolved by reintegrating. **

 

1. Simply navigate to the **Connections** menu and open the **Integrations** tab

 

2. Click the blue **Change** button next to your Current Integration and choose **&#x27;Sage Accounting&#x27;** again.

 

3. Complete the process by following the steps as directed.
