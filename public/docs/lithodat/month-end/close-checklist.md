### Xero Reconciliation

- [ ] Schedule monthly finance meeting

## Close Verification

Before marking month as closed:

- [ ] All bank accounts reconciled
- [ ] All invoices processed
- [ ] All bills paid or scheduled
- [ ] Dext inbox empty
- [ ] No Xero items needing attention
- [ ] Finance meeting completed

---

## Lock Period

After close is verified:

1. Go to **Settings → General Settings → Financial Settings**
2. Set **Lock Date** to last day of closed month
3. This prevents accidental changes to closed periods
