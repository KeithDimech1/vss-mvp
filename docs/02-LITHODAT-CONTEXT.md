# Lithodat Context for VSM Platform

**Purpose:** Provide essential context about Lithodat's current state, utopia visions, and strategic challenges to inform platform design decisions.

---

## 📊 Current State (October 2025)

### Company Metrics
- **Size**: 14 employees (scaling to 20-25+)
- **Revenue**: ~$1.3M annual turnover
- **Structure**: 3 Directors + development/operations team
- **Geographic Distribution**: Melbourne team + Mexico team + distributed
- **Growth Stage**: Transitioning from startup to scale-up

### Three Operational Systems (System 1)

#### 1. LithoSurfer (Front-End Platform)
- **Current State**: Core software product for geological data management
- **Status**: Operational, central to business
- **Revenue Model**: Currently project-based, moving toward subscription
- **Key Strength**: Already works, has all core functionality
- **Opportunity**: Every exploration company could use it

#### 2. LithoBuild (Custom Development)
- **Current State**: Software development consulting and custom tool creation
- **Status**: Primary revenue generator historically
- **Strategic Role**: Bootstrap capital for next 1-2 years
- **Key Challenge**: Not sustainable long-term, creates maintenance burden
- **Leadership View**: Temporary necessity, not core business

#### 3. LithoData (Back-End Data)
- **Current State**: Data collection, cleaning, and curation
- **Sub-systems**: LithoClean (cleaning), LithoMine (extraction)
- **Products**: Curated geological/geochemical datasets
- **Strategic Role**: Future core differentiation
- **Key Opportunity**: Private data aggregation at scale

#### 4. LithoAI (Future/Potential)
- **Current State**: Not yet operational
- **Status**: Planned capabilities, not producing yet
- **Potential**: Prospectivity analysis, integrated AI tools

## 🚧 Current Challenges

### System 1: Operations
- Staff work across multiple systems (e.g., Nora, Kimberly)
- Unclear task categorization by system
- Need better system boundary definition
- Resource allocation is ad-hoc

### System 2: Coordination
- Communication sometimes goes up-then-across (not horizontal)
- Staff unsure who to ask for what (e.g., Kimberly asking Wayne instead of Juan)
- Multiple Signal groups without clear structure
- Jira usage not systematic across systems

### System 3: Control/Optimization
- Limited real-time financial visibility (Xero has data, not dashboards)
- No formal prioritization framework
- Ad-hoc resource allocation and scheduling
- Quality control works for Build, less clear for Data/Surfer
- Current model worked for 3 directors, doesn't scale to 14-25 people

### System 4: Intelligence
- No dedicated market research or customer relationship function
- Ad-hoc competitive analysis
- Weak social media presence, weak website
- Customer workflow understanding is informal
- Intelligence gathering distributed, not systematic

### System 5: Policy/Identity
- Need clarity for management and staff on specific utopia for each system
- Branding decision pending (formalize LithoSurfer/Build/Data names?)
- Career paths undefined (technical vs. management tracks)
- Vision communication: "No one in our team including us clearly knows exactly where we want to go" (Keith)

## 💡 Critical Requirements for Platform

### Must Enable
1. **Diagnosis across all 5 VSM systems** with Lithodat-specific context
2. **Multi-stakeholder visioning** accommodating 5+ different perspectives
3. **Cross-functional strategy** covering Dev, Marketing, HR, Finance, Branding, Data
4. **Multiple time horizons** from 3 months to 5 years
5. **System and function views** (by Surfer/Build/Data AND by business function)
6. **Democratic prioritization** with leadership approval
7. **Execution tracking** integrated with existing Jira workflow
8. **Continuous evolution** as company scales from 14 to 25+ people

### Must Avoid
1. **Bureaucracy** - structure without unnecessary overhead
2. **Micromanagement** - enable autonomy while maintaining visibility
3. **Tool fragmentation** - integrate with existing tools, don't add chaos
4. **Over-complexity** - accessible to all team members
5. **Analysis paralysis** - bias toward action and decision-making

## 🎯 Platform Design Implications

### Must Be
- **Fast to value**: Can't wait 6 months, need results in weeks
- **Collaborative**: 5+ stakeholders with different views must align
- **Visual**: Complex systems need visual representation
- **Flexible**: Will evolve as company grows and learns
- **Integrated**: Works with existing tools and workflows
- **AI-enhanced**: Leverage Lithodat's AI expertise and existing Claude access

### Should Consider
- **Product potential**: Could this become a Lithodat offering?
- **Competitive advantage**: Does it give Lithodat unique capability?
- **Team learning**: Build internal expertise in strategic planning
- **Recursive application**: Can be applied to each operational unit
