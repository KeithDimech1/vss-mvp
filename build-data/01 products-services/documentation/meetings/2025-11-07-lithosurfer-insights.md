# Lithosurfer Insights Meeting

**Date:** November 7, 2025
**Duration:** 2 hours
**Attendees:** Keith Dimech, Fabian Kohlmann, Wayne Noble, Vinko Novak, Moritz Theile

---

## Meeting Purpose

Strategic alignment session to review company vision, current state, and future direction for the Lithodat platform and marketplace.

---

## Summary

Moritz Theile provided a historical overview of the company's vision ("international leading marketplace to buy and sell geological sample-based data") and discussed financial bootstrapping through projects like Earthbank. Key tensions emerged between focusing exclusively on the marketplace versus taking strategic opportunities to maintain viability. Critical concerns were raised about organizational structure, architectural governance, and team burnout.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Historical Context & Vision
- Company vision crystallized since 2019: "International leading marketplace to buy and sell geological sample-based data"
- Earthbank project served as bootstrapping mechanism ("double dipping" - matching project requirements with platform vision)
- Additional funding from NR Canada and CSIRO while maintaining focus on original vision

### 2. Current Platform Status
- Strong team, expertise, branding, data models, and platform in place
- Marketplace not yet operational - all work to date has been preparation
- Ready to execute on the original vision

### 3. Strategic Direction Disagreement
- **Moritz's View:** Focus on completing marketplace; use existing business to finance it
- **Wayne/Fabian's View:** Must take strategic opportunities to remain viable; all current building supports marketplace goal
- **Consensus:** Leveraging data flow through "oil rigs for the data" is the right approach

### 4. Energy & Burnout Concerns
- Moritz expressed being "really tired" with only 10% energy remaining
- Opposition to growth into new businesses (AI consulting, project development)
- Concern about overestimating company's current capabilities
- May need to step back from director role

### 5. Architectural Governance
- Critical need for architectural governance to protect Lithosurfer platform
- Risk of platform ruin through overloading or bad design decisions
- Must be in capable hands as company grows

### 6. Content & Data Strategy
- Data is crucial for marketplace success - empty marketplace won't work
- Data team must keep building content independently
- Academic data has limited commercial value
- Strategy: attract private exploration data by showcasing analysis capabilities

### 7. Product Monetization
- Current product could attract 15 exploration companies by February with minor UX adjustments
- Initial focus on small exploration companies as trials before targeting majors
- Securing 5-6 initial customers opens doors to larger companies (e.g., Glencore)

### 8. Organizational Structure
- Company lacks structure, causing founders to be overworked
- Need to implement structure and define roles/responsibilities
- Essential for managing growth and allowing focus on core tasks

---

## Decisions Made

1. Agreed that leveraging existing tools to finance path to marketplace is correct approach
2. Moritz can commit 50-80% time to cash flow projects, remainder to marketplace
3. Directors to choose strategic stream, then map feasibility and pathway to marketplace
4. Wayne and Fabian to complete Utopia quiz for VSS platform
