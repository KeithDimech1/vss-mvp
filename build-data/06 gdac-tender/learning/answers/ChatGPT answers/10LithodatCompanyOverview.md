## 1. Brief Company History

Lithodat Pty Ltd was founded in 2018 in Melbourne, Australia, with the vision of building the world’s most advanced integrated platform for geochemistry, geochronology, thermochronology, isotopes and geological data. Beginning as a small specialised team with deep expertise in geology, data science and cloud architecture, Lithodat has grown into a global provider of scientific data platforms used by government agencies, research institutions and industry.

Key milestones include:  
- **2019–2020:** Development of the LithoSurfer data platform and early adoption by Australian research institutions.  
- **2021:** Official launch of AusGeochem (now EarthBank). 
- **2022:** First Canadian tender sucess to help build a national thermochronology database. 
- **2024:** Launch of multi-agency national platforms including the **Stable Isotope Repository (Isotopes-AU)**.  
- **2025:** Launch of first global public data platfrom for standardised exterrestrial geochemistry data LithoSpace

Today, Lithodat delivers advanced geoscience data infrastructures on a global scale, powered by a mature technology stack, a highly experienced scientific team and a strong track record of government partnerships.

## 3. Core Competencies and Services

Lithodat specialises in:

- **National-scale scientific data platforms** (geochemistry, geochron, thermochron, isotopes)  
- **AI/ML-ready data standardisation**, harmonisation and ingestion  
- **Advanced geoscience modelling**, including deep-time tectonics and thermal history  
- **Cloud-native systems on AWS**, with ISO-aligned security and governance  
- **Automated QA/QC**, provenance tracking and FAIR-compliant metadata systems  
- **Custom analytics dashboards and spatial data services**  
- **Scientific data architecture, ontology and data model design**

All platforms are built on **LithoSurfer**, Lithodat’s modular, extensible software environment enabling rapid deployment, scalability and low-risk customisation for new projects.
