# Earth Composition & Evolution (ECE) Program
**URL:** https://www.auscope.org.au/ece
**Captured:** 2025-11-30

---

## Program Purpose

The ECE program supports research into Australian continental evolution, informing mineral exploration, climate studies, and natural hazard assessments. It provides "world-class laboratory facilities, instruments, and data systems" enabling researchers to access advanced geochemistry, geochronology, and thermochronology tools across Australia.

---

## Key Objectives

1. Expand access to advanced analytical tools through coordinated laboratory networks
2. Partner with Geoscience Australia and state surveys to produce consistent, national-scale geochemical datasets
3. Develop eResearch tools and trusted repositories following FAIR Data Principles
4. Build EarthBank with the AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN)

---

## Research Facilities

ECE co-funds instrumentation at four major institutions:

| Institution | Facility | Capabilities |
|-------------|----------|--------------|
| **Curtin University** | John de Laeter Centre | Mass spectrometers and micro-analysis systems |
| **University of Western Australia** | CMCA | Cameca IMS 1280 ion microprobe |
| **University of Melbourne** | Geohistory Laboratory | Thermochronology instrumentation |
| **Australian National University** | SHRIMP Centre | SHRIMP and TerraneChron® capability |

---

## EarthBank Platform

A national platform for visualizing, analyzing, and extracting georeferenced geochemical data, developed collaboratively with AGN partners.
