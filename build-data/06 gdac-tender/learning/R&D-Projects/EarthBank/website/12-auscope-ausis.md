# Australian Seismometers In Schools (AuSIS) Program
**URL:** https://www.auscope.org.au/ausis
**Captured:** 2025-11-30

---

## Overview

AuSIS places earthquake-measuring seismometers in over 50 Australian schools to advance geoscience education and collect research data. The program combines community engagement with scientific infrastructure.

---

## Core Mission

The initiative aims to:
- "Raise community awareness of regional earthquakes"
- Promote geoscience as a career path
- Provide teaching tools for physics and earth science instruction

---

## Educational Infrastructure

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Schools equipped | **50+ nationwide** |
| Data access | Live earthquake recordings at ausis.edu.au |
| Research data | Available through AusPASS database |
| Engagement | Interactive workshops and school visits |

---

## Key Activities

### Science Quake of the Year (2024)
Schools participate by jumping to create ground vibrations recorded by seismometers during National Science Week.

### Student Engagement Programs
Programs like Girls into Earth and Marine Sciences (GEMS) use immersive technologies—including 3D goggles—to introduce students to seismology fieldwork.

---

## Research Impact

Data supports studies by:
- Geoscience Australia
- Seismological Research Centre
- Geological Survey of South Australia

### Published Research Topics
- Crustal structure studies
- Seismic velocity analysis
- Archaean crust formation in Western Australia
