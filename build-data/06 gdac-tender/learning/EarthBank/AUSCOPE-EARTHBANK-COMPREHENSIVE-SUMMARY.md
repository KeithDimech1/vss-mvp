# AuScope & EarthBank: Comprehensive Summary
**Document Type:** Research Summary
**Compiled:** 2025-11-30
**Purpose:** Understanding AuScope's history, funding, programs, and the EarthBank/Lithodat relationship

---

## Executive Summary

AuScope is Australia's national provider of research infrastructure for the geoscience community, established in 2006 through the National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS). The organization has received over **$100 million in cumulative funding** to build integrated geoscience infrastructure across Australia. EarthBank (formerly AusGeochem) is a key platform developed by AuScope in partnership with Lithodat Pty Ltd, representing a major investment in FAIR geochemistry data infrastructure.

## Part 1: AuScope Organization Overview

### 1.1 Mission & Purpose

**Mission Statement:** "To enable world-class Earth science research that benefits all Australians."

**Core Function:** AuScope provides shared, open-access data and infrastructure enabling researchers to:
- Observe and measure Earth's processes in real-time
- Analyze samples to understand Earth's composition and history
- Model and simulate systems to predict change

**National Priorities Supported:**
- Climate change research
- Resource security (critical minerals)
- Groundwater management
- Natural hazard resilience

## Part 3: The Eight Programs

AuScope operates eight major programs providing instruments, services, data, and analytics:

### 3.1 Earth Composition & Evolution (ECE)

**Purpose:** Support research into Australian continental evolution for mineral exploration, climate studies, and hazard assessment

**Leader:** Professor Brent McInnes (Curtin University)

**Key Facilities:**
- Curtin University - John de Laeter Centre
- University of Western Australia - CMCA (Cameca IMS 1280 ion microprobe)
- University of Melbourne - Geohistory Laboratory
- Australian National University - SHRIMP and TerraneChron®

**Key Output:** EarthBank platform

**Impact:** Supports 800+ researchers annually, contributes to hundreds of publications

### 3.2 AuScope Virtual Research Environment (AVRE)

**Purpose:** "Australia's home of geoscience data and tools"—FAIR data ecosystem

**Leader:** Jens Klump (CSIRO)

**Active Since:** 2006

**Key Tools:**
- AuScope Portal
- AuScope Inversion Laboratory (iEarth)
- Virtual Geophysics Laboratory (VGL)
- Model Atlas of the Earth (M@TE)
- AusPass Portal

**Access:** https://avre.auscope.org.au/

### 3.3 National Virtual Core Library (NVCL)

**Purpose:** World's largest online drill core mineralogical database

**Leader:** Shane Mule (CSIRO – Mineral Resources)

**Data Volume:** Over 1.6 million meters of drill core data

**Technology:** HyLogger-3 and HyLogger-4 hyperspectral scanning systems

**Geographic Coverage:** All Australian states and territories

**Key Achievements:**
- 192,000 meters of previously inaccessible drill core scanned
- 75+ theses and 50+ peer-reviewed publications supported
- 500+ industry geologists and students trained

### 3.4 Simulation, Analysis & Modelling (SAM)

**Purpose:** Open-source software for modelling Earth's dynamic systems

**Leader:** Prof. Louis Moresi (Australian National University)

**Active Since:** 2006

**Key Software:**
- **Underworld:** High-performance geodynamic simulation
- **GPlates:** Interactive plate tectonic exploration
- **GAdopt:** Glacial isostatic adjustment modeling
- **M@TE:** Thermal and mechanical mantle evolution

### 3.5 Earth Imaging & Sounding

**Purpose:** Geophysical equipment monitoring earthquake activity and imaging the continent

**Key Capabilities:** Seismometer networks, passive seismic data collection

### 3.6 Geodesy & Geodynamics

**Purpose:** Observational instruments monitoring movement and changes in the Australian Plate

**Key Infrastructure:** Queensland Corner Reflector Array (essential to ESA and NASA for satellite calibration)

### 3.7 Australian Seismometers In Schools (AuSIS)

**Purpose:** STEM education and research data collection

**Leader:** Prof. Malcolm Sambridge (ANU)

**Active Since:** 2011

**Schools Equipped:** 50+ nationwide

**Data Access:** ausis.edu.au and AusPASS database

### 3.8 Subsurface Observatory

**Purpose:** Understanding Australian continent character for minerals, energy, and water resources

**Leader:** Dr. Dan Sandiford (University of Melbourne)

**Key Capability:** Mobile petrophysics laboratory (launched June 2022)

## Part 4: EarthBank Platform Deep Dive

### 4.1 Platform History

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **2019-2020** | AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN) formed by Curtin, Melbourne, Macquarie universities |
| **2020-2021** | Partnership established with Lithodat Pty Ltd |
| **2021** | $450,000+ funding secured for platform development |
| **October 2021** | AusGeochem platform goes live |
| **2022-2023** | AGN+ project expands to 10+ institutions |
| **April 2025** | Rebrand to "EarthBank" announced at European Geophysical Union |

### 4.2 Development Partnership

**AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN):**
- Led by Prof Brent McInnes (Curtin University)
- 28 specialists across 4 Expert Advisory Groups
- 10+ Australian university partners

**Lithodat Pty Ltd:**
- Dr Fabian Kohlmann — CEO & Managing Director
- Wayne Noble — CIO / Development Lead
- Moritz Theile — Director / Lead Developer

**Development Time:** 15+ months of collaborative effort

### 4.3 Platform Capabilities

**Data Types Supported:**
- LA-ICP-MS and SIMS U-Pb geochronology
- Major, minor, and trace element geochemistry
- K-Ar and Ar-Ar geochronology
- Fission track thermochronology
- (U-Th-Sm)/He thermochronology
- Thermal history simulations
- Hf-isotope geochemistry

**Built-in Analysis Tools:**
- Cumulative age histograms
- Age versus elevation plots
- Step-heating diagrams
- Interactive mapping

### 4.4 Platform Statistics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Users** | 1,300+ (research, policy, industry) |
| **Registered Samples** | 350,000+ |
| **Notable Collections** | Museums Victoria specimens (dating to 1854), Steve Barnes CSIRO Ni-PGE Collection (5,700+ records), ThermochronOz2002 |

### 4.5 Rebrand to EarthBank

**Reason:** International partners hesitant to use platform with Australian-specific name "AusGeochem"

**New Name Inspiration:** "Biobank" concept from biological sciences—commitment to open data and global reach

**Announcement:** European Geophysical Union meeting, April 2025

**Future Investment:** $19 million for expansion, including 10 EarthBank fellows

## Part 7: Strategic Direction (2020-2030)

### 7.1 Strategy Development Process

**Independent Review (2018):** Board-commissioned review by Prof Neil Williams, Prof Anya Reading, Prof Peter Betts, Prof Matt King

**Community Engagement:**
- Two-day Adelaide workshop (October 2018) with 100+ researchers
- Series of thematic workshops on field-based, laboratory, and digital infrastructure
- Working groups to formalize proposals

### 7.2 Strategic Priorities

1. **4D Australia** — Temporal and spatial geoscience understanding
2. **Observations** — Improved data collection across infrastructure types
3. **Data Assimilation** — Integration of research information

### 7.3 Key Infrastructure Concept: "Downward Looking Telescope"

Five integrated components:
1. **Temporal Lens** — Evolutionary history
2. **Observational Lens** — Structural changes
3. **Characterisation Lens** — Material properties
4. **Analysis Framework** — Modeling/simulation
5. **Data Framework** — Standardized, FAIR-compliant data
