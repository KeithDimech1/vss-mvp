## Description

AuScope is Australia's national provider of research infrastructure for the geoscience community, helping scientists, government, and industry understand Earth's processes from its deep interior to its surface.

## Mission & Purpose

**Mission Statement:** "To enable world-class Earth science research that benefits all Australians."

The organization supports national priorities including:
- Climate change
- Resource security
- Groundwater management
- Natural hazard resilience

## Funding

AuScope receives funding through the Australian Government's National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS). The organization recently received a **$45 million funding boost** to accelerate major geoscience infrastructure projects.

## Core Functions

AuScope provides shared, open-access data and infrastructure enabling researchers to:
- Observe and measure Earth's processes in real-time
- Analyze samples to understand Earth's composition and history
- Model and simulate systems to predict change

## Key Infrastructure: "Downward Looking Telescope"

Integrated system featuring five components:

1. **Temporal Lens** - Evolutionary history
2. **Observational Lens** - Structural changes
3. **Characterisation Lens** - Material properties
4. **Analysis Framework** - Modeling/simulation
5. **Data Framework** - Standardized, FAIR-compliant data
