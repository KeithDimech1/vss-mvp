## 10-Year Strategy (2020-2030)

AuScope developed a comprehensive strategic plan through community engagement between 2018-2020. The organization focused on addressing "national geoscience challenges in the coming decade" through structured infrastructure investment.

---

## Strategic Development Process

### Independent Review
An independent board commissioned review in 2018 examined existing programs.

| Activity | Details |
|----------|---------|

| **Thematic Workshops** | Series on field-based, laboratory, and digital infrastructure |
| **Working Groups** | Formed to formalize infrastructure proposals |

---

## Strategic Priorities

The initiative emphasized three key themes:

1. **4D Australia** — Temporal and spatial geoscience understanding
2. **Observations** — Improved data collection across infrastructure types
3. **Data Assimilation** — Integration of research information

---

## Core Objectives

The workshops aimed to:
- Identify gaps in national research infrastructure
- Build rationales for priority investments
- Develop proposals forming the basis of the Strategy document and 5-Year Investment Plan
