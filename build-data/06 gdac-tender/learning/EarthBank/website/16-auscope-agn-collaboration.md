## Core Development Team

### AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN)

**Leadership:** Prof Brent McInnes (Curtin University)

**Mission:** The AGN comprises geochemists from major Australian institutions. The network "aims to create, coordinate, promote and develop national geochemistry research infrastructure."
