## Overview

AuScope is Australia's geoscience research infrastructure funded through NCRIS (National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy). The organization enables fundamental and applied geoscience research addressing critical challenges in mineral resources, energy, water security, and natural hazard mitigation.

## Economic Impact

- **ROI:** Estimated at **"$15 for every $1 spent"** (Lateral Economics, 2016)
- Focus on translating research into practical applications

---

## Key Research Outcomes & Case Studies

### Mineral Exploration

**Julimar Discovery:**
AuScope seismology infrastructure underpinned Chalice Mining's polymetallic deposit discovery in Western Australia, yielding palladium, nickel, copper, and platinum resources.

**HyLogger4 Technology:**
New hyperspectral imaging system advancing mineral exploration while reducing environmental impact through digitized geological data.

### Climate & Antarctic Research
- Infrastructure supporting researchers studying climate-response mechanisms beneath Antarctic ice
- Contributions to sea-level rise modeling for community preparedness

### Geodesy & Space Applications

**Queensland Corner Reflector Array:**
Over 10 years, this NCRIS-enabled network has become "essential to agencies like ESA and NASA" for satellite calibration.

### Applied Seismology
- Forensic seismology collaboration with RAAF detecting unexploded ordnance
- Australian Seismometers In Schools (AuSIS) program

### COVID-19 Research
- Infrastructure supporting researchers modeling pandemic mobility data

### Volcanology
- Laser technology enabling enhanced volcanic monitoring through magma chemistry analysis (2021 La Palma eruption study)
