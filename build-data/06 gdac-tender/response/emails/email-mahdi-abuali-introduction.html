The GDAC-SA Tender Opportunity

Current Stage: Request for Qualifications (RFQ)

The Saudi Geological Survey has issued an RFQ for the GDAC-SA Advanced Analytics Platform - the Saudi Center for Geoscience Data Analysis. This is a two-stage procurement process:

  1. Stage 1 (Current): RFQ - Pre-qualification of eligible bidders
  2. Stage 2 (Next): RFP - Detailed proposal submission for qualified bidders

Bidders must score >70% on the RFQ to proceed to the RFP stage. Evaluation is 60% technical/administrative and 40% financial capacity.

Key RFQ Requirements

  • Valid Commercial Registration Certificate
  • Valid Zakat and Income Certificate (+ VAT if registered)
  • Certificate from General Organization for Social Insurance
  • Valid Chamber of Commerce Certificate
  • Investment license for foreign bidders (MISA registration required)
  • Saudization certificate (Taqat)
  • Quality and HSE policies/certificates
  • Financial statements for past 3 years
  • Details of 3 similar projects implemented in past 5 years

Our Immediate Challenge: Etimad Platform Registration

We are planning to apply directly through the Etimad platform, which is the official Saudi government tenders and procurement portal. However, registration requires:

  • Valid Commercial Registration Certificate - issued through the Ministry of Commerce
  • MISA Investment License - required for foreign companies under the Foreign Investment Regulation

We are currently initiating the process to obtain these through the Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia (MISA) to establish our eligibility to bid directly on the Etimad platform.
