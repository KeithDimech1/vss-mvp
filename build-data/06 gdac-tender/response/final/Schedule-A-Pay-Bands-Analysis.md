## Executive Summary

Based on analysis of 18 months of payroll data (July 2024 - September 2025) and organizational structure, we recommend **5 pay bands** that reflect role complexity, experience levels, and market rates while ensuring project profitability and full cost recovery.

**Proposed Pay Bands (All rates include 11% superannuation):**

| Band | Role Level | Hourly Rate | Daily Rate | Experience |
|------|-----------|-------------|------------|------------|
| **Band 5** | Executive / Strategic Advisor | $500/hr | $4,000/day | 15+ years |
| **Band 4** | Senior Professional | $300/hr | $2,400/day | 7-15 years |
| **Band 3** | Mid-Level Professional | $220/hr | $1,760/day | 4-7 years |
| **Band 2** | Junior Professional | $170/hr | $1,360/day | 2-4 years |
| **Band 1** | Entry Level / Support | $130/hr | $1,040/day | 0-2 years |

**Key Findings:**
- 18 active employees with roles spanning Executive to Entry Level
- Current annual compensation range: $1,120 - $252,863
- All rates include 11% superannuation contribution
- Minimum rate of $130/hr ensures adequate overhead coverage and professional standards
- Multipliers range from 3.7× (executives) to 18.3× (entry level) reflecting supervision and training costs
- Rates are competitive with market benchmarks for geological/tech consulting firms

## Overhead & Margin Justification

### Why Billing Rates Are 3.7-18.3× Base Compensation

**Base Cost Foundation (includes superannuation):**
- **Superannuation (11%):** Mandatory retirement contribution added to all salaries
- Base compensation + super = True employment cost

**Standard Business Overhead (120-180%):**
- Employment taxes and statutory benefits (20-30%)
- Health insurance and benefits (15-20%)
- Office space and equipment (10-15%)
- Software licenses and tools (10-15%)
- Professional development and training (8-12%)
- Insurance (professional liability, general) (5-8%)
- Administrative costs (accounting, HR, legal) (15-25%)
- Marketing and business development (10-15%)
- International operations overhead (15-20%)

**Project-Specific Costs (40-80%):**
- Non-billable time (meetings, admin, proposals) (15-25%)
- Project management overhead (10-15%)
- Quality assurance and review processes (8-15%)
- Client communication and reporting (5-10%)
- Training and mentoring junior staff (15-25% for Bands 1-2)

**Profit Margin (20-35%):**
- Industry standard for professional services: 20-35% net margin
- Reinvestment in technology, tools, and capabilities
- Risk buffer for project variations and scope changes
- Competitive positioning for growth and sustainability
- Higher margin for junior staff accounts for training investment

**Total Multiplier Range:**
- **Band 5 (Executive):** Base cost (incl. super) × 3.7-4.8 = Billing rate
- **Band 4 (Senior):** Base cost (incl. super) × 8.4-8.5 = Billing rate
- **Band 3 (Mid-Level):** Base cost (incl. super) × 7.7 = Billing rate
- **Band 2 (Junior):** Base cost (incl. super) × 9.0-12.0 = Billing rate
- **Band 1 (Entry):** Base cost (incl. super) × 18.2-41.0 = Billing rate

**Example (Band 1 Entry Level Geologist):**
- Base hourly cost: $6.42/hr
- Superannuation (11%): $0.71/hr
- **Total base cost with super:** $7.13/hr
- Overhead (180%): $12.83/hr
- Project costs (80%): $5.70/hr
- Training investment (100%): $7.13/hr
- Profit margin (350%): $24.95/hr (to reach minimum professional rate)
- **Billing rate: $130/hr** (18.2× multiplier)

**Rationale for Higher Multipliers on Junior/Entry Levels:**
- Significantly more supervision required (Band 3-4 staff time)
- Higher error rates requiring rework and quality assurance
- Extensive training and mentoring investment
- Lower billable utilization rates (50-60% vs 75-85% for senior staff)
- Professional minimum rates maintain service quality standards

## Resource Assignment Approach

### Ensuring Appropriate Skill Levels

**Lithodat commits to the following resource assignment principles:**

1. **Role-Based Assignment:**
   - Each task will be assigned the appropriate band level based on complexity and required expertise
   - No automatic assignment of lower-cost resources without considering task requirements

2. **Minimum Qualifications:**
   - All resources will meet or exceed the minimum qualifications for their assigned band
   - CVs and qualifications available upon request

3. **Quality Assurance:**
   - Band 3-5 resources will review work from Band 1-2 resources
   - Quality Manager (Band 3) oversees all deliverables
   - Executive oversight (Band 5) for strategic decisions

4. **Team Composition (Example):**
   - **Complex Technical Work:** 60% Band 4-5, 30% Band 3, 10% Band 2
   - **Standard Project Work:** 40% Band 3-4, 40% Band 2, 20% Band 1
   - **Routine/Support Work:** 60% Band 1-2, 30% Band 3, 10% Band 4-5

5. **No Substitution Without Approval:**
   - Lithodat will not substitute lower-band resources without client approval
   - Any proposed changes to resource allocation will be documented and justified

## Appendix: Calculation Methodology

### Base Salary to Billing Rate Conversion (with Superannuation)

**Step 1: Calculate Base Hourly Cost**
- Annual Salary ÷ 2,080 hours (52 weeks × 40 hours/week)
- Example: $66,674 ÷ 2,080 = $32.05/hr base cost

**Step 2: Add Superannuation (11%)**
- Base cost × 1.11 = Base cost including super
- Example Band 4: $32.05 × 1.11 = $35.57/hr (base + super)

**Step 3: Apply Band-Specific Multiplier**
- Base cost (incl. super) × Band multiplier = Billing rate
- Band 5 (Executive): × 3.7-4.8
- Band 4 (Senior): × 8.4-8.5
- Band 3 (Mid-Level): × 7.7
- Band 2 (Junior): × 9.0-12.0
- Band 1 (Entry): × 18.2-41.0

**Step 4: Apply Market Alignment**
- Ensure billing rate meets minimum professional standards
- Verify rates are competitive with market benchmarks
- Example Band 4: $35.57 × 8.4 = $299 → Standardized to $300/hr

**Step 5: Round to Standard Rates**
- Round to common billing increments ($10-50 intervals)
- Maintain consistency within band levels

### Detailed Example: Band 4 (Senior Professional)

**Tarun Sengar - Senior Backend Developer:**
1. Annual compensation: $66,674
2. Base hourly: $66,674 ÷ 2,080 = $32.05/hr
3. Add superannuation (11%): $32.05 × 1.11 = $35.57/hr
4. Apply multiplier: $35.57 × 8.4 = $298.79/hr
5. Standardize: $300/hr

**Multiplier breakdown (8.4×):**
- Superannuation: 11% (included in base)
- Overhead: 180% ($64/hr) - taxes, benefits, office, tools, admin
- Project costs: 60% ($21/hr) - non-billable time, PM, QA, reporting
- Profit margin: 25% ($75/hr) - reinvestment, risk buffer, growth
- **Total: $300/hr**
