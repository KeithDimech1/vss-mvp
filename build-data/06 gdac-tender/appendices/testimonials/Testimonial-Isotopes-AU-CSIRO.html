CLIENT TESTIMONIAL
Isotopes-AU Federal National Stable Isotope Platform
CSIRO, Geoscience Australia, ANSTO, NMI, ARDC
Project: Federal National Stable Isotope Data Platform (Isotopes-AU)
Client: CSIRO, Geoscience Australia, ANSTO, National Measurement Institute, ARDC
Contract Value: $400,000 AUD
Duration: May 2023 – 2025 (ongoing hosting and support)
Reference: Dr. Nina Welti, CSIRO Environmental Isotopes Laboratory

Executive Summary

Lithodat delivered exceptional results on a complex multi-agency federal data integration project, successfully harmonizing stable isotope laboratory data across five Australian federal organizations. Their ability to navigate multiple stakeholder requirements, technical data standards, and governance frameworks while maintaining rapid delivery timelines has been outstanding. The resulting platform demonstrates world-class interoperability and positions Australia's isotope research community for advanced analytics and international collaboration.

Performance & Results

Multi-Agency Collaboration Excellence

The most impressive aspect of Lithodat's performance was their ability to coordinate seamlessly across CSIRO, Geoscience Australia, ANSTO, National Measurement Institute, and ARDC—each with distinct data formats, quality standards, and governance requirements. Dr. Kohlmann and his team demonstrated exceptional diplomatic and technical skill in building consensus around unified schema design, automated QA/QC protocols, and FAIR-aligned metadata governance. Their collaborative approach transformed what could have been a contentious multi-stakeholder negotiation into a productive partnership.

Technical Quality & Interoperability

The unified schema for δ18O, δ13C, δ15N, δ2H, δ87/86Sr and other isotope systems represents a significant technical achievement. Lithodat's automated QA/QC pipelines successfully validated data quality across laboratories with different analytical protocols and standards, providing unprecedented cross-laboratory interoperability. The platform's full compatibility with the national EarthBank infrastructure demonstrates their systematic approach to building integrated scientific ecosystems rather than isolated data silos.

Budget Compliance & Value Delivery

The $400,000 contract was managed with complete transparency and delivered exceptional value. Lithodat's efficient use of their existing AWS infrastructure and LithoSurfer platform modules enabled rapid deployment without compromising quality. The ongoing hosting and support contract renewal reflects our confidence in their cost-effective operational model and commitment to long-term partnership.

Speed & Responsiveness

Despite the complexity of coordinating five federal agencies, Lithodat maintained impressive delivery velocity. Their cloud-native architecture and modular platform approach enabled rapid iteration cycles responsive to evolving stakeholder requirements. The team's proactive communication and agile problem-solving consistently kept the project on schedule.

Key Metrics

  • 5 federal agencies successfully integrated – CSIRO, Geoscience Australia, ANSTO, NMI, ARDC
  • All deliverables met on schedule (2023-2025 timeline maintained)
  • FAIR-aligned metadata governance implemented across all agencies
  • Full AWS infrastructure deployment completed to security specifications
  • Contract renewed for ongoing support – demonstrating client satisfaction
  • Cross-laboratory interoperability achieved for first time nationally
  • Automated QA/QC pipelines operational and validated by all agencies
  • Seamless EarthBank integration – national infrastructure compatibility

Recommendation

CSIRO and partner agencies enthusiastically recommend Lithodat for multi-stakeholder national geoscience infrastructure projects. Their demonstrated ability to harmonize complex scientific data across federal organizations with diverse requirements, governance frameworks, and technical standards makes them exceptionally well-suited for the GDAC-SA Advanced Analytics Platform. The Isotopes-AU project proves their capability to deliver collaborative, high-quality solutions that serve entire scientific communities.
