## Chemical Geology Publication (2025)

**Title:** EarthBank: National Geochronology Database and Advanced Analytics Platform
**Authors:** Lithodat Team (SC Boone, F Kohlmann, W Noble, M Theile, et al.)
**Journal:** Chemical Geology
**Year:** 2025
**Description:** Published results from the EarthBank project, presenting the national geochronology database architecture, data management workflows, and advanced analytical capabilities for mineral exploration and tectonic history reconstruction.

**Key Highlights:**
- Management of 50,000+ geochronology samples
- Machine learning-powered anomaly detection algorithms
- Spatial querying and visualization capabilities
- Integration across 8 Australian universities
