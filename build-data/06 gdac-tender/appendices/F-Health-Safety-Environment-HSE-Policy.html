APPENDIX F: Health, Safety & Environment Policy
Digital Workplace Safety & Environmental Sustainability Framework
GDAC-SA Advanced Analytics Platform RFQ | Lithodat Pty Ltd
Policy Overview: Lithodat is committed to maintaining a safe, healthy and environmentally responsible workplace across all operations. As a software, cloud and scientific data company working with global government and research organisations, Lithodat follows industry best practices for digital safety, environmental sustainability, and responsible workplace management. This HSE framework applies to all staff, contractors, partners and collaborators.

1. Environmental Management Approach

Although Lithodat is not an industrial or field-operations organisation, it actively incorporates environmental stewardship into its digital operations and cloud infrastructure:

Sustainable Cloud Hosting

Lithodat operates entirely on Amazon Web Services (AWS), which maintains:

  • 100% renewable energy commitment by 2025
  • ISO 14001–aligned environmental management programs
  • Highly energy-efficient data centers

Paperless Operations

Lithodat maintains near-zero paper usage in daily operations through:

  • Digital invoicing, documentation and approvals
  • Cloud-based project management
  • Online collaboration with government and academic partners

Remote Work to Reduce Environmental Footprint

Most staff work remotely or hybrid, significantly reducing:

  • Commuting emissions
  • Office energy consumption
  • Physical infrastructure requirements

Responsible Equipment Lifecycle

  • Recycling and proper disposal of electronics
  • Sustainable procurement of IT hardware
  • Cloud-based processing to minimise local compute waste

2. Health and Safety Standards Followed

Lithodat's workplace health and safety framework aligns with:

ISO 45001 — Occupational Health and Safety

While not formally certified, Lithodat adheres to ISO 45001 principles, including:

  • Hazard identification
  • Risk mitigation procedures
  • Preventive and corrective actions
  • Health and safety documentation

Status: Alignment (not formally certified)

Remote & Digital Workplace Safety

Lithodat has dedicated safety measures for remote staff, including:

  • Ergonomic workstation guidelines
  • Safe home-office setup recommendations
  • Break and screen-time management policies
  • Guidelines for stress management and mental wellbeing

Inclusive and Safe Work Environment

  • Zero-tolerance policies for harassment or discrimination
  • Equal opportunity employment
  • Confidential mechanisms to raise concerns

4. Workplace Safety Policies

Lithodat maintains strong internal digital safety practices:

Ergonomics & Workspace Setup

  • Recommendations for adjustable desks, safe monitor height, proper lighting
  • Health-conscious work scheduling

Safe Work Hours & Fatigue Management

  • Encouragement of breaks during long coding or analysis sessions
  • Flexible hours to avoid overwork and stress

Mental Health & Wellbeing

  • Supportive culture promoting work–life balance
  • Manager check-ins and open communication channels

Secure Working Environments

  • Mandatory encrypted devices
  • Up-to-date antivirus and security patches
  • VPN and two-factor authentication for sensitive systems

5. Environmental Sustainability Initiatives

Cloud-based Workflows Reduce:

  • Hardware waste
  • Local energy consumption
  • On-premises server requirements

Teleconferencing-First Approach

Lithodat prioritises virtual meetings, reducing:

  • Domestic and international travel
  • Carbon footprint
  • Physical office footprint

Minimised Resource Consumption

  • Digital signatures
  • Cloud document storage
  • Minimised printing and physical documentation

7. Incident Reporting Procedures

Lithodat maintains a formal incident reporting workflow for health, safety and environmental issues.

Reporting Process

  1. Immediate reporting to the Managing Director or designated HSE contact
  2. Incident documentation using internal digital forms
  3. Root cause analysis
  4. Corrective and preventive action plan
  5. Follow-up assessment to ensure resolution

Types of Incidents Covered

  • Digital safety issues (e.g., security breaches, hardware failures)
  • Health issues related to remote or in-office work
  • Environmental concerns (e.g., equipment disposal, energy use)

Confidentiality

All reports are handled confidentially without prejudice or retaliation.

HSE Policy Summary: Lithodat maintains a robust and modern HSE framework suitable for a software, AI and scientific data company. Through ISO-aligned practices, AWS environmental and safety certifications, digital-first workplace policies, and strong incident management procedures, Lithodat ensures a safe, healthy and environmentally responsible operating environment aligned with GDAC-SA expectations.
