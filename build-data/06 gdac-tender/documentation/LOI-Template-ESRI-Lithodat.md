# Letter of Intent (LOI) Template
## ESRI South Africa - Lithodat Partnership
### Saudi Geological Survey GDAC-SA RFQ

## Standard LOI Structure (2-3 pages)

### 1. HEADER

```

[Address]

    Advanced Analytics Platform Request for Qualification (RFQ)

---

### 2. RECITALS (Background)

```
WHEREAS:

A. The Saudi Geological Survey ("SGS") has issued a Request for
   Qualification (RFQ) for an Advanced Analytics Platform project
   as part of the Saudi Center for Geological Science Data Analysis
   (GDAC-SA);

B. ESRI SA is a leading provider of ArcGIS Enterprise geospatial
   platforms with established presence in Saudi Arabia;

C. Lithodat is a geological data management and AI/ML specialist
   with proven expertise in national geological database projects;

D. The Parties believe that a partnership combining ESRI SA's
   platform capabilities and Saudi market presence with Lithodat's
   geological domain expertise would create a compelling proposal
   for SGS;

E. The Parties wish to jointly pursue the RFQ opportunity and intend
   to negotiate a formal Consortium Agreement upon successful
   pre-qualification;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:
```

---

### 3. PURPOSE & INTENT

```
1. PURPOSE

   1.1 The Parties intend to form a partnership to jointly pursue and
       respond to the SGS GDAC-SA RFQ.

   1.2 The purpose of this Letter of Intent is to establish the
       framework for collaboration during the RFQ pre-qualification
       stage.

   1.3 This LOI expresses the Parties' intention to negotiate and
       execute a formal Consortium Agreement if the joint RFQ
       submission is successful and the Parties receive the full
       Request for Proposal (RFP).
```

---

### 4. PROPOSED PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE

```
2. PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE

   2.1 Prime Contractor: ESRI SA shall act as the prime contractor
       and lead entity for all interactions with SGS and Saudi
       governmental authorities.

   2.2 Subcontractor: Lithodat shall act as a technical subcontractor
       to ESRI SA, responsible for geological data management, AI/ML
       development, and related technical deliverables.

   2.3 The Parties acknowledge that this structure may be modified
       based on the requirements set forth in the RFP, subject to
       mutual agreement.
```

---

### 5. HIGH-LEVEL ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

```
3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES (High-Level)

   3.1 ESRI SA shall be primarily responsible for:
       a) Submission and coordination of the RFQ response
       b) All Saudi legal, regulatory, and compliance matters
       c) ArcGIS Enterprise platform and licensing
       d) Contract execution with SGS (if awarded)
       e) Overall project management and governance
       f) Invoicing and payment administration

   3.2 Lithodat shall be primarily responsible for:
       a) Geological data architecture and modeling
       b) AI/ML model development for mineral exploration
       c) LithoSurfer visualization platform capabilities
       d) Geological quality assurance and validation
       e) Technical documentation and methodology

   3.3 The Parties acknowledge that detailed task allocation will be
       defined in a RACI matrix as part of the formal Consortium
       Agreement.
```

---

### 6. EXCLUSIVITY & COMMITMENT

```
4. EXCLUSIVITY

   4.1 During the term of this LOI, each Party agrees not to engage
       in discussions or negotiations with any other party regarding
       the SGS GDAC-SA opportunity.

   4.2 Each Party agrees to work exclusively with the other Party for
       the purpose of responding to the RFQ and subsequent RFP (if
       successful).

   4.3 This exclusivity clause shall expire [6 months from date of
       signing] or upon termination of this LOI, whichever is earlier.
```

---

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

```
5. CONFIDENTIALITY

   5.1 All information exchanged between the Parties regarding the
       SGS GDAC-SA opportunity, including technical approaches,
       pricing strategies, and commercial terms, shall be treated as
       confidential.

   5.2 Neither Party shall disclose any information obtained during
       the RFQ process to any third party without the prior written
       consent of the other Party.

   5.3 This confidentiality obligation shall survive the termination
       of this LOI for a period of [2 years].
```

---

### 8. NEXT STEPS

```
6. NEXT STEPS AND FORMAL AGREEMENT

   6.1 Upon successful pre-qualification and receipt of the RFP, the
       Parties agree to negotiate in good faith a formal Consortium
       Agreement that will include:

       a) Detailed scope of work allocation (RACI matrix)
       b) Revenue sharing and pricing methodology
       c) Payment terms and milestone schedules
       d) Intellectual property ownership and licensing
       e) Risk allocation and liability caps
       f) Performance guarantees and penalties
       g) Dispute resolution mechanisms
       h) Termination clauses

   6.2 The Parties acknowledge that the formal Consortium Agreement
       will be informed by the specific requirements, scope, and
       commercial terms outlined in the RFP.
```

---

### 9. NON-BINDING NATURE

```
7. NON-BINDING PROVISIONS

   7.1 This Letter of Intent is intended to express the mutual
       intentions of the Parties and to facilitate their joint
       pursuit of the RFQ opportunity.

   7.2 Except for Sections 4 (Exclusivity), 5 (Confidentiality),
       and 10 (Governing Law), this LOI does NOT create legally
       binding obligations on either Party.

   7.3 Neither Party is obligated to:
       a) Submit a joint RFQ response
       b) Execute a formal Consortium Agreement
       c) Continue negotiations if the RFP terms are unacceptable
       d) Commit to any specific financial or resource allocations

   7.4 Either Party may withdraw from this LOI with [30 days] written
       notice to the other Party.
```

---

### 10. COSTS & EXPENSES

```
8. COSTS AND EXPENSES

   8.1 Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in
       connection with the preparation and submission of the RFQ
       response.

   8.2 The allocation of costs for RFP preparation (if invited) shall
       be determined by mutual agreement at that time.
```

### 11. TERM & TERMINATION

```

   9.3 Effect of Termination: Upon termination, all obligations under

## KEY PRINCIPLES FOR YOUR LOI

### ✅ WHAT TO INCLUDE:

1. **Clear intent** to work together on RFQ
2. **High-level roles** (not detailed RACI)
3. **Exclusivity** during RFQ period
4. **Confidentiality** obligations
5. **Non-binding language** (except exclusivity & confidentiality)
6. **Commitment to negotiate** formal agreement after RFP
7. **Exit clause** (30-day notice)

### ❌ WHAT TO AVOID:

1. ❌ Specific revenue split percentages (wait for RFP)
2. ❌ Detailed task allocations (wait for RFP)
3. ❌ Binding financial commitments
4. ❌ Fixed pricing or rates
5. ❌ Performance guarantees
6. ❌ Detailed IP ownership terms
7. ❌ Liability provisions (wait for full agreement)

---

## TYPICAL LOI LENGTH

- **Short LOI:** 2-3 pages (adequate for RFQ stage)
- **Moderate LOI:** 4-5 pages (includes more detail on roles)
- **Comprehensive LOI:** 6-8 pages (almost a mini-agreement)

**Recommendation for your situation:** 3-4 pages - enough to satisfy SGS RFQ requirement without over-committing before seeing the full RFP.

---

## CRITICAL CLAUSES

### 1. **Non-Binding Language (Essential)**

Always include a clear statement that the LOI is mostly non-binding:

```
"This Letter of Intent is intended to express mutual intentions
only and, except for [specific sections], does not create legally
binding obligations."
```

### 2. **Exceptions to Non-Binding (Binding Clauses)**

Make these clauses BINDING:
- **Exclusivity** (can't negotiate with competitors during RFQ)
- **Confidentiality** (protect shared information)
- **Governing Law** (which jurisdiction applies)

### 3. **Exit Clause (Critical)**

Always have an easy exit:

```
"Either party may terminate this LOI with [30 days] written notice,
without liability or penalty."
```

This protects both parties if the RFP terms are unacceptable.

---

## TONE & LANGUAGE

✅ **Professional but not overly legal**
✅ **Clear and concise**
✅ **Collaborative tone** ("the Parties intend to..." not "must")
✅ **Future-focused** ("will negotiate" not "agrees to")
✅ **Flexible** ("may be modified" not "shall be fixed")

## COMPARISON: LOI vs. FULL CONSORTIUM AGREEMENT

| Aspect | LOI (RFQ Stage) | Consortium Agreement (RFP Stage) |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| **Length** | 2-4 pages | 15-30 pages |
| **Binding** | Mostly non-binding | Fully binding |
| **Detail** | High-level roles | Detailed RACI, SOW |
| **Financials** | None | Revenue split, pricing |
| **IP** | Not addressed | Full IP ownership terms |
| **Liability** | Not addressed | Liability caps, insurance |
| **Term** | 6 months | Duration of project |
| **Exit** | Easy (30 days) | Complex (penalty clauses) |

---

## QUESTIONS TO ASK ESRI WHEN REVIEWING THEIR LOI DRAFT

1. **Is the non-binding language clear?** (You want flexibility)
2. **Are exclusivity and confidentiality the ONLY binding clauses?**
3. **Is there a 30-day exit clause?**
4. **Does it avoid committing to specific revenue splits?**
5. **Does it clearly state "subject to full agreement after RFP"?**
6. **Is the term reasonable (6 months)?**
7. **Which jurisdiction governs?** (South Africa or Australia?)
8. **Are roles described at high-level only?** (Not detailed tasks)

---

## RED FLAGS IN AN LOI

🚩 **Avoid if LOI includes:**
- Specific revenue percentages or pricing
- Binding commitment to submit joint RFP
- Detailed task allocations before seeing RFP scope
- No exit clause or very long notice period (>60 days)
- Liability or indemnity provisions
- Performance guarantees
- Exclusive IP assignments

**If you see these, push back!** LOI should be lightweight and flexible.
