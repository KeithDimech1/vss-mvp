MEETING OBJECTIVE

To finalize partnership structure and strategy for joint submission to the Saudi Geological Survey (SGS) Geological Data Analysis Center (GDAC-SA) Advanced Analytics Platform Request for Qualification (RFQ).

1. PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE OPTIONS

Discussion Points:

Three partnership models were presented for consideration:

Option A: Prime-Subcontractor Model

  • ESRI SA = Prime Contractor (contract holder with SGS)
  • Lithodat = Subcontractor (technical delivery partner)
  • Pros: Clean legal structure, ESRI handles Saudi compliance, clear client relationship
  • Cons: Lithodat has indirect client relationship, less visibility with SGS

Option B: True Consortium / Joint Venture

  • Both parties = Equal partners (joint contract with SGS)
  • Pros: Equal standing, shared risk/reward, direct client relationships
  • Cons: Complex legal structure, joint liability, requires formal JV entity

Option C: Lead Partner Model

  • ESRI SA = Lead (contract holder), Lithodat = Named Partner (not subcontractor)
  • Pros: Balance between visibility and simplicity
  • Cons: May require more complex legal agreement than Prime-Sub
