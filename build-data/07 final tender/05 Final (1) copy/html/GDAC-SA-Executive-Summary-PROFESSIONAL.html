Why Semantic Consistency Enables AI Success

The AI Requirement: Machine learning algorithms identify patterns by comparing feature vectors across thousands of observations. When identical geological concepts use inconsistent terminology (e.g., "granodiorite" vs "granitic rock" vs "felsic intrusive"), algorithms cannot recognize equivalence—fragmenting datasets and reducing model accuracy.

Lithodat's Solution: During Stage 1 ingestion, all data undergoes controlled vocabulary mapping to international standards (CGI vocabularies, OneGeology protocols). Rock types, alteration categories, and mineral associations receive consistent semantic labels across decades of data sources. This semantic coherence is foundational—Phase 2's mineral system models require it to identify meaningful geological patterns and generate high-confidence exploration targets.
