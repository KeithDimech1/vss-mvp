## Project 5 — Natural Resources Canada (NRCan): National Thermochronology & Thermal History Data Models  
**Timeline:** 2021–2023  
**Type of Work:** National-scale scientific data modelling; thermochronology & thermal history  
**Client Sector:** Government (NRCan – Geological Survey of Canada)  
**Description:**  
Lithodat designed the official national data structures for Canada’s thermochronology and thermal history datasets, including:

- Country-wide dataset harmonisation  
- Automated ingestion and QA/QC pipelines  
**Outcomes:**  
- First unified Canadian thermochronology infrastructure  
- AI-ready deep-time datasets at continental scale  
- Directly relevant to GDAC-SA Phase 1 (Data Integration) & Phase 3 (Expansion)
