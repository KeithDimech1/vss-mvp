# Subsurface Observatory Program
**URL:** https://www.auscope.org.au/subsurface-observatory
**Captured:** 2025-11-30

---

## Overview

The Subsurface Observatory assists geoscientists in understanding "the character of the Australian continent: how it responds to earthquakes, and which parts might be prospective for minerals, energy and water resources."

---

## Key Operations

The program deploys seismometers to earthquake-prone regions across Australia and the Asia-Pacific, while utilizing petrophysical instruments to gather thermal, structural, and geophysical data from deep exploration boreholes nationwide.

---

## Petrophysics Laboratory

**Location:** University of Melbourne, School of Earth Sciences
**Led by:** Dr. Dan Sandiford

### Instruments

| Instrument | Function |
|------------|----------|
| **Natural Gamma sensors** | Radioactivity measurements |
| **X-Ray Fluorescence equipment** | Elemental composition analysis |
| **Multi-Sensor Core Logger** | Multiple property measurements |
| **Thermal conductivity measurement tools** | Heat flow analysis |

### Current Work
Profiling geothermal heat production potential in core samples from New South Wales.

## Mobile Laboratory Initiative

**Launched:** June 2022

The mobile petrophysics laboratory expands the ability to conduct field-based measurements beyond traditional laboratory settings.

---

## Notable Achievements

### Tynong Project
Collaborated with Victoria's Geological Survey on thermal measurements for the "Geothermal Heatflow Map of Victoria"

### Stavely Project
Provided foundational petrophysical data for mineral systems research

### Earthquake Monitoring
- Deployed instruments following the 2016 Petermann Ranges earthquake
- Deployed instruments following the 2018 Papua New Guinea magnitude 7.5 event
