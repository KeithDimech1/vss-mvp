## Project Overview

The AGN+ project expanded the AuScope Geochemistry Network by piloting "the expansion of the AuScope Geochemistry Network (now known as 'EarthBank')" to include additional Australian universities with significant geological sample collections.

## Scope & Institutions

The project expanded the network to include eight new institutional nodes:

1. James Cook University
2. University of Queensland
3. University of Wollongong
4. Australian National University
5. University of Adelaide
6. Monash University
7. University of Tasmania
8. University of Western Australia

---

## Key Outcomes

The initiative established Australia's first "trusted domain repository for geochemistry data" and enabled multiple university laboratories to report geochemical collections on the AusGeochem platform, benefiting industry and researchers targeting critical minerals exploration.
