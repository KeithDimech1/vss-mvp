## Partnership Overview

AuScope, established in 2006, is a government-funded initiative supported by the National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS). It operates as a network connecting universities, government agencies, and research organizations across Australia to advance geoscience research and mineral exploration.

---

## Core Mission

AuScope aims to build Australia's "Downward Looking Telescope"—an integrated infrastructure system enabling researchers to explore Earth's subsurface rather than space. The initiative provides "tools, data and technical infrastructure" to support geoscience innovation.

---

## Key Collaborative Projects

### National Virtual Core Library (NVCL)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Development** | Jointly by CSIRO and AuScope in 2006 |
| **Data Volume** | Over 1.5 million meters of digitized drillcore data |
| **Distinction** | World's largest drillcore mineralogical database |
| **Technology** | HyLogger hyperspectral scanning technology |

### Virtual Research Environment (AVRE)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Type** | Ecosystem integrating geoscience datasets and analytical tools |
| **Foundation** | Built on CSIRO's Spatial Information Services Stack (SISS) |
| **Capability** | Enables data sharing across institutions without requiring standardized data models |
| **Access** | Provides open access to imagery, services, and applications |

---

## Research Infrastructure Investments

Since 2006, investments span:
- Laboratories for geochemistry and geochronology
- Characterization technologies
- Geophysical equipment
- Data repositories housing pre-competitive datasets

## Institutional Support

CSIRO's Mineral Resources Business Unit identifies this partnership as "the biggest project," emphasizing its national significance for mineral exploration capabilities.
