## Overview

AuScope is an Australian research infrastructure organization funded through the National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS). The organization provides researchers with "instruments, services, data and analytics via eight Programs."

---

## The Eight Programs

### 1. Earth Imaging & Sounding
Delivers "geophysical equipment that monitor earthquake activity, image the Australian continent" and distributes data online.

### 2. Geodesy & Geodynamics
Operates "observational instruments that monitor movement and changes in the state of the Australian Plate."

### 3. Earth Composition & Evolution
Provides "geochemical laboratories, instruments, data collections and data analytics" enabling researchers to trace planetary development.

### 4. Subsurface Observatory
Maintains "fixed and mobile laboratories containing petrophysical equipment, as well as aftershock seismometers" plus logging services.

### 5. AuScope Virtual Research Environment (AVRE)
Grants "access to FAIR and diverse geoscience data in one online home, as well as the software to process and analyse this data."

### 6. National Virtual Core Library (NVCL)
Deploys "hyperspectral logging instruments for drill core" with accompanying research tools and platforms.

### 7. Australian Seismometers In Schools (AUSIS)
Installs "high quality earth monitoring" in secondary schools, supporting STEM education and research.

### 8. Simulation, Analysis & Modelling (SAM)
Supplies "simulation, analysis and modelling platforms" for investigating Earth's evolutionary history.
