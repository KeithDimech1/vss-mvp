## History & Development

**Launch:** Late 2022 as an open-access geochemistry data repository

**Development:** Created by the AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN):
- Initially three universities: Curtin, University of Melbourne, Macquarie

## The Rebrand to EarthBank

### Reason for Change

The original name created perception barriers with international researchers, who viewed "AusGeoChem" as Australia-only. As laboratories began analyzing global samples, the name became limiting.

**Key Quote:**
> "When we started going internationally, people said, 'It's called AusGeoChem. We don't want to put data in an Australian platform.'"
> — Prof. Brent McInnes

### New Name Inspiration

EarthBank references the biological sciences concept of "biobanks," reflecting the platform's commitment to open data and global reach.
