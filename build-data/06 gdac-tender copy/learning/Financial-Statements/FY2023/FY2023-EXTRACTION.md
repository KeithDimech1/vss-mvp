## 8. Notes to the Financial Statements

### Note 1: Statement of Significant Accounting Policies

#### Reporting Entity Status
The directors have determined that the company is **not a reporting entity** and accordingly, this financial report is a **special purpose report** prepared for the sole purpose of distributing a financial report to members and must not be used for any other purpose.

#### Basis of Preparation
- Prepared on an **accrual basis**
- Under the **historical cost convention**, except for certain assets which have been written down to fair value as a result of impairment
- Accounting policies adopted are consistent with those of the prior year

#### Income Tax
- Income tax expense comprises **current income tax expense only**
- Company does **not apply deferred tax**
- Current income tax rates enacted as at 30 June 2023

#### Property, Plant and Equipment
- Initially recorded at **cost of acquisition or fair value** less accumulated depreciation and impairment losses
- Reviewed annually to ensure carrying amount does not exceed recoverable amount
- Recoverable amount based on expected net cash flows discounted to present value

#### Intangibles (Goodwill)
- Recognised as excess of purchase price over fair value of net assets at acquisition
- Assessed for impairment annually
- Carried at cost less accumulated impairment losses

#### Cash and Cash Equivalents
Includes:
- Cash on hand
- Deposits held on call with banks
- Short-term highly liquid investments (original maturities < 3 months)
- Bank overdrafts

#### Revenue Recognition
- Sale of goods: upon delivery
- Rendering of services: upon delivery
- Commissions: upon delivery of services
- Interest: using effective interest rate method
- Dividends: when entity has right to receive
- All revenue stated **net of GST**

#### Goods and Services Tax (GST)
- Transactions recognised net of GST (except where not recoverable from ATO)
- Receivables and payables stated inclusive of GST
- Net GST recoverable/payable included with other receivables/payables
