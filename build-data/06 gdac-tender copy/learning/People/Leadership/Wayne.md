## About Lithodat
Lithodat Pty Ltd was founded in June 2018 by a group of international senior geologists, data scientists and developers in Melbourne Australia who saw the need for more clean and structured data. The company aims to make data easily accessible and simple to use in exploration workflows.

**Vision:** Provide the resource industry with geospatial geoscientific databases and built-in analytics to simplify and accelerate the usage of specialist datasets to gain fast new insights about the region of interest. Recognized as industry leaders in the provision of thermochronological datasets and thermal histories.
