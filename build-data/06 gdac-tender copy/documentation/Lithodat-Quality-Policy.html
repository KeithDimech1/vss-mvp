QUALITY POLICY

Geology First - Excellence Through Expertise

OUR COMMITMENT

To deliver the highest quality geological and geotechnical data through expert-led processes, rigorous validation, and unwavering commitment to scientific excellence. Our goal is to ensure every data point is accurate, reliable, and fit for purpose - validated by qualified geologists and grounded in FAIR data principles.

OUR APPROACH
Expert-Led

We put qualified geologists at every stage of the data lifecycle by:

  • Employing academically trained and professionally qualified geologists for all data validation and quality control processes
  • Ensuring every dataset is reviewed and verified by geoscience professionals who understand the context, constraints, and critical nuances of geological data
  • Investing in continuous professional development to keep our team at the forefront of geological science and data management best practices
Rigorous

We maintain the highest standards through systematic validation by:

  • Implementing multi-stage human checking processes where every data point undergoes review by experienced geologists before release
  • Conducting peer review of all critical datasets, ensuring independent validation and reducing the risk of systematic errors or biases
  • Establishing clear quality metrics, audit trails, and documentation standards that provide transparency and traceability throughout the data lifecycle
FAIR-Driven

FAIR data principles are at our core - we ensure our data is:

  • Findable: Properly catalogued with rich metadata, persistent identifiers, and searchable repositories that make discovery effortless
  • Accessible: Available through standardized protocols with clear access conditions and long-term preservation strategies
  • Interoperable: Formatted using community standards and vocabularies that enable seamless integration across platforms and systems
  • Reusable: Thoroughly documented with provenance information, usage licenses, and quality indicators that enable confident reuse
Excellence

We're committed to continuous improvement and exceeding expectations by:

  • Setting internal quality standards that go beyond industry requirements, driven by our passion for geological excellence
  • Regularly reviewing our processes, capturing lessons learned, and implementing improvements based on feedback and emerging best practices
  • Collaborating with clients, researchers, and regulatory bodies to understand evolving needs and deliver data that creates lasting value
  • Fostering a culture where every team member takes personal ownership of quality and feels empowered to raise concerns or suggest improvements

This Quality Policy reflects LithoDat's fundamental belief that geological data is only as valuable as the expertise behind it. By combining qualified professionals, rigorous processes, and FAIR principles, we ensure our data delivers reliable insights that drive better decisions.
