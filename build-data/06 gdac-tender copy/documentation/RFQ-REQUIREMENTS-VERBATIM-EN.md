## 1. Required Documents and Licenses

## 2. Application Instructions (Disqualification Triggers)

> **VERBATIM from RFQ (Page 11, Lines 355-389):**
>
> "Please read and follow the following instructions: Any violation of any of the following instructions will result in disqualification. The application shall be submitted to the Saudi Geological Survey in a correct form by an authorized person as follows:
>
> 1. The application must be submitted in advance in either Arabic and/or English and in accordance with the requirements set out in this qualification application form. All documents must be in a searchable format.
> 2. One contact administrator shall be identified, stating their job title and all contact details.
> 3. The presentation should contain concise written materials and illustrations to allow for a deeper understanding and clearer assessment of the applicant's abilities. Only the information required in the qualification application will be provided, and additional information that is not required will be discarded when included in the application.
> 4. A complete, searchable structured digital copy of the qualification applications must be submitted via the platform before the deadline for submitting the pre-qualification application.
> 5. Applications will not be received after the deadline, and the applicant will be disqualified.
> 6. All attachments and forms must be filled out electronically.
> 7. All copies of official documents must be stamped with the applicant's seal to prove the accuracy of the information received.
> 8. If the attached forms are not filled out with the correct information, the applicant will be disqualified.
> 9. Failure to provide the necessary data and documents in full, or failure to fill in the required data in the mentioned annexes, will result in disqualification from the competition.
> 10. The results of the qualification will be announced on the tenders and procurement platform, and if this is not possible, it will be announced on the official website of the Geological Survey.
> 11. Excluded competitors can inquire about the reasons for disqualification by contacting the entity's representative.
> 12. All documents submitted are the property of the Geological Survey and will not be returned to the bidders once submitted."

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

> **VERBATIM from RFQ (Page 13, Lines 397-403):**
>
> "All applications for qualification are evaluated electronically through the Etimad platform, and the total score for the evaluation of the qualification application must exceed 70% of the total to be eligible to receive the RFP and participate in the bidding process. Technical and administrative capacities constitute 60 per cent, and financial capacities 40 per cent of the total grade. The Geological Survey reserves the right to use evaluation criteria (whether subjective or objective) based on the Government Tenders and Procurement System."

## 4. General Conditions (Consortium Requirements)

> **VERBATIM from RFQ (Page 10, Lines 330-344):**
>
> "The purpose of the Request for Qualifications (RFQ) is to provide the opportunity for companies or consortiums with experience and qualification to implement the Advanced Analytics Platform project of the Saudi Center for Geological Science Data Analysis.
>
> Eligible companies are invited to submit their bids (financial and technical) for the Saudi Center for Geoscience Data Analysis (GDAC-SA) Advanced Analytics Platform briefly described here based on the Proposal and Specification Booklet (RFP), which will be sent to the qualified bidders.
>
> The bidder can be either a single company/entity or a group of interested and qualified entities. In the case of an alliance, qualification applications must be submitted to all members of that alliance, including all partners and subcontractors. To be considered for the qualification process, the bidder must provide the following:
>
> 1. A brief statement of the company/entity, its history and any related projects implemented in the last five years.
> 2. Formal agreements between trade alliance partners as well as subcontractors."

## 8. Award Mechanism

> **VERBATIM from RFQ (Page 16, Lines 528-539):**
>
> "In order to ensure clarity and transparency in the procurement process, it is important to determine the mechanism by which contracts are awarded at the end of the competition process, to this end, this Request for Qualifications (RFQ) serves as the initial stage of a two-tier selection process. During the evaluation of qualification submissions, bidders who meet and exceed the established qualification criteria for the subsequent stage—the Request for Proposals (RFP)—will be notified. The RFP stage provides an opportunity for the selected bidders to submit additional details, and serves as a more in-depth exploration of each bidder's capabilities and proposed solutions, thereby enabling a comprehensive assessment to ensure the selection of the most suitable candidate for contract award. Accordingly, participation in the RFP stage is contingent upon successfully passing the qualification requirements during this RFQ stage."
