# Saudi Embassy Document Authentication Guide for Lithodat

**Purpose:** Step-by-step process to authenticate Australian commercial documents for MISA registration
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-01
**Status:** Ready to Execute

---

## Executive Summary

To register with MISA (Ministry of Investment Saudi Arabia), Lithodat needs:

1. **Commercial Register** (ASIC Company Extract) - certified by Saudi Embassy
2. **Financial Statements** (3 years) - authenticated by Saudi Embassy
3. **Activity-specific requirements** - per MISA guidelines

**Good News:** Saudi Arabia joined the Hague Apostille Convention on **7 December 2022**. This significantly simplifies the process - **embassy attestation may no longer be required** for most documents. A DFAT Apostille may be sufficient.

**However:** There's an exception for "administrative documents related to customs transactions, such as establishment certificates" - so ASIC documents may still require embassy attestation. Confirm with MISA before proceeding.

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Gather Original Documents

- [ ] Download ASIC Current Company Extract (fresh, dated within 30 days)
- [ ] Obtain certified copies of FY2023, FY2024, FY2025 Annual Reports
- [ ] Draft Board Resolution (if not done)
- [ ] Prepare LEI certificate (when received)

### Step 2: Find a Notary Public

**Melbourne Options:**

| Provider | Address | Contact | Est. Cost |
|----------|---------|---------|-----------|
| Notary Public Melbourne | Level 13, 200 Queen St, Melbourne | notarypublicmelbourne.com | $180-250/doc |
| Swift Notary | Online booking | swiftnotary.com.au | $180-300/doc |
| SC Notary | Melbourne CBD | scnotary.com.au | Contact for quote |

**What the Notary Does:**
1. Verifies document with ASIC (for company extracts)
2. Certifies as "true copy of the original"
3. Applies Notary seal and signature
4. Prepares document for DFAT

**Book Appointment:** Bring originals + copies. Allow 30-60 minutes.

### Step 3: DFAT Apostille/Authentication

**Where:**
Australian Passport Office (any capital city)

**Melbourne Location:**
- Address: Casselden Place, 2 Lonsdale Street, Melbourne VIC 3000
- Phone: 131 232
- Hours: Mon-Fri 8:30am - 4:30pm

**Process:**
1. Complete DFAT application form (online or at office)
2. Submit notarised documents
3. Pay fee: **$102 per document**
4. Processing: 1-3 business days (counter service often same-day)

**Online Booking:**
https://www.dfat.gov.au/about-us/our-services/apostilles-authentications-certificates-of-no-impediment-to-marriage

**Choose:**
- **Apostille** - if MISA accepts Hague Convention documents
- **Authentication** - if embassy attestation still required

### Step 4: Saudi Embassy Attestation (If Required)

**Royal Embassy of Saudi Arabia - Canberra**
- Address: 38 Guilfoyle Street, O'Malley ACT 2606
- Phone: (02) 6250 7000
- Website: https://saudiembassy.sa/

**Saudi Consulate-General - Sydney**
- Address: Level 3, 33 Bligh Street, Sydney NSW 2000
- Phone: (02) 9221 0704

**Process:**
1. Submit DFAT-authenticated documents
2. Pay attestation fee (contact embassy for current fees)
3. Processing: 3-10 business days typically

**Note:** Contact embassy directly to confirm:
- Current fees
- Whether Apostille is now accepted (post-Hague Convention)
- Any specific requirements for commercial documents

### Step 5: MOFA Verification in Saudi Arabia (If Required)

Some documents may require final verification by Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs after arrival in the Kingdom. This is typically done by your Saudi sponsor or legal representative.
