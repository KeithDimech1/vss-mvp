## 1. MISA Investment License

**Status:** Applied (waiting)

### What It Is
The Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia (MISA) license allows foreign companies to operate legally in Saudi Arabia with 100% foreign ownership.

### Requirements
| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Commercial registration from home country | Must be authenticated by Saudi Embassy |
| Financial statements (1 year) | Prepared by recognized accounting firm, Saudi Embassy authenticated |
| Board resolution | Authorizing Saudi expansion |
| Articles of Association | Translated to Arabic |
| Passport copies | Of shareholders/directors |

### Timeline
- **Standard:** 5-7 business days (after document submission)
- **Total with document prep:** 2-4 weeks

### Cost
- License fee varies by activity type
- Minimum capital: SAR 25,000 for LLC

### Portal
- [investsaudi.sa](https://www.investsaudi.sa)

### Sources
- [Peninsula CS - MISA License Guide](https://www.peninsulacs.com/post/misa-license-how-to-obtain-and-renew-your-saudi-investment-license---a-step-by-step-guide)
- [Setup in Saudi - MISA License](https://www.setupinsaudi.com/en/misa-license)

## 2. Commercial Registration (CR)

**Prerequisite:** MISA License approved

### What It Is
Official registration with the Ministry of Commerce allowing you to operate commercially in Saudi Arabia.

### Process
1. Reserve company name via Ministry of Commerce portal
2. Submit Articles of Association (Arabic)
3. Pay registration fees
4. Receive CR electronically

### Requirements
| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| MISA License | Must be approved first |
| Company name reservation | Via MoC portal |
| Articles of Association | Defining ownership, structure |
| Capital deposit proof | Minimum SAR 25,000 |

### Timeline
- **Standard:** 2-4 weeks after MISA approval

### Cost
- SAR 1,200 - 2,000 per year

### Portal
- [mc.gov.sa](https://mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/)

### Sources
- [Setup in Saudi - CR Guide](https://www.setupinsaudi.com/en/news/announcements/how-to-obtain-a-commercial-registration-cr-in-saudi-arabia-a-full-guide-for-foreign-businesses)

## 3. Chamber of Commerce Certificate

**Prerequisite:** Commercial Registration

### What It Is
Membership certificate from the Saudi Chamber of Commerce, required for government tenders.

### Process
1. Apply immediately after receiving CR
2. Submit CR copy and company documents
3. Pay membership fees
4. Receive certificate

### Timeline
- **Must complete within 30 days of CR**
- Processing: 1-3 days

### Cost
- Annual membership fee (varies by chamber)

### Sources
- [Setup in Saudi - CR Guide](https://www.setupinsaudi.com/en/news/announcements/how-to-obtain-a-commercial-registration-cr-in-saudi-arabia-a-full-guide-for-foreign-businesses)

## 4. GOSI Certificate (Social Insurance)

**Prerequisite:** Commercial Registration + At least 1 employee

### What It Is
Registration with the General Organization for Social Insurance, mandatory for all employers.

### Requirements
| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Commercial Registration | Valid CR number |
| At least 1 registered employee | Can be Saudi or expat |
| Company bank account | For contributions |

### Process
1. Create account on GOSI portal (gosi.gov.sa)
2. Submit company details and CR
3. Register employee(s)
4. Pay contributions monthly
5. Generate GOSI certificate

### Contribution Rates
| Employee Type | Employer Pays | Employee Pays |
|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Saudi | 9% | 9% |
| Expat | 2% | 0% |

### Timeline
- **Registration deadline:** Within 2 weeks of hiring
- **Certificate generation:** Immediate once compliant
- **Certificate validity:** 30 days (must regenerate)

### Key Note
> "Without a GOSI certificate, companies are not eligible to bid on government tenders."

### Portal
- [gosi.gov.sa](https://www.gosi.gov.sa)

### Sources
- [AstroLabs - GOSI System](https://astrolabs.com/business-growth/understanding-the-gosi-system-in-saudi-arabia)
- [Motaded - GOSI Certificate](https://motaded.com.sa/gosi-certificate)

## 5. Zakat and Income Certificate

**Prerequisite:** Commercial Registration

### What It Is
Tax compliance certificate from ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority).

### For Foreign Companies
- Subject to **20% corporate income tax** on net profits
- VAT registration mandatory if supplies > SAR 375,000

### Process
1. Register with ZATCA after receiving CR
2. File required tax returns
3. Obtain compliance certificate

### Portal
- [zatca.gov.sa](https://zatca.gov.sa)

### Sources
- [AstroLabs - Business Setup](https://insight.astrolabs.com/business-setup-in-saudi-arabia-for-foreigners/)

## 6. Saudization Certificate (Taqat/Nitaqat)

**Prerequisite:** Commercial Registration + GOSI Registration + Saudi employee(s)

### What It Is
Certificate proving compliance with Saudi workforce nationalization requirements.

### Requirements for Foreign Companies
| Company Size | Saudi Employee Requirement |
|--------------|---------------------------|
| 1-5 employees | 1 Saudi (after GM) |
| 6-49 employees | Varies by sector (5-30%) |
| 50-99 employees | Higher percentage |
| 100+ employees | Minimum 30% |

### Key Rule for Foreign Companies
> "For international companies entering Saudi, their second employee after the General Manager must be a Saudi national."

### Nitaqat Color Categories
| Color | Meaning | Benefits |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Platinum | Highest compliance | Expedited visas, priority services |
| Green (High/Medium/Low) | Compliant | Normal services |
| Yellow | Below target | Warnings, restrictions |
| Red | Non-compliant | Blocked services |

### 2024 Update
> "Foreign investors who own private establishments in Saudi Arabia are counted the same way as Saudi nationals when calculating Saudization quotas."

### Why It Matters
> "Companies not meeting the Saudization ratio are not able to attain the Saudization certificate which is mandatory for bidding on Etimad."

### Portal
- [hrsd.gov.sa](https://hrsd.gov.sa) (Ministry of Human Resources)
- [taqat.sa](https://www.taqat.sa) (National Labor Portal)

### Sources
- [Peninsula CS - Saudization Guide](https://www.peninsulacs.com/post/saudization-nitaqat-policy-guide)
- [Setup in Saudi - Saudization](https://www.setupinsaudi.com/en/saudization)

## 7. Quality Policy/Certificate

**Can obtain NOW (parallel track)**

### Option A: ISO 9001 Certification (Recommended)

**Timeline:** 2-6 months (fast-track: 10 days possible for small companies)

**Cost:**
| Item | Cost Range |
|------|------------|
| Documentation/prep | $3,000 - $10,000 |
| External audit & certificate | $2,000 - $8,000 |
| **Total** | **$5,000 - $25,000** |

**Fast-Track Requirements:**
- Less than 20 employees
- Single location
- Relatively straightforward processes
- Senior leadership fully committed

**Sources:**
- [Sprinto - ISO 9001 Guide](https://sprinto.com/blog/iso-9001-certification/)
- [SkillFront - Fast Track](https://www.skillfront.com/ISO-9001-Company-Certification)

### Option B: Quality Policy Document (Alternative)

The RFQ says "quality policy/certificate" - a documented Quality Policy MAY be acceptable.

**What to Include:**
- Company commitment to quality
- Quality objectives
- Roles and responsibilities
- Continual improvement commitment
- Customer focus statement

**Template Sources:**
- [ISO 9001 Help - Free Templates](https://www.iso9001help.co.uk/Free-ISO-9001-2015-templates.html)
- [ISO 9001 Checklist - Quality Policy](https://www.iso-9001-checklist.co.uk/5.2-quality-policy.htm)

### Recommendation
Given the competitive nature of this RFQ, **ISO 9001 certification is strongly recommended**. A policy document alone may score lower than competitors with formal certification.

## 8. HSE Policy/Certificate

**Can obtain NOW (parallel track)**

### Option A: ISO 45001 + ISO 14001 (or Combined HSE-MS)

**What It Is:**
- ISO 45001: Occupational Health & Safety
- ISO 14001: Environmental Management
- HSE-MS: Combined system

**Timeline:** 3-6 months

**Cost:** Similar to ISO 9001 ($5,000 - $25,000)

### Option B: HSE Policy Document (Alternative)

**What to Include:**
- Health and safety commitment
- Environmental commitment
- Emergency procedures
- Risk assessment process
- Roles and responsibilities
- Training requirements

**Template Sources:**
- [HSE Study Guide - Policy Sample](https://www.hsestudyguide.com/hse-policy-sample/)
- [UK HSE - Policy Guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/policy/index.htm)

### Recommendation
Same as Quality - formal certification (ISO 45001/14001) will likely score better than a policy document alone.

## 9. Financial Statements (3 Years)

**Can prepare NOW (parallel track)**

### Requirements
| Year | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Year 0 | Most recent fiscal year (audited) |
| Year -1 | Previous year (audited) |
| Year -2 | Two years prior (audited) |

### Data Needed (from Balance Sheet)
- Current Assets
- Cash & Cash Equivalents
- Accounts Receivable
- Current Liabilities

### Financial Ratios Calculated
| Ratio | Formula | Weight |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Cash Ratio | (Cash + Equivalents) / Current Liabilities | 40% |
| Current Ratio | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | 30% |
| Quick Ratio | (Cash + Equivalents + Receivables) / Current Liabilities | 30% |

### Critical Note from RFQ
> "Financial statements for last 3 fiscal years for the qualifying applicant (not for the parent company or subsidiaries)"

## 10. Alliance Agreement (If Consortium)

**Only if applying as consortium**

### What to Include
- All partner companies listed
- Roles and responsibilities
- Lead partner designated
- Profit/work sharing arrangement
- Joint liability statement

### Note
> "In the case of an alliance, qualification applications must be submitted to all members of that alliance, including all partners and subcontractors."

This means EVERY consortium member needs ALL the certificates listed above.
