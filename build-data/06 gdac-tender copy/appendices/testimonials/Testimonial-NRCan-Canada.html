CLIENT TESTIMONIAL
NRCan National Thermochronology & Thermal History Platform
Natural Resources Canada – Geological Survey of Canada
Project: National Thermochronology & Thermal History Data Model Development
Client: Natural Resources Canada (Geological Survey of Canada)
Contract Value: $400,000 AUD
Duration: October 2021 – March 2023 (18 months)
Reference: Dr. Jeremy Powell, Geological Survey of Canada (NRCan)

Executive Summary

Lithodat delivered outstanding results on a transformational national data infrastructure project for the Geological Survey of Canada. In just 18 months, they designed and implemented a completely new national data model for thermochronology and thermal history datasets, standardized decades of provincial and historic data, and harvested all published thermochronology data across Canada. The project was completed on time, within budget, and has positioned Canada's geoscience community for modern machine learning and advanced analytics capabilities.

Performance & Results

Technical Innovation & Quality

Lithodat's creation of Canada's first unified national thermochronology data model represents a significant scientific and technical achievement. The schema elegantly handles AHe, ZHe, AFT, and ZFT analytical methods while accommodating the complex kinetic parameters and metadata requirements of thermal history modeling. Their systematic approach to harmonizing inconsistent legacy laboratory outputs from multiple provincial jurisdictions demonstrates exceptional data engineering expertise and deep understanding of geoscience analytical workflows.

National Scope Delivery

The project's success in standardizing thermochronology data across Canada's entire territory—spanning provincial boundaries, historic laboratory variations, and decades of published research—demonstrates Lithodat's capability to execute at true national scale. Their automated ingestion and QA/QC pipelines successfully processed diverse data formats while maintaining scientific integrity and provenance tracking. The resulting cloud-ready, ML-compatible structure positions Canadian researchers at the forefront of deep-time thermal analysis.

On-Time & On-Budget Execution

The 18-month delivery timeline from October 2021 to March 2023 was met without compromise to scope or quality. Lithodat's transparent project management, clear milestone planning, and proactive risk communication enabled Natural Resources Canada to maintain confidence throughout the engagement. The $400,000 budget was managed with complete accountability, delivering exceptional value for a nation-building infrastructure investment.

Speed & Efficiency

Despite the complexity of designing new national data models, harvesting historic published data, and building automated QA/QC systems, Lithodat maintained impressive velocity throughout the project. Their ability to rapidly prototype schema designs, gather stakeholder feedback, and iterate toward consensus accelerated delivery without sacrificing quality. The efficient integration with their LithoSurfer platform architecture eliminated redundant development effort.

  • 18-month on-time delivery – completed March 2023 as scheduled
  • National data standardization – complete coverage across all Canadian provinces

  • Continued partnership engagement – ongoing relationship demonstrates satisfaction

Their demonstrated ability to design innovative data models, standardize diverse provincial datasets, and deliver complex technical solutions on time and within budget makes them an ideal partner for the GDAC-SA Advanced Analytics Platform.
