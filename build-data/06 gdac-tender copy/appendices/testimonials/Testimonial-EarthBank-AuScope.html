CLIENT TESTIMONIAL
EarthBank National Geoscience Data Platform
AuScope Ltd – Australian National Research Infrastructure
Project: EarthBank National Geoscience Data Platform
Client: AuScope Ltd
Contract Value: $2.9M AUD
Duration: October 2019 – Present (6+ years, ongoing)
Reference: Prof. Brent McInnes, AuScope EarthBank Director

Executive Summary

Lithodat has delivered exceptional results on Australia's flagship national geoscience data platform. Over six years of continuous partnership, they have built and operate a CoreTrustSeal-certified, FAIR-compliant repository integrating over 12 million analytical results from federal and state geological surveys. The platform represents world-class quality in scientific data infrastructure and continues to exceed our expectations for technical excellence and collaborative delivery.

Performance & Results

Technical Achievement

Lithodat successfully integrated four complex analytical domains—geochemistry, geochronology, isotopes, and thermochronology—into a unified cloud-native platform hosted entirely on AWS. The technical sophistication of their unified data models, automated QA/QC pipelines, and spatial-temporal analytics capabilities positions EarthBank as one of the most advanced geological data repositories globally. Their achievement of CoreTrustSeal certification validates the platform's adherence to the highest international standards for trusted digital repositories.

Collaboration & Communication

Working with Lithodat has been seamless. Dr. Kohlmann and his team demonstrated exceptional ability to coordinate with multiple federal and state geological survey agencies, harmonizing diverse data standards and ingestion workflows. They proactively communicate progress, risks, and opportunities, making complex technical decisions accessible to our stakeholder community. Their responsiveness to evolving scientific requirements has been outstanding.

Budget & Timeline Compliance

Over the six-year engagement, Lithodat has consistently delivered all milestones on time and within budget. The $2.9M investment has yielded measurable returns through operational efficiency, research enablement, and international recognition of Australia's geoscience infrastructure. Their transparent cost management and commitment to value delivery have enabled multiple successful contract renewals.

Speed & Agility

Despite the complexity of integrating 12+ million analytical records from diverse sources, Lithodat maintained rapid iteration cycles and responsive development. Their cloud-native architecture enables continuous deployment of new features and data updates without service disruption. The team's ability to quickly adapt to new data types and analytical requirements has been critical to the platform's success.

Key Metrics

  • 100% on-time delivery of all contractual milestones (2019-2025)
  • 12+ million analytical results successfully integrated and quality-assured
  • CoreTrustSeal certification achieved – highest global standard for trusted repositories
  • FAIR compliance verified – Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable
  • 100% system uptime SLA maintained across 6-year operational period
  • Multi-agency integration – federal + state geological surveys harmonized
  • Zero critical defects in production environment
  • API services operational – supporting machine learning and advanced analytics

Recommendation

AuScope unreservedly recommends Lithodat for complex, multi-stakeholder national geoscience data infrastructure projects. Their technical expertise, scientific rigor, collaborative approach, and commitment to excellence make them an ideal partner for the GDAC-SA Advanced Analytics Platform. The six-year partnership on EarthBank demonstrates their capability to deliver sustained value on mission-critical national infrastructure.
