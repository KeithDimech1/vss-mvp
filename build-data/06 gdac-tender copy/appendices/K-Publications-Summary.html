Research Excellence Across Three Domains

Lithodat's core team has established international recognition in three critical areas directly relevant to GDAC-SA's requirements:

1. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning in Geosciences

Our team has pioneered AI applications in geological data analysis, including:

  • Automated Pattern Recognition: Machine learning algorithms for mineral exploration, geochemical anomaly detection, and resource prospectivity modeling
  • Fractal & Multifractal Analysis: Advanced mathematical frameworks for analyzing spatial patterns in geological and geochemical data
  • Deep Learning for Exploration: Neural networks for spatio-temporal prospectivity modeling and predictive analytics
  • Statistical Geochemistry: Novel algorithms for clustering, correlation analysis, and spatial interpolation published as open-source Python packages

2. Geological Data Science & Thermochronology

Extensive research experience in geological data systems and laboratory sciences:

  • National Data Platforms: Published research on design and implementation of federal geochemistry repositories (AusGeochem, Isotopes.au)
  • Big Data Thermochronology: Geospatial platforms for interpreting large-scale thermochronology datasets
  • Petroleum Systems Modeling: Basin modeling, source rock characterization, and hydrocarbon generation studies
  • Low-Temperature Thermochronology: Tectonic interpretation using fission track and (U-Th)/He dating methods

3. FAIR Data Systems & Scientific Data Management

Leadership in establishing international standards for scientific data infrastructure:

  • CoreTrustSeal Certification: Achieved for AusGeochem repository, validating trusted data stewardship
  • World Data System Recognition: EarthBank/AusGeochem recognized by International Science Council
  • Multi-Agency Data Harmonization: Research on integrating heterogeneous datasets across federal organizations
  • FAIR Principles Implementation: Published recommendations for 21st-century geochemical data services
