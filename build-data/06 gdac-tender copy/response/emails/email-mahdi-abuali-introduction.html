About Lithodat

Lithodat is an Australian geoscience technology company specialising in geological data management, standardisation, and AI-enabled analytics. Scientific & Academic Foundation: The EarthBank Partnership

Lithodat is the core technology partner for AuScope's EarthBank platform (formerly AusGeochem), Australia's national geochemistry data infrastructure funded by the Australian Government through NCRIS (National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy).

AuScope has received over $100 million in cumulative NCRIS funding since 2006, with $19 million specifically allocated to EarthBank expansion. The platform now serves 1,300+ users across research, policy, and industry sectors, with 350,000+ registered geological samples.

Our partnership with AuScope demonstrates:

- Peer-reviewed scientific methodology (published in Chemical Geology, 2025)
- FAIR data principles implementation (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable)
- Government-grade infrastructure delivery at national scale
- Collaboration with 10+ leading Australian universities including ANU, Curtin, Melbourne, and Macquarie
- Support from Australia's Science Advisory Panel including representatives from CSIRO, Geoscience Australia, and State Geological Surveys

The EarthBank platform was launched in 2021 in collaboration with the AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN), comprising 28 specialists across 4 Expert Advisory Groups. This rigorous academic oversight ensures our data models and standardisation processes meet the highest scientific standards.

The GDAC-SA Tender Opportunity

The Saudi Geological Survey has issued an RFQ for the GDAC-SA Advanced Analytics Platform (Saudi Center for Geoscience Data Analysis). This is a two-stage procurement process:

Stage 1 (Current): RFQ - Pre-qualification of eligible bidders
Stage 2 (Next): RFP - Detailed proposal submission for qualified bidders

Bidders must score >70% on the RFQ to proceed. Evaluation is 60% technical/administrative and 40% financial capacity.

Key RFQ Requirements

- Valid Commercial Registration Certificate
- Valid Zakat and Income Certificate (+ VAT if registered)
- Certificate from General Organization for Social Insurance
- Valid Chamber of Commerce Certificate
- Investment license for foreign bidders (MISA registration required)
- Saudization certificate (Taqat)
- Quality and HSE policies/certificates
- Financial statements for past 3 years
- Details of 3 similar projects from past 5 years
