BACKGROUND

The Directors of Lithodat Pty Ltd (the "Company"), being all of the Directors entitled to vote on the matters set out below, hereby pass the following resolutions in accordance with the Company's Constitution and the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth).

WHEREAS:

A. The Company has been invited to participate in the Request for Qualifications (RFQ) for the Saudi Center for Geoscience Data Analysis (GDAC-SA) Advanced Analytics Platform, issued by the Saudi Geological Survey;

B. The Company wishes to establish a presence in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to pursue this and other business opportunities in the region;

C. The Foreign Investment Law of Saudi Arabia requires foreign companies to obtain an Investment License from the Ministry of Investment (MISA) prior to conducting business activities in the Kingdom; and

D. The Directors consider it to be in the best interests of the Company to establish operations in Saudi Arabia.

RESOLUTIONS

RESOLUTION 1: Approval of Saudi Arabia Expansion

IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Company is hereby authorized to establish a business presence in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including but not limited to:

  • Applying for and obtaining an Investment License from the Ministry of Investment of Saudi Arabia (MISA);
  • Establishing a branch office, subsidiary, or representative office in Saudi Arabia;
  • Obtaining Commercial Registration (CR) from the Saudi Ministry of Commerce;
  • Registering with all relevant Saudi government authorities as required by law.

RESOLUTION 2: Authorized Capital for Saudi Operations

IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Company is authorized to commit capital of up to SAR 500,000 (Saudi Riyals Five Hundred Thousand), equivalent to approximately AUD 200,000, for the purpose of establishing and operating the Saudi Arabia office, including but not limited to:

  • MISA Investment License application and registration fees;
  • Commercial Registration and associated government fees;
  • Initial working capital requirements;
  • Office establishment and operational costs.

RESOLUTION 3: Appointment of General Manager - Saudi Arabia

IT IS RESOLVED THAT Dr. Fabian Kohlmann, Managing Director of the Company, is hereby appointed as the General Manager of the Company's operations in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with the following authorities:

  • To represent the Company before all Saudi government ministries, agencies, and authorities;
  • To sign all applications, forms, and documents required for MISA registration, Commercial Registration, and related licenses;
  • To open and operate bank accounts in the name of the Company in Saudi Arabia;
  • To enter into contracts, agreements, and commercial arrangements on behalf of the Company in Saudi Arabia;
  • To hire employees and engage contractors for Saudi operations;
  • To appoint representatives, agents, or sub-delegates as necessary;
  • To take all actions necessary and incidental to the establishment and operation of the Company's business in Saudi Arabia.

RESOLUTION 4: Saudi Arabia Office Location

IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Company's principal place of business in Saudi Arabia shall be established in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with the General Manager authorized to secure appropriate office premises and register the Company's address with relevant authorities.

RESOLUTION 5: Document Authentication

IT IS RESOLVED THAT any Director or the Company Secretary is authorized to:

  • Arrange for notarization, apostille, and authentication of Company documents as required by Saudi authorities;
  • Engage legal, accounting, or professional services in Australia and Saudi Arabia as necessary;
  • Execute any certificates, declarations, or attestations required for the registration process.

RESOLUTION 6: General Authority

IT IS RESOLVED THAT any Director of the Company is authorized to do all things and execute all documents necessary or desirable to give effect to the above resolutions.

CERTIFICATION

We, the undersigned, being all of the Directors of Lithodat Pty Ltd, hereby certify that the foregoing resolutions were duly passed and adopted as of the date first written above.
