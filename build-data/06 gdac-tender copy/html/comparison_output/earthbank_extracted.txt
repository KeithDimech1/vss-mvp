Project Overview: AuScope EarthBank (formerly AusGeochem) is one of Australia's national cloud-based FAIR-compliant geoscience data repositories, developed and operated by Lithodat Pty Ltd in partnership with the AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN). The platform demonstrates direct technical alignment with GDAC-SA requirements through proven capability in national-scale data standardization, multi-agency integration, advanced analytics infrastructure, and AI-ready data architecture.
1. Executive Summary
1.1 What is EarthBank?
EarthBank is an Australian national cloud-based public data repository for geochemistry, geochronology, thermochronology, and geological sample metadata. Lithodat designed, built, and operates the entire platform under contract to AuScope Geochemistry Network (AGN), funded through Australia's National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS).
The platform is fully FAIR-compliant (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) and CoreTrustSeal-certified, making it one of the most trusted geological scientific data infrastructures globally. EarthBank serves as a centralized repository for international analytical geoscience data, unifying datasets from federal and state geological surveys, universities, museums, and research institutions.
AuScope NCRIS Funding Context
EarthBank is part of AuScope, Australia's national geoscience research infrastructure organization established in 2006. Since its inception AuScope has received over $260 million in direct Commonwealth investment through the National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy (NCRIS), which has catalyzed a total collaborative investment exceeding $340 million from all sources.
	$42.8 million initial NCRIS allocation (2006) for integrated national geoscience infrastructure
	$45 million NCRIS RIIP funding (2023) for Earth, coastal, Antarctic monitoring, drilling, data, and modelling
	$19 million Specifically for the Australian Geochemistry Network to improve data science roles, data preservation, and instrument upgrades.
This substantial government investment demonstrates Australia's recognition of national geoscience data 
infrastructure as critical research and mineral exploration infrastructure.

	Cloud-Native Infrastructure: 100% AWS-hosted architecture provides scalable compute resources for on-	demand data processing
